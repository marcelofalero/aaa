Alternity RPG - Skills Summary (Broad and Specialty)
=====================================================

Broad Skills can generally be used untrained (at a +1 step penalty to the Ability check) unless marked as "NO".
Specialty Skills can generally be attempted if you have the parent Broad Skill (defaulting at a +1 step penalty) unless marked as "NO" (Trained Only).
