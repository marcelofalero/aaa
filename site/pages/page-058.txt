STUTTER WEAPONS

Popular with police and riot troops, stutter weapons expel
blasts of compressed air that can knock out subjects with-
out causing serious injury. Used carelessly, stutter
weapons can cause concussions, broken bones, or lung
injuries, and there are a number of recorded instances in
which people have died from accumulated stutter gun
fire.
