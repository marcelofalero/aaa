An ancient weren design, the chuurkhna is still favored
by members of that race. It's a glaive or halberd with a
haft about a meter and a half long. Its blade is large and
heavy, intended for chopping rather than thrusting. A
weren can wield it one- or two-handed, but most humans
would have to use both hands to control it. Weren have
been known to drive chuurkhnas through the heaviest
body armor.

The dait'sya is a traditional t'sa weapon, a double-bladed
dagger held in the wielder’s closed fist. TheyTe always
used in pairs (the listed cost, mass, and damage is for two
weapons). Combined with the lightning speed of maost tsa,
the dait'sya is especially dangerous in close-guarters
combat. Because of the dait'sya's light design, any penal-
ties for fighting with a weapon in each hand are reduced
by 1 step.
