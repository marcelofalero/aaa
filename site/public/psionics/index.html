Psionics

Psionics are the powers of the mind. This section details the various disciplines and skills available to psionic characters.

Disciplines

  • Biokinesis: Control over biological functions.
  • ESP: Extrasensory perception.
  • Telekinesis: Moving objects with the mind.
  • Telepathy: Mind-to-mind communication.
  • Teleportation: Instantaneous travel.
