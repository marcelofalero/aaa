Space Vehicle (U) (DEX)

This skill can’t be used untrained.

This specialty skill enables a hero to become trained in the use of a specific type of space vehicle (e.g., space fighters, scouts, medium freighters, capital ships). If he wants to be skilled in more than one type, he must purchase each separately. This skill is also used to operate the helm of large, multicrewed space vessels.

Vehicle Type Durability
Space fighter 10 or less
Scout/Light freighter 11-32
Medium freighter/Corvette 33-60
Capital ship more than 60
