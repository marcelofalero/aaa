Bribe (PER)

Bribe is used when a hero wants to offer another character money, goods, information, or services to get what he wants.

Results

  • Critical Failure: Bribe is refused; target becomes Hostile or Combative.
  • Failure: Bribe is refused; each subsequent attempt must raise the value.
  • Ordinary: Bribe is accepted, but the attitude of the target is unchanged.
  • Good: Bribe is accepted; the target’s attitude improves by one grade for 2d6 hours.
  • Amazing: Bribe is accepted; the target’s attitude improves by two grades for 4d6 hours.
    Results Description
    Ordinary Success indicates that the bribe is accepted.
    Good Success indicates that the target’s attitude improves by one grade.
    Amazing Success indicates that the target’s attitude improves by two grades.
