Gamble (PER)

This specialty skill represents a hero’s proficiency at various games of chance. With each purchase, the player selects a different game.

Success allows estimating odds, determining if a game is rigged, counting cards, and spotting a cheater.

Contests

If playing honestly, the winner is the one with the highest success level. Situation modifiers are based on the opponent’s skill:

  • Untrained opponent: -2 bonus.
  • Trained opponent (same or lower rank): no modifier.
  • Trained opponent (higher rank): +2 penalty.

Cheating

If a hero decides to cheat, different modifiers apply:

  • Untrained opponent: -3 bonus.
  • Trained opponent (same or lower rank): +1 penalty.
  • Trained opponent (higher rank): +3 penalty. On any Critical Failure, the hero is caught cheating.
