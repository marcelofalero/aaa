Clairaudience / Clairvoyance (INT)

Select a location and project your mind to that spot to hear sounds (Clairaudience) or see images (Clairvoyance).

Rules

  • Duration: Ordinary: 1 round; Good: 2 rounds; Amazing: 3 rounds. Extend for 1 psionic energy point per round.
  • Constraints: Does not block normal senses (double vision/sound). Clairvoyance must be to an unobstructed location.

Distance Modifiers

  • Familiar: -1; Unfamiliar: none.
  • 1-10m: -1; 11-100m: none; 101m-1km: +1; 2-10km: +2; 11-100km: +3; 101-1,000km: +4; 1,001-10,000km: +5.
    Results Description
    Ordinary Success indicates the projection lasts for 1 round.
    Good Success indicates the projection lasts for 2 rounds.
    Amazing Success indicates the projection lasts for 3 rounds.
