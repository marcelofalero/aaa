Rifle (DEX)

This specialty skill allows a hero to improve his proficiency with modern rifles and shotguns of all types.

Some rifles are automatic weapons capable of firing multiple rounds of ammunition in every attack.

Rank Benefits

  • Rank 3 [Improved Aim]: Hit a target with more dependability, providing a -1 bonus to the situation die for any rifle attack.
  • Rank 6 [Precision Shooting]: Situation die penalties for making an autofire attack are reduced to 0, +1 step, and +2 steps.
  • Other Benefits: See Ranged Weapons Rank Benefits.
