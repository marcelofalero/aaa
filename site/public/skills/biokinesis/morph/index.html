Morph (U) (CON)

This skill can’t be used untrained.

By using this skill, a character can alter his physical features for disguise or to accomplish tasks (squeeze into small holes, increase reach). Requires an entire round (4 phases) to complete. Mass doesn’t change, but volume can.

Results

Check determines initial duration:

  • Ordinary: 1 round
  • Good: 2 rounds
  • Amazing: 3 rounds Extend for 1 psionic energy point per round.

Rank Benefits

  • Rank 1 & 3: Select one: Elongate fingers (+0.5m reach), Elongate arms (+1m reach), Elongate legs (+1m height).
  • Rank 5 & 7: Select one: Disguise (+2 penalty to Awareness to recognize), Elongate arms and legs simultaneously, Elongate entire body (pass through 0.5m openings).
  • Rank 10 & 12: Select one: Improved disguise (+4 penalty to Awareness), Lessen damage (flexible body reduces LI damage: Ordinary d4, Good d4+2, Amazing d6+2), Morph control (any two forms simultaneously).
    Results Description
    Ordinary Success indicates the form lasts for 1 round.
    Good Success indicates the form lasts for 2 rounds.
    Amazing Success indicates the form lasts for 3 rounds.
