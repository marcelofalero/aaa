Fotocinética (WIL)

Excite molecules in an object to produce illumination. Takes 1 phase to reach maximum luminosity; lasts rest of round and all next round.

Results (Illumination Diameter)

  • Ordinary: 2 meters
  • Good: 4 meters
  • Amazing: 6 meters
    Results Description
    Ordinary Success indicates illuminating a 2m area.
    Good Success indicates illuminating a 4m area.
    Amazing Success indicates illuminating a 6m area.
