Telepatía (U) (PER)

This skill can’t be used untrained.

This broad skill allows a character to open his mind to the thoughts of others or send his own thoughts into the minds of others.

Specialty Skills

  • Contacto
  • Enlace de datos
  • Escudo mental
  • Espejismo
  • Explosión mental
  • Neumático
  • Sugerir
  • Contact: Send and receive thoughts with another.
  • Datalink: Link mind with a computer or cybernetic machine.
  • Illusion: Project illusory sight or sound into a target’s mind.
  • Mind Blast: Powerful blast of pure mental energy.
  • Mind Shield: Mental defense against specific psionic powers.
  • Suggest: Plant a thought into another’s mind.
  • Tire: Inflict fatigue damage upon a target.
