Resolución física (WIL)

This specialty skill is used to determine a hero’s ability to cope with physical trauma of all kinds, including torture, pain, and damage.

Results

  • Critical Failure: Break under torture and reveal everything.
  • Failure: Break under torture and reveal at least one fact.
  • Ordinary: Withstand torture for d4 time units.
  • Good: Withstand torture for d6+2 time units.
  • Amazing: Withstand torture for 2d4+3 time units.

Knockout Recovery

After being knocked out, a character makes checks to regain consciousness:

  • Ordinary: recover 2 stun points.
  • Good: recover 4 stun points.
  • Amazing: recover 6 stun points.

Fatigue Recovery

The result of a check determines points recovered after rest:

  • Failure: no points.
  • Ordinary: 1 point.
  • Good: 2 points.
  • Amazing: 3 points.

Avoiding Exhaustion

Success allows a hero to keep going after all fatigue points are used:

  • Ordinary: 1 time unit.
  • Good: 2 time units.
  • Amazing: 3 time units.
    Results Description
    Ordinary Success indicates that the hero can recover 2 stun points.
    Good Success indicates that the hero can recover 4 stun points.
    Amazing Success indicates that the hero can recover 6 stun points.
