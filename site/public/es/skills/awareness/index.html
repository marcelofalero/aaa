Conciencia (WIL)

This broad skill represents a hero’s powers of observation, perception, and intuition. Skill checks are called for by the Gamemaster to determine if a hero notices something.

Results

  • Failure: The hero doesn’t notice a thing.
  • Success: Provides at least a small clue. The better the level of success, the more details are revealed.

Specialty Skills

  • Intuición
  • Percepción
  • Intuition: representation of a hero’s natural instincts and subconscious sensing of danger.
  • Perception: representation of a hero’s alertness and powers of observation using normal senses.
