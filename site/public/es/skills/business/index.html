Negocio (INT)

This skill provides a basic knowledge of the theories of production, distribution, supply and demand, advertising, inventory control, and business planning. Skill checks are called for when a hero wants to play the stock market, conclude a trade, determine the value of goods, or locate marketplaces.

If a character uses Business to haggle, the other party’s Intelligence resistance modifier applies.
