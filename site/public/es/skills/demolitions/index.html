Demoliciones (U) (INT)

This skill can’t be used untrained.

A skilled hero has a working knowledge of handling explosives, using primers and timing devices, and placing charges for maximum effect.

Setting Charges

Setting a prepared charge can be a simple activity. A Failure means the device is a dud; a Critical Failure can result in premature detonation.

Concealing Charges

Requires a complex skill check. Success makes the charge harder to spot, providing a penalty to an opponent’s Awareness check:

  • Marginal: +1 penalty
  • Ordinary: +2 penalty
  • Good: +3 penalty
  • Amazing: +4 penalty

Vulnerable Placement

Successfully completing a complex skill check of Good complexity improves damage by one grade; Amazing complexity improves it by two grades.

Specialty Skills

  • Desarmar
  • Establecer explosivos
  • Explosivos construidos desde cero
