Xenomedicina [Específica] (U) (INT)

This skill can’t be used untrained.

This specialty skill involves the medical study of a specific alien species, allowing a hero to use his other medical skills on members of that species. Initially, a +3 step penalty is applied to healing alien characters.

Rank Benefits

  • Improved Treatment: Penalty reduction to +2 at rank 3, +1 at rank 6, eliminated at rank 9, and -1 bonus at rank 12.
