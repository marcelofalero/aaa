The Spineless flaw hampers a character by weakening his will, making him fearful, indecisive, and easier to manipulate. This is reflected in a reduction to the character's Will resistance modifier.
* For 2 bonus skill points, a character's Will resistance modifier is reduced by 1 step.
* For 4 points, a character's Will resistance modifier is reduced by 2 steps.
* For 6 points, a character's Will resistance modifier is reduced by 3 steps.
