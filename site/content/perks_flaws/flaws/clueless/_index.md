A character with the Clueless flaw thinks he's proficient in a particular specialty skill, when in fact he's not as good as he thinks he is. The specialty skill is chosen by the Gamemaster secretly—the character must discover the fact that he's clueless over the course of the campaign, and he may not ever realize it at all. The chosen specialty skill is never one that’s associated with a character's profession; it will either be a skill from the Open group or from one of the other profession groups.
* For 2 bonus skill points, the character receives a +1 penalty to the skill in question.
* For 4 bonus skill points, the character receives a +2 penalty to the skill in question.
* For 6 bonus skill points, the character receives a +3 penalty to the skill in question.
