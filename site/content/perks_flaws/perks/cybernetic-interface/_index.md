---
title: "Cybernetic Interface"
date: 2026-08-03
type: "perk"
---

This perk provides a character with the neural compatibility and intensive physical training required to install and operate cybernetic implants and body gear without additional preparation costs. Normally, preparing a character for cyber gear installation costs 10 skill or experience points to master nanocomputers and function with passive neural implants. With this perk, the character gains this capability immediately.

Characters of the **Mechalus** species and citizens of the **Nariac Domain** receive this perk for free during character creation.
