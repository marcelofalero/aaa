Animals of all kinds innately trust a character who has this perk. The character can easily befriend animals, and he makes all encounter rolls involving normal animals with a -2 bonus (use the situation modifiers for the Animal Handling skill, page 91). The character makes Animal Handling skill checks with a -1 bonus, in addition to other modifiers that may apply. This bonus has the effect of making it possible for the character to train an animal in less time than would be needed by a character who does not have this perk. The perk has no effect on sentient or unintelligent creatures.
