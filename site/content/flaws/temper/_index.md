A character with this flaw has a short fuse. The player and the Gamemaster must agree on what type of event triggers the character's Temper flaw.
* For 2 bonus skill points, the character becomes gruff and mean, suffering a +1 penalty to all actions until the triggering event ends and he calms down.
* For 4 bonus skill points, the character reacts without thinking, suffering a +2 penalty to all actions until the triggering event ends and he calms down.
* For 6 bonus skill points, the character flies into a berserk rage, suffering a +3 penalty until the triggering event ends and he calms down.
