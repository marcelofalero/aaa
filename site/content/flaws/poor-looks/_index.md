A character with the Poor Looks flaw suffers a +1 penalty to all Personality-based skill checks when appearance could have a negative effect. Poor Looks isn't just a measure of physical beauty; it includes manners, grooming, and overall appearance. Bear in mind that a human character's Poor Looks might not be seen as such by an alien, and even some other human cultures may have a different idea of what Poor Looks are. The Gamemaster determines when the flaw's penalty is applied and when it isn't, depending on the situation.
