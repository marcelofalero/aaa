This flaw indicates that a character has come from a primitive culture and upbringing, which causes problems for him when dealing with modern technology.
* For 2 bonus skill points, a character receives a +1 penalty whenever he uses modern technology.
* For 4 bonus skill points, a character receives a +2 penalty whenever he uses modern technology.
* For 6 bonus skill points, a character receives a +3 penalty whenever he uses modern technology, and he is either awed into inactivity or flees in terror for d4+1 time units in the face of some technological wonder he hasn’t seen before.
