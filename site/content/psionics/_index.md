# Psionics

Psionics are the powers of the mind. This section details the various disciplines and skills available to psionic characters.

## Disciplines

- **[Biokinesis](/skills/biokinesis)**: Control over biological functions.
- **[ESP](/skills/esp)**: Extrasensory perception.
- **[Telekinesis](/skills/telekinesis)**: Moving objects with the mind.
- **[Telepathy](/skills/telepathy)**: Mind-to-mind communication.
- **[Teleportation](/skills/teleportation)**: Instantaneous travel.
