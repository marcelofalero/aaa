*This skill can't be used untrained.*

This broad skill allows a character to open his or her mind to the thoughts of others or send thoughts into the minds of others.

## Specialty Skills

{{% list %}}
- **[Contact](contact)**: Send and receive thoughts to and from another character for information exchange.
- **[Datalink](datalink)**: Link mind with a computer or cybernetic machine to operate systems.
- **[Drain](drain)**: Draw energy or attribute points from another living being.
- **[Empathic Projection](empathic-projection)**: Alter the emotional state or mood of other beings.
- **[Guidance](guidance)**: Temporarily grant one of the character's non-psionic skills to another mind.
- **[Illusion](illusion)**: Project visual or auditory illusions into a target's mind.
- **[Mind Blast](mind-blast)**: Direct a powerful blast of pure mental energy at another mind.
- **[Mind Shield](mind-shield)**: Establish a mental defense against incoming psionic powers or parry them.
- **[Mind Wipe](mind-wipe)**: Erase or edit memories, or even specific skill knowledge, from a target's mind.
- **[Psychic Armor](psychic-armor)**: Create a mental buffer zone that provides psionic defense points.
- **[Psychic Projection](psychic-projection)**: Separate the mind from the physical body to travel invisibly and intangibly.
- **[Subdual](subdual)**: Engender feelings of calm or detachment in a target to reduce violence.
- **[Suggest](suggest)**: Plant a thought into another person's mind and convince them it is their own.
- **[Tire](tire)**: Inflict fatigue damage upon a target.
- **[Undo](undo)**: Reverse the effects of telepathic illusions, mind wipes, or suggestions.
{{% /list %}}
