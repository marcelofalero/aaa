title = "Timeslip"
attribute = "WIL"
+++

*This skill can't be used untrained.*

Perhaps most prized of all the abilities that manipulate time, Chronal distortion—also known as "timeslipping"—allows a character to "jump ahead" in time by a few moments, or even a few hours. The character vanishes from all perception, then reappears later, in the very same location, relative to local coordinates. In effect, this means that a character who timeslips forward a few moments in seat 23-B of a moving plane will return moments later to that same seat, as the "local coordinates" are determined by the character's location within the aircraft.

A character who wishes to timeslip makes a skill check to determine how far forward he or she can go—though the psion can always choose not to go that far. A character must decide how far forward to go when the power activates. Timeslipping does not allow the character to view events that occur in that location in the intervening time, so he or she cannot simply wait and return at an opportune time.

The character may not take any actions while timeslipping. Relative to the character, only a moment passes between exit into the timestream and reentry into reality. For those left behind, time passes normally.

The basic unit of time is one round; that unit increases with rank benefits. Regardless of the result of the skill check, the character can always choose to travel a shorter period of time.

The character may travel one, two, or three such time units ahead in time, depending on the result of the skill check (Ordinary, Good, or Amazing). On a Failure, however, the character only reappears at the Gamemaster's discretion (though no longer than twice the maximum length of time the character could choose).

On a **Critical Failure**, the character loses d6s.

**Rank Benefits:**
- **At rank 4 [Extended Slip]**, the basic unit of time is 1 minute.
- **At rank 6 [Dodge]**, the character can "dodge attacks" by timeslipping. This is accomplished in the same fashion as a reaction parry, as described in Chapter 4: Skills, in the Player's Handbook.
- **At rank 8 [Extended Slip]**, the basic unit of time is 1 hour.
- **At rank 12 [Extended Slip]**, the basic unit of time is 1 day.
