This skill allows the character to cause an object to either appear out of nowhere or disappear to nowhere.

In actuality, the character is "reaching" into the past to retrieve the item, in the case of materializing the object, or "moving" it to the future, in the case of dematerializing it. An object to be dematerialized must be within the character's line of sight, and no more than 4 meters away (range 1/2/4). Apply a +1 step penalty at medium range (2 meters) and a +3 step penalty at long range (4 meters). Objects the psion sends into the future will appear at the same location, relative to known local coordinates; only the point in time varies. "Relative to known local coordinates" means that an item sent forward in time at the corner of 12th and Elm Streets will arrive at the corner of 12th and Elm Streets some time later, even though the point in space will actually be different when using an astronomical coordinate system.

This ability is limited by the mass of the target item, and living material is not affected by this skill. For each point of the psion's Will, he or she can move 1 kilogram of material through time.

How quickly this occurs is based on the result of the skill check:
- **Ordinary:** the effect takes two additional actions beyond the action taken to activate the ability.
- **Good:** the effect requires one additional action.
- **Amazing:** the effect occurs as soon as the skill check is made.

When attempting to materialize items, the psion may target specific items (for example, "a gold coin from the Spanish galleon that sank in 1505"), though doing so imposes a +3 step penalty on the action. Targeting an item of a general description (such as "a gold coin from a Spanish galleon") imposes a +2 step penalty. Items that the character has never seen create a penalty of +1 step, which may be cumulative with other penalties.

**Rank Benefits:**
- **At rank 4 [Increased Effect]**, the psion can affect up to 2 kilograms per point of Will.
- **At rank 6 [Timed Return]**, the psion can cause a dematerialized object to reappear at a location at a particular time. The psion need not be within 4 meters to effect the dematerialization. At rank 6, the object must be set to dematerialize within a number of minutes equal to the psion's Will score.
- **At rank 8 [Increased Effect]**, this increases to 5 kilograms per point of Will.
- **At rank 9 [Timed Return]**, the unit of time for reappearance extends to hours.
- **At rank 12 [Increased Effect]**, this increases to 10 kilograms per point of Will.
- **At rank 12 [Timed Return]**, the unit of time for reappearance extends to days.
