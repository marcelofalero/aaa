title = "Alter Speed"
attribute = "WIL"
+++

*Extended duration.*

With this skill, the character or another living target within 8 meters can move at a higher or lower rate of speed than everything else in the vicinity. For as long as this ability is in effect, the target either gains a bonus to his or her action check or suffers a penalty. The psion must decide on the desired effect before attempting the skill check.

| Skill Check Result | Modifier to Action Check |
| :--- | :---: |
| **Ordinary** | –1 or +1 |
| **Good** | –2 or +2 |
| **Amazing** | –3 or +3 |

**Rank Benefits:**
- **At rank 6 [Action Points]**, the target of this ability either gains or loses one action—depending on whether the desired overall effect was speeding or slowing, respectively—for as long as the ability is in effect.
- **At rank 12 [Action Points]**, the target gains or loses another action, again depending on whether the desired effect is speeding or slowing. Note that the successful use of this skill cannot increase the number of actions a character can take to more than four. In addition, it cannot reduce a character's actions below zero (in other words, it cannot carry over the effect into the next round).
