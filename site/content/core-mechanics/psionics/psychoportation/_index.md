*This skill can't be used untrained.*

Psychoportation allows a character to manipulate the fabric of space and time.

---

## Alter Speed
### (WIL - Trained Only)
*This skill can’t be used untrained.*

This skill allows a character to accelerate or decelerate an object or living being within a limited range (25/50/100 meters). The result of the skill check determines the percentage of the target's current speed that is affected:
- **Ordinary:** 10%
- **Good:** 25%
- **Amazing:** 50%

**Rank Benefits:**
- **At rank 4 [Increased Effect]**, percentages increase to 25/50/75%.
- **At rank 8 [Increased Effect]**, percentages increase to 50/75/100%.
- **At rank 12 [Instant Stop/Start]**, the psion can instantly stop a moving object or bring a stationary object to its maximum speed.

---

## Apportation
### (WIL - Trained Only)
*This skill can’t be used untrained.*

The psion can mentally transport objects or living beings from one location to another within limited range (25/50/100 meters). This differs from Teleportation in that the psion does not travel with the target.

**Rank Benefits:**
- **At rank 4 [Increased Range]**, range increases to 1 kilometer.
- **At rank 12 [Increased Range]**, range increases to 100 kilometers.

---

## Duplicate
### (WIL - Trained Only)
*This skill can’t be used untrained.*

The character can reach into an alternate timeline and bring a future version of himself or herself into the present. This future self acts as an independent entity and can assist the character.

- **Ordinary:** Future self lasts 1 round; copy has +1 rank in one skill.
- **Good:** Future self lasts 2 rounds; +2 ranks in skills and one Achievement benefit.
- **Amazing:** Future self lasts 3 rounds; +3 ranks in skills and two Achievement benefits.

**Critical Failure:** The character returns from the attempt having suffered damage or loss of equipment.

---

## Teleportation
### (WIL - Trained Only)
*This skill can't be used untrained.*

Instantly travel from one location to another within 100 meters. The psion must be familiar with the target location.

**Rank Benefits:**
- **At rank 3, 6, 9, 12 [Increased Mass]**, the weight of passengers/equipment increases from 1x Will to 2x, 5x, 10x, and finally 20x Will score.
- **At rank 4, 8, 12 [Increased Distance]**, maximum distance increases to 1 km, 100 km, and 1000 km.

---

## Timeslip
### (WIL - Trained Only)
*This skill can't be used untrained.*

Jump forward in time by moments, minutes, or even hours. The character disappears and reappears at the same local coordinates at the specified future time.

- **Ordinary:** Travel 1 time unit forward.
- **Good:** Travel 2 time units.
- **Amazing:** Travel 3 time units.

**Rank Benefits:**
- **At rank 4, 8, 12 [Extended Slip]**, the base time unit increases from 1 round to 1 minute, 1 hour, and finally 1 day.
- **At rank 6 [Dodge]**, perform a reaction timeslip to avoid an attack.
