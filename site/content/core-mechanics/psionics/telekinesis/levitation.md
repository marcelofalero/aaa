*Extended duration.*

This skill allows a character, with only the power of the mind, to rise into the air and propel him- or herself as though flying.

How high and how fast a character can move while levitating depends on the result of a skill check, as shown below. The first entry is how many meters the character can ascend or descend per phase, the second is the speed of flight. These figures are doubled in gravity conditions lighter than Earth normal and halved in gravity conditions heavier than Earth normal.

| Result | Ascent/Descent | Flight Speed |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Ordinary** | 2 meters | walk x 1 |
| **Good** | 4 meters | walk x 1.5 |
| **Amazing** | 6 meters | walk x 2 |

The levitation skill has a duration of 1 round, which may be extended by spending additional psionic energy points at a rate of 1 point for each additional round.

If a character chooses to stop levitating or runs out of psionic energy points while out of touch with the ground, he or she suffers damage from a fall as indicated on Table P15: IMPACT DAMAGE (see the *Player's Handbook*, Chapter 3: Heroes in Action).

Performing an additional action while levitating provides a +1 step penalty to that action.

**Rank Benefits:**
- **At rank 3 [No Concentration]**, a character no longer must take a +1 step penalty to actions taken while maintaining levitation.
- **At rank 6 [Extended Duration]**, the duration of a single use of levitation is extended to 1 minute. It may be extended at a rate of 1 minute per psionic energy point expended to do so.
- **At rank 9 [Extended Duration]**, the duration increases to 5 minutes, with 5 minute increases for each additional psionic energy point spent.
- **At rank 12 [Extended Duration]**, the duration becomes 30 minutes, with 30 minute increases.
