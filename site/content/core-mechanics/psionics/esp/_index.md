*This skill can't be used untrained.*

This skill allows a character to experience his environment through an agency beyond normal senses (Extrasensory Perception).

## Specialty Skills

- **[Battle Mind](battle-mind)**: Focus on the battle for bonuses to action checks.
- **[Clairaudience](clairaudience)**: Project the mind to a distant location to hear sounds.
- **[Clairvoyance](clairvoyance)**: Project the mind to a distant location to see images.
- **[Dream Hunt](dream-hunt)**: Concentrating and mentally engaging in a hunt to pinpoint a living being's location.
- **[Empathy](empathy)**: Read the surface emotional state of another character in visual contact.
- **[Mind Reading](mind-reading)**: Read the surface thoughts of another character in visual contact.
- **[Navcognition](navcognition)**: Instinctively determine present location and plot courses without instruments.
- **[Postcognition](postcognition)**: Receive impressions about the recent past of a location or person.
- **[Precognition](precognition)**: Receive impressions and flashes about possible future events.
- **[Psychometry](psychometry)**: Read psychic impressions from inanimate objects through touch.
