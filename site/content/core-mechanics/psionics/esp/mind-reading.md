+++
title = "Mind Reading"
attribute = "INT"
+++

This skill enables a psion to "read" the surface thoughts of another character. The target must be within 500 meters (100m/200m/500m) of the psionics-user. The mental contact remains in effect for a limited time and can't be extended by the use of psionic energy points. The better the degree of success achieved, the longer the contact lasts and the clearer the impressions of the thoughts being read:

| Result | Duration | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ordinary** | 1 phase | random and disjointed thoughts (such as the target's name or the identity of someone or something he or she is thinking about or looking at). |
| **Good** | 2 phases | random thoughts of the sort described above, plus more detail and more coherence (such as why the target is thinking about someone or something, or why the target is in his or her present location). |
| **Amazing** | 3 phases | complete surface thoughts can be read—the sort of information that's recovered on an Ordinary or Good success, plus some key fact that's related to what the psionics-user hoped to discover. |

**Rank Benefits:**
- **At rank 3 [Extra Time]**, the character may add an additional phase to the duration listed for each level of success: **Ordinary:** 2 phases; **Good:** 3 phases; **Amazing:** 4 phases.
- **At rank 6 [Probe]**, this focused form of mind reading allows an individual to search through a target's mind for a specific piece of information. Attempting a probe on an alien species inflicts a +2 step penalty on the attempt. Will resistance modifiers also apply if the target is actively resisting the attempt. On an Ordinary success, the Mindwalker gains the information desired. On a Good success, the Mindwalker also gains some context for the information, such as its source. On an Amazing success, the Mindwalker gains not just the information's source but also the target's opinion of information, such as whether it is credible, dangerous, or valuable. Note that the probe rank benefit has the same duration as the basic mind reading ability, assuming a sufficiently high rank in ESP-mind reading.
- **At rank 9 [Extra Time]**, the duration increases as follows: **Ordinary:** 1 round; **Good:** 2 rounds; **Amazing:** 3 rounds.
- **At rank 12 [Extra Time]**, it increases again: **Ordinary:** 1 minute; **Good:** 2 minutes; **Amazing:** 3 minutes.
