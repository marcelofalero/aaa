This specialty skill allows a hero to become proficient in stage magic, also known as prestidigitation. In addition to entertaining others, the skill can also be used to slip small objects out of sight.

### Results
- **Critical Failure:** The hero drops the item he is manipulating or otherwise fumbles the task severely.
- **Failure:** The manipulation takes place, but the task is performed clumsily or slowly enough that anyone paying attention can see what happened.
- **Ordinary/Good/Amazing:** The trick or task is pulled off without a hitch.
