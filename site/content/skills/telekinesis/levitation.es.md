*Extended duration.*

Lift yourself into the air and propel yourself as though flying. Speed and height depend on success. Figures are doubled in light gravity and halved in heavy gravity. Performing an additional action gives a +1 penalty.

### Results (Ascend per phase / Speed)
- **Ordinary:** 2m / Walk x 1
- **Good:** 4m / Walk x 1.5
- **Amazing:** 6m / Walk x 2
| Results | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ordinary** | Success indicates levitating at 2m/phase and walk speed. |
| **Good** | Success indicates levitating at 4m/phase and walk x 1.5. |
| **Amazing** | Success indicates levitating at 6m/phase and walk x 2. |
