*This skill can't be used untrained.*

Cause an electrical charge to build up and direct it up to 16 meters away (range 4/8/16). Charge builds in one phase and can be released in any subsequent phase (requires second skill check). Cannot initiate other psionic skills while charge is present.

### Results
- **Ordinary:** d4+2s/d6+2s/d4w.
- **Good:** d4+2s/d6+2s/d4w.
- **Amazing:** d4+2s/d6+2s/d4w.

### Rank Benefits
- **Rank 5 [Increased Damage]:** d6+2s/d4w/d4+2w.
- **Rank 9 [Increased Damage]:** d4+2w/d6+2w/d8+2w.
