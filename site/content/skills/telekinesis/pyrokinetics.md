*This skill can't be used untrained.*

Excite molecules within an object or the air to cause fire. Range 10/20/30 (penalties at medium/long range). Target catches fire in the following phase.

### Flash Fire Storm
Target the air for effect similar to incendiary grenade. Damage reduced for targets within 2m (-2), 4m (-3), and 6m (-4).

### Results
- **Ordinary:** d4+2w/d6+2w/d8+2w.
- **Good:** d4+2w/d6+2w/d8+2w.
- **Amazing:** d4+2w/d6+2w/d8+2w.

### Rank Benefits
- **Rank 5 [Increased Damage]:** d6+2w/d8+2w/d4m.
- **Rank 9 [Increased Damage]:** d8+2w/d4m/d4+2m.
