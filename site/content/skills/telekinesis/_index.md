*This skill can't be used untrained.*

This broad skill allows a hero to manipulate his physical environment with the power of his mind.

## Specialty Skills

{{% list %}}
- **Electrokinetics:** Generate and direct electrical charges.
- **Kinetic Shield:** Create an invisible defensive barrier.
- **Levitation:** Lift and propel self through the air.
- **Photokinetics:** Cause objects to give off light.
- **Psychokinetics:** Move objects with the mind.
- **Pyrokinetics:** Generate heat and cause fire.
