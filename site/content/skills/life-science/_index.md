This broad skill represents a character's training in the life sciences. Skill checks involving this skill are made the same as Knowledge skill checks.

---

# Specialty Skills

## Biology, Botany, Zoology (INT - Trained Only)


*This skill can't be used untrained.*

These specialty skills must be purchased separately.
- **Biology:** The study of organisms and biochemical processes.
- **Botany:** The study of plants and identification.
- **Zoology:** The study of animals and animal life.

⊗ **Increased Effect:** A character receives a **-1 bonus** at rank 3, **-2** at rank 6, **-3** at rank 9, and **-4** at rank 12 on certain other skill checks that can be assisted by this specialty. These skills include Survival, Animal Handling, and Medical Science—treatment.

---

## Genetics (INT - Trained Only)


*This skill can't be used untrained.*

Genetics is the study of heredity and genes, forming the basis of genetic engineering and cloning.

⊗ **Increased Effect:** A character receives a **-1 bonus** at rank 3, **-2** at rank 6, **-3** at rank 9, and **-4** at rank 12 on certain other skill checks that can be assisted by this specialty. These skills include Medical Science—treatment, Forensic Science, and certain Bio-Kinesis or Mutation checks.

---

## Xenology (INT - Trained Only)


*This skill can't be used untrained.*

Xenology is the study of animal life forms of an alien nature and can be used to identify or classify such life forms, including alien plant life. It allows a character to make educated guesses as to the nature, behavior, and abilities of alien life forms.

⊗ **Increased Effect:** A character receives a **-1 bonus** at rank 3, **-2** at rank 6, **-3** at rank 9, and **-4** at rank 12 on certain other skill checks that can be assisted by this specialty. These skills include Survival (in alien environments), Animal Handling (with alien creatures), and Medical Science—xenomedicine.
---

## Psionics (INT - Trained Only)


*This skill can't be used untrained.*

This specialty skill represents the scientific study of the biological and biochemical nature of psionic abilities.

⊗ **Increased Effect:** A character receives a **-1 bonus** at rank 3, **-2** at rank 6, **-3** at rank 9, and **-4** at rank 12 on certain other skill checks that can be assisted by this specialty. These skills include Telepathy—drain, Bio-Kinesis—bioluminescent, and Bio-Kinesis—bioweapon.
