This broad skill represents a character''s training in the life sciences. Skill checks involving this skill are made the same as Knowledge skill checks.

## Psionics
{{< specialty attr="WIL" untrained="no" cost="3" >}}

This specialty skill represents the scientific study of the biological and biochemical nature of psionic abilities.
⊗ **Increased Effect:** A character receives a **-1 bonus** at rank 3,
**-2** at rank 6, **-3** at rank 9, and **-4** at rank 12 on certain other skill checks that can be assisted by this specialty. These skills include Telepathy—drain, Bio-Kinesis—bioluminescent, and Bio-Kinesis—bioweapon.

---

## Biology
{{< specialty attr="WIL" untrained="yes" cost="3" >}}

Biology is the study of life and living organisms, including their structure, function, growth, origin, evolution, and distribution.

---

## Botany
{{< specialty attr="WIL" untrained="yes" cost="3" >}}

Botany is the study of plant life, from the smallest mosses to the giant trees of a rainforest.

---

## Genetics
{{< specialty attr="WIL" untrained="yes" cost="3" >}}

Genetics is the study of heredity and genes, forming the basis of genetic engineering and cloning.
⊗ **Increased Effect:** A character receives a **-1 bonus** at rank 3,
**-2** at rank 6, **-3** at rank 9, and **-4** at rank 12 on certain other skill checks that can be assisted by this specialty. These skills include Medical Science—treatment, Forensic Science, and certain Bio-Kinesis or Mutation checks.

---

## Zoology
{{< specialty attr="WIL" untrained="yes" cost="3" >}}

Zoology is the study of animals and animal life, of both terrestrial and extraterrestrial origin.
