This broad skill provides a hero with the training and ability to examine things systematically. Unlike Awareness skills, Investigate takes time.

### Results
The accuracy and level of detail are tied to the degree of success.
- **Failure:** The hero wasn't able to reach an accurate conclusion in the time allowed.

## Specialty Skills

{{% list %}}
- **Interrogate:** Asking the right questions to gain information from a target.
- **Search:** Developing an eye for detail and ability to spot hidden objects.
- **Track:** Improving the ability to follow a person, creature, or land vehicle.
