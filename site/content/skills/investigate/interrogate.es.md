title = "Interrogar"
attribute = "WIL"
+++

This skill represents a hero's ability to ask the right questions to gain information from a specific target character. The character's Will resistance modifier provides protection from probing questions.

Interrogation includes calm and reasonable debate, rhetoric, and trick questions. It may also include a hint of violence or actual pain-inducing techniques.
| Results | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ordinary** | Success reveals a significant bit of information. |
| **Good** | Success reveals a more significant bit of information. |
| **Amazing** | Success reveals a very significant bit of information. |
