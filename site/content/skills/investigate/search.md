This specialty skill enables a hero to develop his eye for detail and his ability to spot hidden objects. Search is used when a hero wants to look for clues, find a particular object or person, or hunt for important objects in as fast and systematic a fashion as possible.

The degree of success achieved on a skill check determines what the hero spots after a certain amount of time spent searching.
| Results | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ordinary** | Success reveals a single small item or piece of information. |
| **Good** | Success reveals several small items or a major piece of information. |
| **Amazing** | Success reveals a very major piece of information. |
