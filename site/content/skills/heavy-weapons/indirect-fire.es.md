This specialty skill allows a hero to improve his proficiency with indirect-fire heavy weapons. These weapons can fire over or around obstacles; they deliver an area effect attack instead of an attack against a specific target. Indirect-fire heavy weapons include grenade launchers, rocket launchers, shoulder-fired missiles, and mortars.

The accuracy of an indirect-fire weapon depends on the range and the degree of success achieved on a skill check.

### Accuracy by Range

| Skill Check Result | Short Range | Medium Range | Long Range |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Critical Failure** | Miss by 4m | Miss by 8m | Miss by 16m |
| **Failure** | Miss by 2m | Miss by 4m | Miss by 8m |
| **Ordinary** | On target | Miss by 2m | Miss by 4m |
| **Good** | On target | On target | Miss by 2m |
| **Amazing** | On target | Always on target | On target |
