This broad skill allows a hero to increase the distance he can travel in a given time and also provides him with the ability to make a journey less arduous for himself and his companions.

A hero with the Movement broad skill can engage in overland movement for longer than an untrained character before needing to make Stamina-endurance checks.
- **Stroll:** Check every 4 hours.
- **March:** Check every 3 hours.
- **Forced March:** Check every 2 hours.
