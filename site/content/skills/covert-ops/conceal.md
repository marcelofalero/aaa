Hiding is the ability to stay unnoticed by keeping quiet, by taking advantage of cover or darkness, and by remaining totally still.

If a hero is alone, no modifier applies. If someone is around, their Will resistance modifier applies to the hero's situation die. Awareness-perception is used by an observer not actively searching; Investigate-search is used if they are specifically looking for the hero.
