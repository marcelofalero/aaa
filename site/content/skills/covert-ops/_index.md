This broad skill provides a hero with the ability to hide, sneak, or otherwise move about without attracting attention. The base situation die is usually altered by the opponent’s Will resistance modifier.

Stealth cannot be used if the hero is presently in plain sight of his opponent, or if the hero is emitting sounds or smells that can reach the opponent.

### Results
The degree of success gives the observer a penalty:
- **Ordinary:** +1 penalty
- **Good:** +2 penalty
- **Amazing:** +3 penalty

### Rank Benefits
- **Increased Effect:** At rank 4, 8, and 12, the character's Stealth checks are more effective (this usually applies to all Stealth specialty skills).
