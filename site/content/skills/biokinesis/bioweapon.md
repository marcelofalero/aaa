*Extended duration.*

When employing this skill, a character generates a staff or club of biokinetic energy that can be used as a melee weapon (requires Melee Attack-bludgeon).

The bioweapon has a damage rating of d4/d4+2/d6+2. The initial skill check used to generate the weapon determines the type of damage:
- **Ordinary:** stun
- **Good:** wound
- **Amazing:** mortal
