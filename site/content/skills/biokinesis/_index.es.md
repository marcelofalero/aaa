*This skill can't be used untrained.*

This broad skill allows a character to harness the power of his mind to enhance his body's functions.

## Specialty Skills

{{% list %}}
- **Bioweapon:** Generate a staff or club of biokinetic energy.
- **Control Metabolism:** Regulate metabolic processes to survive hostile environments.
- **Heal:** Concentration to heal wound damage or disease.
- **Morph:** Alter physical features for disguise or utility.
- **Rejuvenate:** Offset fatigue or stun damage.
- **Transfer Damage:** Absorb another character's damage or disease.
