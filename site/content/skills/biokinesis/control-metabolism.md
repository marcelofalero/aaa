*Extended duration.*

Through the use of this skill, a character can regulate his metabolic processes to survive without food/water, in extreme climates, or slow functions to feign death.

### Hostile Environments
Result determines simulated protection:
- **Ordinary:** vacuum mask
- **Good:** jumpsuit
- **Amazing:** soft e-suit
After initial use, protection is maintained at 1 psionic energy point per hour.
