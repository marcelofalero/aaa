+++
title = "Rejuvenecer"
attribute = "CON"
+++

A character can offset fatigue or stun damage (but not psionic energy loss). Body's reaction lasts one hour; cannot be attempted more than once per hour.

### Results
Check provides "rejuvenation points" (2 per fatigue, 1 per stun):
- **Ordinary:** 2 points
- **Good:** 4 points
- **Amazing:** 6 points
Points must be used immediately or lost.
| Results | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ordinary** | Success indicates the hero receives 2 rejuvenation points. |
| **Good** | Success indicates the hero receives 4 rejuvenation points. |
| **Amazing** | Success indicates the hero receives 6 rejuvenation points. |
