*This skill can't be used untrained.*

By concentrating, a character can heal himself of wound damage or disease. Results are immediate but the body's reaction lasts one hour; cannot be attempted more than once per hour.

### Results
- **Ordinary:** 1 wound point; reduce disease by one grade.
- **Good:** 2 wound points; reduce disease by two grades.
- **Amazing:** 3 wound points; reduce disease by three grades.

### Rank Benefits
- **Rank 6 [Healing Mortal Damage]:** Able to heal mortal damage. Results change to: Ordinary: 2 wounds; Good: 3 wounds or 1 mortal; Amazing: 4 wounds or 2 mortals.
