*This skill can't be used untrained.*

This broad skill allows a character to harness the power of his mind to enhance his body's functions.

## Specialty Skills

### Bioweapon
*Extended duration.*

When employing this skill, a character generates a staff or club of biokinetic energy that can be used as a melee weapon (requires Melee Attack-bludgeon).

The bioweapon has a damage rating of d4/d4+2/d6+2. The initial skill check used to generate the weapon determines the type of damage:
- **Ordinary:** stun
- **Good:** wound
- **Amazing:** mortal

### Control Metabolism
*Extended duration.*

Through the use of this skill, a character can regulate his metabolic processes to survive without food/water, in extreme climates, or slow functions to feign death.

#### Hostile Environments
Result determines simulated protection:
- **Ordinary:** vacuum mask
- **Good:** jumpsuit
- **Amazing:** soft e-suit
After initial use, protection is maintained at 1 psionic energy point per hour.

### Heal
*This skill can't be used untrained.*

By concentrating, a character can heal himself of wound damage or disease. Results are immediate but the body's reaction lasts one hour; cannot be attempted more than once per hour.

#### Results
- **Ordinary:** 1 wound point; reduce disease by one grade.
- **Good:** 2 wound points; reduce disease by two grades.
- **Amazing:** 3 wound points; reduce disease by three grades.

#### Rank Benefits
- **Rank 6 [Healing Mortal Damage]:** Able to heal mortal damage. Results change to: Ordinary: 2 wounds; Good: 3 wounds or 1 mortal; Amazing: 4 wounds or 2 mortals.

### Morph
*This skill can't be used untrained.*

By using this skill, a character can alter his physical features for disguise or to accomplish tasks (squeeze into small holes, increase reach). Requires an entire round (4 phases) to complete. Mass doesn't change, but volume can.

#### Results
Check determines initial duration:
- **Ordinary:** 1 round
- **Good:** 2 rounds
- **Amazing:** 3 rounds
Extend for 1 psionic energy point per round.

#### Rank Benefits
- **Rank 1 & 3:** Select one: Elongate fingers (+0.5m reach), Elongate arms (+1m reach), Elongate legs (+1m height).
- **Rank 5 & 7:** Select one: Disguise (+2 penalty to Awareness to recognize), Elongate arms and legs simultaneously, Elongate entire body (pass through 0.5m openings).
- **Rank 10 & 12:** Select one: Improved disguise (+4 penalty to Awareness), Lessen damage (flexible body reduces LI damage: Ordinary d4, Good d4+2, Amazing d6+2), Morph control (any two forms simultaneously).
| Results | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ordinary** | Success indicates the form lasts for 1 round. |
| **Good** | Success indicates the form lasts for 2 rounds. |
| **Amazing** | Success indicates the form lasts for 3 rounds. |

### Rejuvenate
A character can offset fatigue or stun damage (but not psionic energy loss). Body's reaction lasts one hour; cannot be attempted more than once per hour.

#### Results
Check provides "rejuvenation points" (2 per fatigue, 1 per stun):
- **Ordinary:** 2 points
- **Good:** 4 points
- **Amazing:** 6 points
Points must be used immediately or lost.
| Results | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ordinary** | Success indicates the hero receives 2 rejuvenation points. |
| **Good** | Success indicates the hero receives 4 rejuvenation points. |
| **Amazing** | Success indicates the hero receives 6 rejuvenation points. |

### Transfer Damage
By laying hands on another, the hero alleviates damage or disease by absorbing it into himself.

#### Results
- **Critical Failure:** Hero suffers 1 wound.
- **Ordinary:** Absorb 1 wound; reduce illness by one grade (infecting self).
- **Good:** Absorb 2 wounds; reduce illness by two grades (infecting self).
- **Amazing:** Absorb 3 wounds or 1 mortal; transfers any illness into self.
| Results | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ordinary** | Success indicates the hero absorbs 1 wound. |
| **Good** | Success indicates the hero absorbs 2 wounds. |
| **Amazing** | Success indicates the hero absorbs 3 wounds or 1 mortal. |
