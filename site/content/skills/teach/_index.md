This broad skill represents a character's ability to instruct others in the use of a skill belonging to the Open group. A skill check is required whenever a character wants to teach a skill to another character.

### Guidance and Costs
Getting instruction from a teacher can decrease the cost for another character when that character wants to buy or improve a skill.
- **Teacher Limitation:** A teacher **cannot teach himself** or herself.
- **Cost Floor:** The cost to buy or improve a skill can **never be decreased to less than 1** skill point.
- **Student Resource:** The character to be taught must have enough skill points (XP) to purchase the skill at its (possibly reduced) cost.

---

### The Teaching Check
The benefit of a teacher depends on the result of the teacher's skill check:

- **Critical Failure:** The student doesn't learn or improve the skill at this time.
- **Failure:** The student must pay the **full skill cost**.
- **Ordinary:** The student pays **cost -1**.
- **Good:** The student pays **cost -2**.
- **Amazing:** The student pays **cost -3**.

### Mentorship Limits
- **One at a Time:** A teacher can't teach more than one student at a time.
- **One per Session:** Only one new skill or one rank of improvement can be gained by a student during any teaching session (usually occurring between adventures).
- **Mastery Limit:** A teacher can only teach a specialty skill up to **one-half of his or her current rank** in that skill. For example, a teacher with Animal Handling—animal training at rank 4 can only teach someone else that skill up to rank 2.

---

# Specialty Skills

## Teach, Specific Field
### (WIL - Trained Only)
*This skill can’t be used untrained.*

This specialty skill allows a character to expand their teaching ability to specialized profession groups. For each separate purchase of this specialty skill, the character selects a profession group: **Combat Spec**, **Diplomat**, **Free Agent**, or **Tech Op**. 

The character then becomes able to teach all skills associated with that profession group in addition to those belonging to the Open group. (e.g., a teacher specialized in Combat Spec can help another character learn or improve a Combat Spec skill).

⊗ **Mindwalker Training:** If the optional rules for Psionics are being used, a teacher who also has psionic abilities can teach psionic skills to another character who also has psionic abilities.
