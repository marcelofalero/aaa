This broad skill represents a hero's proficiency at deceiving others through conversation and interaction. It covers all verbal tricks and subterfuges, but changes in attitude brought about by this skill are not necessarily long-lasting.

A hero's situation die is modified by the target's Intelligence resistance modifier.

## Specialty Skills

### Bluff (PER)

Bluff is the talent for misleading or deceiving through false bravado, outright lies, or displaying extreme confidence. It is the ability to make another character believe something that isn't true.

#### Results
- **Critical Failure:** Bluff is seen through; target becomes Hostile or Combative.
- **Failure:** Patter is considered annoying and condescending.
- **Ordinary:** Bluff works for the immediate scene.
- **Good:** Target's attitude improves by one grade for d8+1 time units.
- **Amazing:** Target's attitude improves by two grades for d12+2 time units.

| **Good** | Success indicates that the target's attitude improves by one grade. |
| **Amazing** | Success indicates that the target's attitude improves by two grades. |

### Bribe (PER)

Bribe is used when a hero wants to offer another character money, goods, information, or services to get what he wants.

#### Results
- **Critical Failure:** Bribe is refused; target becomes Hostile or Combative.
- **Failure:** Bribe is refused; each subsequent attempt must raise the value.
- **Ordinary:** Bribe is accepted, but the attitude of the target is unchanged.
- **Good:** Bribe is accepted; the target’s attitude improves by one grade for 2d6 hours.
- **Amazing:** Bribe is accepted; the target's attitude improves by two grades for 4d6 hours.
| Results | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ordinary** | Success indicates that the bribe is accepted. |
| **Good** | Success indicates that the target's attitude improves by one grade. |
| **Amazing** | Success indicates that the target's attitude improves by two grades. |

### Gamble (PER)

This specialty skill represents a hero's proficiency at various games of chance. With each purchase, the player selects a different game.

Success allows estimating odds, determining if a game is rigged, counting cards, and spotting a cheater.

#### Contests
If playing honestly, the winner is the one with the highest success level. Situation modifiers are based on the opponent's skill:
- **Untrained opponent:** -2 bonus.
- **Trained opponent (same or lower rank):** no modifier.
- **Trained opponent (higher rank):** +2 penalty.

#### Cheating
If a hero decides to cheat, different modifiers apply:
- **Untrained opponent:** -3 bonus.
- **Trained opponent (same or lower rank):** +1 penalty.
- **Trained opponent (higher rank):** +3 penalty.
On any Critical Failure, the hero is caught cheating.
| Results | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ordinary** | Success indicates that the hero wins the pot plus 1/2. |
| **Good** | Success indicates that the hero wins the pot plus an equal side bet. |
| **Amazing** | Success indicates that the hero wins the pot plus twice that amount. |
