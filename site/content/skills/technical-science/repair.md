This skill provides a hero with training in the repair and maintenance of mechanical and electronic technology. Any use of repair requires a complex skill check.

### Complexity
- **Stun Damage:** Ordinary complexity.
- **Wound Damage:** Good complexity.
- **Mortal Damage:** Amazing complexity (may require more than 10 successes).
The time unit is designated by the Gamemaster (minutes, hours, days, etc.).
