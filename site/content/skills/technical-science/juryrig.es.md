This skill enables a hero to make fast, patchwork repairs in order to get an electronic or mechanical device up and running for a short period of time. A juryrig repair can be made successfully once on a specific system.

Making a juryrig skill check is a single action, and the character can't attempt other actions for the rest of the round.

### Results
- **Critical Failure:** System becomes unusable and requires full repairs.
- **Failure:** Juryrig attempt doesn't work.
- **Ordinary:** Operational for d6+1 time units; 3 more successes needed for full repairs.
- **Good:** Operational for 2d4+2 time units; 2 more successes needed for full repairs.
- **Amazing:** Operational for 3d4+2 time units; 1 more success needed for full repairs.

### Rank Benefits
- **Improved Juryrig:** At rank 6: Ordinary produces a Good result, Good produces an Amazing result, and Amazing success means the system is fully repaired and operational.
