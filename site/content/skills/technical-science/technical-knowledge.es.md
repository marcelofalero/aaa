This skill represents the study of technology and how up-to-date a hero is on techniques or procedures.

### Rank Benefits
- **Increased Effect:** Situation die bonus to Technical Science, Invention, Juryrig, or Repair: -1 at rank 3, -2 at rank 6, -3 at rank 9, and -4 at rank 12.
