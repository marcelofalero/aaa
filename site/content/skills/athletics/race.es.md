*This skill can't be used untrained.*

This specialty skill allows a hero to improve his ability to move faster and cover more ground in a combat scene. A character who purchases rank 1 in this specialty immediately improves his run movement rate by 2 meters.

### Rank Benefits
- **Run Rate:** Increases by 2 meters at ranks 5 and 9.
- **Sprint Rate:** Increases by 2 meters at ranks 4, 7, and 12.
