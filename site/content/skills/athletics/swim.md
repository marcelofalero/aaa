*Except to determine if a hero can tread water to avoid drowning, this skill can’t be used untrained.*

This specialty skill is necessary for a hero to be able to use his swim movement rate. Without this skill, a hero can only tread water and paddle in a crude fashion.

### Rank Benefits
- **Rank 1 [Holding Breath]:** Any Stamina-endurance checks a hero must make to keep holding his breath are made with a -1 bonus.
- **Rank 4, 8, 12 [Improved Breathing]:** The bonus to Stamina-endurance checks improves to -2 (rank 4), -3 (rank 8), and -4 (rank 12).
- **Rank 4, 8, 12 [Movement Rate]:** Both swim and easy swim rates increase by 1 meter at each of these ranks.
