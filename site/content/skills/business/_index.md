This broad skill provides a character with basic knowledge of the theories of production, distribution, supply and demand, advertising, inventory control, and business planning.

### Uses
Skill checks are called for when a character wants to:
- Play the stock market.
- Apply business knowledge to conclude a trade.
- Attempt an illegal business transaction.
- Determine the value of goods in their own society.
- Locate commodity marketplaces.
- Compute the local fair market value of goods and services.

### Interaction and Haggling
If a character uses Business to haggle over the buying or selling of a commodity, the other party's **Intelligence** resistance modifier is applied to the situation die.
- **Character vs. Character:** If the other party also has the Business skill, the Gamemaster may handle it as a contested check.
- **Encounter Skill:** Business can be treated as an [encounter skill](/core-mechanics/characters-in-action/#encounter-scene). If the target's attitude becomes Friendly or better, it provides a bonus to subsequent cooperation checks.

### Modifiers
- **Penalties:** Usually apply when dealing in exotic, rare, or unfamiliar goods; attempting illegal transactions; or dealing with an unfamiliar culture.
- **Bonuses:** Apply if the character has inside information or is dealing with a familiar commodity or culture.

## Corporate
{{< specialty attr="INT" untrained="yes" cost="3" >}}

This specialty skill improves a character's ability to function in the realm of large-scale trade and commerce. It covers stock markets, mergers, corporate acquisitions, and speculative business. It also provides an understanding of how large organizations and corporations operate.

---

## Illicit business
{{< specialty attr="INT" untrained="yes" cost="3" >}}

This specialty skill allows a character to function in the underside of the business world, providing knowledge of extortion, smuggling, fencing stolen goods, and the black market. It is the primary skill of white-collar criminals (embezzlers, insider traders, corporate spies).

### Illegal Transactions
This skill reduces the penalty for attempting illegal transactions.
- **Initial Purchase:** Penalty reduced by -1 step.
- **Rank 4:** Penalty reduction of -2 steps.
- **Rank 7:** Penalty reduction of -3 steps.
- **Rank 10:** Penalty reduction of -4 steps.
*Note: This benefit only reduces or eliminates penalties; it never provides a bonus.

---

## Small business
{{< specialty attr="INT" untrained="yes" cost="3" >}}

This specialty skill allows a character to function in small-scale trade and commerce. It improves the ability to haggle on the consumer level, determine the value of goods and services, and find buyers and sellers in a local area.

### Financial Bargaining
This skill provides a situation die bonus when making deals, haggling, or bargaining in small-business finance.
- **Initial Purchase:** -1 step bonus.
- **Rank 4:** -2 step bonus.
- **Rank 8:** -3 step bonus.
- **Rank 12:** -4 step bonus.
