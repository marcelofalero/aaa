title = "Beneficios de rango"
attribute = "STR"
+++

*   **Resistance Modifier:** The hero's Dexterity resistance modifier improves by +1 at rank 3, by +1 again at rank 7, and by +1 again at rank 11.
*   **Reaction Parry:** At rank 4, a hero with a Melee Weapons specialty skill can make a “reaction parry” as his first action in a round, allowing him to parry an attack made in a phase earlier than his own.
*   **Double-Strike:** At rank 6, a hero can make two attacks as a single action (+2 penalty).
    *   **Ordinary:** 1 attack, normal damage.
    *   **Good:** 2 attacks, normal damage.
    *   **Amazing:** 2 attacks, Good damage.
*   **Multistrike:** At rank 12, an Amazing success on a Double-Strike allows three attacks that each inflict Good damage.
