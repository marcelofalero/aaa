This specialty skill enables a hero to hone his abilities to use reason and logic to reach a conclusion. It is used by detectives, scientists, engineers, and others to discover the answer to a skill-related problem.

### Rank Benefits
- **Resistance Modifier:** The hero's Intelligence resistance modifier (only for deceiving the hero) improves by +1 at rank 4, by +1 again at rank 8, and by +1 again at rank 12.
