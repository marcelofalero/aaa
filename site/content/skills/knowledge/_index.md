This broad skill represents a hero's ability to know at least a little bit about any particular subject.

### Knowledge Categories
For a Knowledge skill check, the Gamemaster determines which category the information sought belongs to:
- **Trivial:** No modifier; Marginal complexity.
- **General:** +1 step; Ordinary complexity.
- **Specific:** +2 steps; Good complexity.
- **Expert:** +3 steps; Amazing complexity.

## Specialty Skills

### Deduce (INT)

This specialty skill enables a hero to hone his abilities to use reason and logic to reach a conclusion. It is used by detectives, scientists, engineers, and others to discover the answer to a skill-related problem.

#### Rank Benefits
- **Resistance Modifier:** The hero's Intelligence resistance modifier (only for deceiving the hero) improves by +1 at rank 4, by +1 again at rank 8, and by +1 again at rank 12.

### Language (INT)

*This skill can't be used untrained.*

Every hero begins with rank 3 in his or her native language (speak and read as well as a native). Lower ranks indicate crude familiarity; higher ranks improve vocabulary and deeper comprehension.

At the Gamemaster's discretion, a hero's skill (or lack thereof) with a language might provide a situation die modifier to the use of other skills involving communication.
- **Rank 1:** +3 penalty on communication attempts.

#### Rank Benefits
- **Improved Communication:** The penalty on communication is reduced to +1 at rank 2 and eliminated at rank 3. Rank 6 provides a -1 bonus, rank 9 a -2 bonus, and rank 12 a -3 bonus.

### First Aid (INT)

*This skill can't be used untrained.*

This specialty skill represents basic training in first aid techniques, including the use of trauma packs and first aid kits. It doesn't provide a hero with the ability to use surgical tools or specialized gauntlets.

#### Heal Stun Damage
Successful skill check restores 2 (Ordinary), 3 (Good), or 4 (Amazing) stun points to a conscious patient.

#### Knockout Recovery
Successful skill check brings a character back to consciousness and recovers 1 (Ordinary), 2 (Good), or 3 (Amazing) stun points.

#### Heal Wound Damage
Healing wounds in a combat scene requires a complex skill check and either a first aid kit or a trauma pack.
- **Good Complexity:** Using a first aid kit (1 wound healed on completion).
- **Ordinary Complexity:** Using a trauma pack (2 wounds healed on completion).
A Critical Failure deals 1 wound and ruins the attempt.

#### Dying
A hero who uses either a first aid kit or a trauma pack and makes a successful skill check can stabilize mortal damage, removing the need for regular Stamina-endurance checks until surgery can be performed.

#### Rank Benefits
- **Improved First Aid:** Reduction in situation die penalties: 1 step at rank 4, 2 steps at rank 8, and 3 steps at rank 12.

### World Knowledge (INT)

Specific info about a planet, system, or academic field (e.g., History, Math).

### Street Knowledge (INT)

Local info about a specific city or region.

### Computer Operation (INT)

*This skill can't be used untrained.*

This skill allows a character to improve his ability to make computers perform, specifically his ability to get the most out of software (including the operating system).
The degree of success achieved relates to the quality or quantity of the information accessed or retrieved, or the speed of performance.
