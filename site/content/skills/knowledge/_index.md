This broad skill represents a hero's ability to know at least a little bit about any particular subject.

### Knowledge Categories
For a Knowledge skill check, the Gamemaster determines which category the information sought belongs to:
- **Trivial:** No modifier; Marginal complexity.
- **General:** +1 step; Ordinary complexity.
- **Specific:** +2 steps; Good complexity.
- **Expert:** +3 steps; Amazing complexity.
