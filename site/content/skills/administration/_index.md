This broad skill provides a hero with knowledge of the forms and procedures for running an office or organization, including scheduling, personnel, and equipment management.

It can also be treated as an encounter skill to change the attitude of an employee of some corporation or organization.

### Bureaucracy Results
Getting results out of a bureaucracy requires a complex skill check. The situation modifier relates to how quickly the character wants results:
- **Amazing (+3 penalty):** Answer in 1 day.
- **Good (+2 penalty):** Settle for d4+1 days.
- **Ordinary (+1 penalty):** Wait for d4+2 days.
- **Marginal:** Wait for d6+3 days.

### Complexity
Relates to the size of the system and ease of request:
- **Marginal:** Small system/extremely easy request.
- **Ordinary:** Medium-sized system/routine request.
- **Good:** Large system/complex request.
- **Amazing:** Extremely large system/restricted or illegal request.
