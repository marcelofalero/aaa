+++
title = "Mind Reading"
attribute = "INT"
+++

Read surface thoughts of another character in visual contact. Cannot be extended with psionic energy.

### Results
- **Ordinary:** Lasts 1 phase. Random/disconnected thoughts (name, what they are looking at).
- **Good:** Lasts 2 phases. More detail/coherence (why they are here).
- **Amazing:** Lasts 3 phases. Complete surface thoughts and a key fact.
| Results | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ordinary** | Success indicates reading surface thoughts for 1 phase. |
| **Good** | Success indicates reading surface thoughts for 2 phases. |
| **Amazing** | Success indicates reading surface thoughts for 3 phases. |
