Instinctively determine present location and mentally plot courses without charts or instruments. Replaces Navigation skill.

- **Rank 1:** Select one specialty: surface, system astrogation, or drivespace astrogation.
- **Rank 5:** Select a second specialty.
- **Rank 9:** Final specialty becomes available.
