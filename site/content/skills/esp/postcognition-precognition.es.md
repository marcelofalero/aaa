Receive impressions about the past (Postcognition) or future (Precognition).

### Results
- **Ordinary:** Sense general emotions (past) or vague images (future).
- **Good:** Brief flashes of events.
- **Amazing:** Brief encounter as though at the scene.

### Postcognition
See time units (hours/days) into the past equal to skill rank.

### Precognition
See time units (hours/days) into the future equal to skill rank. Usually GM-triggered.
- **Forcing a flash:** Cost is doubled (2/4/6 pts); +3 penalty; cannot use consciously again for 2d6 days.
| Results | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ordinary** | Success indicates vague images/emotions. |
| **Good** | Success indicates brief flashes. |
| **Amazing** | Success indicates a brief encounter as though at the scene. |
