This broad skill provides a hero with training in the use of modern ranged weapons. This skill covers pistols, rifles, shotguns, and submachine guns of all types, including projectile and energy weapons.

All modern weapons have ammunition clips. To change a clip during a combat scene, a hero must use an available action. If he wants to change a clip and shoot in the same phase, a +2 penalty applies to the attack action.
