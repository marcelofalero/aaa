This specialty skill allows a hero to improve his proficiency with modern pistols of all types, including projectile and energy weapons.

If a hero wants to draw and fire his pistol in the same phase, the action receives a +1 penalty.

### Rank Benefits
- **Rank 3 [Quick Draw]:** Perform a quick draw and fire in the same phase without the +1 penalty.
- **Other Benefits:** See Ranged Weapons Rank Benefits.
