This specialty skill allows a hero to improve his proficiency with modern submachine guns of all types.

All SMGs are automatic weapons capable of firing multiple rounds of ammunition in every attack.

### Rank Benefits
- **Rank 3 [Rock-n-Roll]:** Change clips more efficiently. The penalty for changing a clip and firing in the same action is reduced to +1 step.
- **Rank 6 [Precision Shooting]:** Situation die penalties for making an autofire attack with an SMG are reduced to 0, +1 step, and +2 steps.
- **Rank 9 [Extra Burst]:** Get off four bursts of ammunition instead of three on every autofire attack. He receives a fourth situation die with a +3 penalty assigned to it.
- **Other Benefits:** See Ranged Weapons Rank Benefits.
