This specialty skill enables a hero to racing over dangerous terrain, making jumps, and engaging in mounted combat.

### Rank Benefits
- **Rank 3 [Trick Riding]:** Direct mount to jump fences, leap chasms, or move backward.
    - **Leap (Horizontal):** Ordinary: +1m; Good: +2m; Amazing: +3m.
    - **Leap (Over Obstacle):** Ordinary: +1m; Good: +1.5m; Amazing: +2m.
    - **Move Backward:** Ordinary: 1/2 walk rate; Good: full walk rate; Amazing: 1.5x walk rate.
    - **Mount as Cover:** Provides a penalty to an opponent's chance to hit the rider: Ordinary (+1); Good (+2); Amazing (+3).
Trick riding can't be combined with any other action.
