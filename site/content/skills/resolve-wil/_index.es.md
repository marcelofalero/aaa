This broad skill provides a hero with the inner strength of will necessary to defend against mental stress or attacks, stand up to physical pain, and stick to his convictions.

## Specialty Skills

{{% list %}}
- **Mental Resolve:** cope with emotional or mental stress of all kinds.
- **Physical Resolve:** cope with physical trauma of all kinds and bounce back from damage.
