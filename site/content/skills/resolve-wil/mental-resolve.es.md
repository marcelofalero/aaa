This specialty skill is used to determine a hero's ability to cope with emotional or mental stress. The higher a hero's rank in mental resolve, the more resistant he is to trauma, fear, mind control, and mental powers.

### Results
- **Critical Failure:** The hero collapses in anguish and can't act, or flees in the face of extreme fear for d4 rounds.
- **Failure:** The hero freezes and loses one action.
- **Ordinary:** The hero can act normally but receives a +1 penalty to all actions for 1 round.
- **Good/Amazing:** The hero puts aside his negative emotions and can act without penalty.

### Rank Benefits
- **Resistance Modifier:** The hero's Will resistance modifier improves by +1 at rank 4, by +1 again at rank 8, and by +1 again at rank 12.
