*This skill can't be used untrained.*

The musical instrument skill represents a talent for playing a particular musical instrument and reading music. For each separate purchase, the hero selects a specific instrument (drums, guitars, violins, flutes, saxophones, etc.).
| Results | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ordinary** | Success indicates that the hero produced a quality piece. |
| **Good** | Success indicates that the hero produced a very quality piece. |
| **Amazing** | Success indicates that the hero produced a masterpiece. |
