This specialty skill represents a talent for playing a part or assuming a dramatic role. It also covers impersonation and the techniques of stage and screen.

### Rank Benefits
- **Rank 4 [Disguise]:** Use stage and film makeup and techniques to change appearance or look like someone else.
- **Applying a Disguise:** Requires a complex skill check (Ordinary, Good, or Amazing complexity). If successful, observers receive a penalty to Awareness checks: +1 (Ordinary), +2 (Good), +3 (Amazing).
| Results | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ordinary** | Success indicates that the hero produced a quality performance. |
| **Good** | Success indicates that the hero produced a very quality performance. |
| **Amazing** | Success indicates that the hero produced a masterpiece performance. |
