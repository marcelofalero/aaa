The sing specialty represents a talent for carrying a note and singing songs. A hero with this skill is a trained vocalist with a strong and melodious voice.
| Results | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ordinary** | Success indicates that the hero produced a quality song. |
| **Good** | Success indicates that the hero produced a very quality song. |
| **Amazing** | Success indicates that the hero produced a masterpiece song. |
