title = "Charm"
attribute = "PER"
+++

This specialty skill represents a hero's ability to change the attitudes of those he interacts with by presenting himself in a likable, friendly manner.

Charm can only be used on targets whose attitude is Neutral or Friendly. A character can't charm a Hostile or Combative target.
| Results | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ordinary** | Success indicates that the hero produced a quality charm. |
| **Good** | Success indicates that the hero produced a very quality charm. |
| **Amazing** | Success indicates that the hero produced a masterpiece charm. |
