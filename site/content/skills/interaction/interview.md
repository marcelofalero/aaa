This skill represents a hero's ability to get information from another character in a non-aggressive way. It differs from interrogate in that the target is generally willing to share information.

Interview cannot be used on someone with a Hostile or Combative attitude toward the questioner.
| Results | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ordinary** | Success indicates that the hero produced a quality interview. |
| **Good** | Success indicates that the hero produced a very quality interview. |
| **Amazing** | Success indicates that the hero produced a masterpiece interview. |
