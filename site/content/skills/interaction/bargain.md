+++
title = "Bargain"
attribute = "PER"
+++

This specialty skill represents a hero's ability to make and close deals. It provides the skills to haggle, negotiate, and bluster.

Situation modifiers include supply and demand, as well as the target's Will resistance modifier. The greater the degree of success, the better the deal for the hero.
| Results | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ordinary** | Success indicates that the hero produced a quality deal. |
| **Good** | Success indicates that the hero produced a very quality deal. |
| **Amazing** | Success indicates that the hero produced a masterpiece deal. |
