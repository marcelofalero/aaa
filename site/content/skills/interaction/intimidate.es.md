title = "Intimidar"
attribute = "PER"
+++

This skill represents a hero's ability to threaten another character, by physical presence or the weight of authority. Intimidate enables a hero to force an opponent to back down, reveal information, or cooperate through fear.

Target's Will resistance modifier and attitude apply. It's not possible to intimidate a Fanatic or Combative character.
| Results | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ordinary** | Success indicates that the hero produced a quality intimidation. |
| **Good** | Success indicates that the hero produced a very quality intimidation. |
| **Amazing** | Success indicates that the hero produced a masterpiece intimidation. |
