*This skill can't be used untrained.*

Surgery is used to heal mortal damage and perform other invasive medical procedures. It is a complex skill check and cannot be performed during combat scenes.

### Complexity
Complexity depends on the amount of damage being treated:
- **Mortal Damage:** 1pt: Marginal; 2pt: Ordinary; 3pt: Good; 4+pt: Amazing.
- **Wound Damage:** 2pt: Marginal; 4pt: Ordinary; 6pt: Good; 8+pt: Amazing.
Every two successes achieved heals 1 point of mortal damage and 2 points of wound damage.

### Conditions
- **Critical (+4):** Poor lighting, no tools, no assistance.
- **Good (-2):** Modern ambulance or ship sick bay.
- **Amazing (-3):** Standard hospital or advanced sick bay.
- **Best (-4):** High-tech tools, trained support staff.

### Rank Benefits
- **Cybernetic Surgery:** At rank 3, repair or heal organisms with cybernetic implants. At rank 6, can install cybernetic implants.
