Forensics is the study of causes of death and physical evidence at the scene of a crime or accident.

### Rank Benefits
- **Increased Effect:** Situation die bonus of -1 at rank 3, -2 at rank 6, -3 at rank 9, and -4 at rank 12 on skill checks that can be assisted by this specialty.
