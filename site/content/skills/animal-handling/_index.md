This broad skill provides a hero with the basic ability to train animals for labor, riding, tracking, and attacking. Purchasing the broad skill entitles a hero to select one type of animal to train.

### Training
Requires a complex skill check and at least 4 hours a day working with the creature. After each week of continuous work, a skill check indicates successes. Interruption of a week means all successes are lost.
- **Critical Failure:** Animal goes berserk and attacks its trainer.
- **Three Failure Results:** Animal refuses to be trained.

### Task Complexity
- **Ordinary:** Simple, one-word commands (Attack, Come, Sit, Stay).
- **Good:** Combination of two Ordinary tasks, or more difficult (pushing a lever).
- **Amazing:** Very few animals can or will do on command (running into a burning building).

## Specialty Skills

- **Animal Training:** Handle, ride, and train a specific type of animal.
