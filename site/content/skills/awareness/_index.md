This broad skill represents a hero's powers of observation, perception, and intuition. Skill checks are called for by the Gamemaster to determine if a hero notices something.

### Results
- **Failure:** The hero doesn't notice a thing.
- **Success:** Provides at least a small clue. The better the level of success, the more details are revealed.

## Specialty Skills

### Intuition (WIL)

This specialty skill represents a hero's natural instincts—his ability to sense danger when no obvious signs are evident, to avoid surprise, or to make an intuitive leap of logic.

#### Avoid Surprise
Success indicates that the hero isn't surprised and can act in the surprise phase of the upcoming round.

#### Intuitive "Best Guess"
The Gamemaster may allow a player to call for an intuition skill check. Any success yields a beneficial result, with greater levels of success yielding more accurate and complete conclusions. This is only permitted to heroes who have purchased the specialty skill.
| Results | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ordinary** | Success yields a beneficial result. |
| **Good** | Success yields a more accurate and complete conclusion. |
| **Amazing** | Success yields a very accurate and complete conclusion. |

### Perception (WIL)

This specialty skill represents a hero's alertness and powers of observation using normal senses—a broken twig, the click of a gun's safety, the scent of perfume.

#### Avoid Surprise
When potential danger obviously exists, a hero's subconscious use of this skill can help him avoid being caught by surprise. Success indicates that the hero isn't surprised and can act in the surprise phase of the upcoming round.
| Results | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ordinary** | Success indicates that the hero isn't surprised and can act in the surprise phase. |
| **Good** | Success indicates that the hero isn't surprised and can act in the surprise phase. |
| **Amazing** | Success indicates that the hero isn't surprised and can act in the surprise phase. |
