## Heavy Weapons

### Weapon Technical Knowledge
Characters who operate heavy weapons — soldiers, combat engineers, and weapons specialists — inevitably absorb a working technical understanding of their ordnance. They learn how propellants, warheads, and firing mechanisms work, what can fail under field conditions, and how to prevent or correct it. As well as keeping informed of what heavy hardware has to offer. 

A character with the [**Technical Science**](/skills/technical-science/) broad skill can use the appropriate Heavy Weapons specialty skill for checks that involve knowing the existence of a particular weapon system, customizing ordnance and munitions, as well as recognizing, repairing, and maintaining heavy weapons in the field. 

### Heavy Weapons Range Modifiers
Each heavy weapon has a set of range figures. Situation die modifiers for range, as shown below on **TABLE P21: HEAVY WEAPONS RANGE MODIFIERS**, may apply when a certain type of heavy weapon is used against a target or a location that lies within a particular range category. In addition, the accuracy of an indirect-fire weapon depends on the range between the shooter and the target location as well as the type of success achieved, as shown on [**TABLE P20: ACCURACY BY RANGE**](/skills/athletics/#accuracy-by-range). A few direct-fire heavy weapons (as well as some rifles and all submachine guns) are [**automatic weapons**](/skills/modern-ranged-weapons/#automatic-weapon-attack-modes) capable of firing multiple rounds of ammunition in every attack.

#### TABLE P21: HEAVY WEAPONS RANGE MODIFIERS
| Range | Direct | Indirect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Short** | -1 step | +2 steps |
| **Medium** | None | -2 steps |
| **Long** | +1 step | None |
