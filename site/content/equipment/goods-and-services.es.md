- <a id="antiscan-weave:"></a>**Antiscan Weave:** A specialized fabric that blocks or distorts low-power sensor scans, making the wearer harder to detect or identify with electronic equipment.
- <a id="backpack:"></a>**Backpack:** A standard rucksack made of durable synthetic material. It can carry up to 20 kg of gear comfortably.
- <a id="bioholster:"></a>**Bioholster:** A weapon holster secured with a biometric lock. It only releases the weapon when it detects the owner's palm print or DNA signature.
- <a id="boots:"></a>**Boots:** Sturdy footwear designed for rough terrain or combat situations.
- <a id="briefcase:"></a>**Briefcase:** A reinforced case for carrying documents or small electronic devices. Often includes a simple combination lock.
- <a id="business-dress:"></a>**Business Dress:** Standard professional attire suitable for office environments or formal meetings.
- <a id="casual-dress:"></a>**Casual Dress:** Comfortable everyday clothing appropriate for off-duty hours.
- <a id="coat:"></a>**Coat:** A heavy outer garment providing protection against cold weather or light rain.
- <a id="fatigues:"></a>**Fatigues:** Utility clothing designed for field work or combat, often with camouflage patterns.
- <a id="formal-dress:"></a>**Formal Dress:** High-end clothing for gala events or diplomatic functions.
- <a id="glasses:"></a>**Glasses:** Corrective eyewear or fashion accessories. Can be fitted with simple display overlays at higher PLs.
- <a id="goggles-protective:"></a>**Goggles, Protective:** Shatter-resistant eyewear to protect against debris, wind, or bright flashes.
- <a id="holster-scabbard:"></a>**Holster/Scabbard:** A standard carrier for a sidearm or melee weapon.
- <a id="jewelry:"></a>**Jewelry:** Decorative items made of precious metals or gems.
- <a id="pouch:"></a>**Pouch:** A small bag attached to a belt or harness for carrying small items like ammo or tools.
- <a id="shoes-athletic:"></a>**Shoes, Athletic:** Lightweight footwear designed for running or sports.
- <a id="shoes-business:"></a>**Shoes, Business:** Formal footwear to match business attire.
- <a id="shoes-formal:"></a>**Shoes, Formal:** Elegant footwear for dress occasions.
- <a id="stealth-cloak:"></a>**Stealth Cloak:** A cloak made of advanced light-bending material that grants a bonus to Stealth checks when stationary or moving slowly.
- <a id="utility-harness:"></a>**Utility Harness:** A webbing system worn over clothing that allows quick access to tools, grenades, and other small equipment.
- <a id="watch:"></a>**Watch:** A wrist-mounted timekeeping device. Higher PL versions may include basic bio-monitors or communicators.

- <a id="mass-transceiver:"></a>**Mass Transceiver:** A heavy-duty, long-range communication array capable of sending signals across continents or to orbital stations.
- <a id="orbital-uplink:"></a>**Orbital Uplink:** A portable satellite dish or laser comm system designed to link directly with satellites or starships in orbit.
- <a id="radio-personal:"></a>**Radio, Personal:** A short-range (1-5 km) handheld or headset radio for direct team communication without relying on external networks.
