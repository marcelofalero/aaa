You make your living in the gray areas between borders and laws. Moving illicit cargo requires a steady nerve, knowledge of the black market, and the ability to lie to an inspector’s face.

## Favored Broad Skill:
*   None.

## Favored Specialty Skills:
*   [Business-Illicit Business](/skills/business/#illicit-business)
*   [Covert Ops-Conceal](/skills/covert-ops/#conceal)
*   [Deception-Bribe](/skills/deception/#bribe)
*   [Street Smart-Criminal Elements](/skills/street-smart/#criminal-elements)

## Favored Perks:
*   [Good Luck](/perks_flaws/#good-luck)

## Automatic Flaw:
*   [Powerful Enemy](/perks_flaws/#powerful-enemy) (A relentless customs inspector or a syndicate boss you double-crossed).

## Starting Equipment:
An oversized coat with hidden sensor-baffled pockets, a holdout blaster, a physical ledger, and a set of fake transponder codes or IDs.

## Special Ability: Smuggler's Hold
You receive a -1 step bonus to [Covert Ops-Conceal](/skills/covert-ops/#conceal) checks when hiding objects, data, or people from physical inspections or routine sensor sweeps.

## Tendencies (Pick 2):
*   **The Contrabandist:** Take a risk to sneak something illegal or restricted into a secure area, even if the mission doesn't strictly require it.
*   **The Burner:** Dump valuable cargo, abandon a lucrative deal, or cut ties with a useful contact at the first sign of official scrutiny.
*   **Fast Talker:** Intentionally "confusion-bomb" a customs agent or inspector with irrelevant info, which might backfire and cause a full inspection.
*   **Shady Contact:** Insist on meeting a specific, unreliable contact in person rather than using a secure drop, risking an ambush for old times' sake.
