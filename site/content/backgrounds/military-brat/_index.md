You grew up in the shadow of the service. You don't have the "character" genes, but you have the discipline.

## Favored Broad Skill:
* None.

## Favored Specialty Skills:
- [Tactics-Infantry Tactics] ({{< relref "/skills/tactics/#infantry-tactics" >}})
- [Resolve-Physical Resolve] ({{< relref "/skills/resolve/#physical-resolve" >}})
- [Knowledge-First Aid] ({{< relref "/skills/knowledge/#first-aid" >}})

## Favored Perks:
* None.

## Automatic Flaw:
* [Code of Honor] ({{< relref "/perks_flaws/#code-of-honor" >}})

## Starting Equipment:
A piece of surplus military gear (like a rugged fatigue jacket or combat boots), a basic field medkit, and a standard-issue sidearm.

## Special Ability:
**Standard Procedure:** You have a -1 step bonus to [Resolve-Mental Resolve] ({{< relref "/skills/resolve/#mental-resolve" >}}) or [Resolve-Physical Resolve] ({{< relref "/skills/resolve/#physical-resolve" >}}) checks during high-stress situations as long as you are following a clear, pre-established plan or order.

## Tendencies (Pick 2):
- **The Order-Follower:** Adhere to a plan or instruction even when circumstances have shifted.
- **The Protector:** Put yourself at risk to cover an ally's flank.
