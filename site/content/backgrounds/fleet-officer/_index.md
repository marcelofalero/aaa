While ground forces fight for meters, you fight for systems. You
understand the complex dance of starship systems and the strategic
weight of the high ground in the void.

## Favored Broad Skill:
* None.

## Favored Specialty Skills:
* [Tactics-Space Tactics]({{< relref "/skills/tactics/#space-tactics" >}})
* [Navigation-Astrogation System]({{< relref "/skills/navigation/#astrogation-system" >}})
* [System Operation-Sensors]({{< relref "/skills/system-operation/#sensors" >}})
* [Leadership-Command]({{< relref "/skills/leadership/#command" >}})

## Favored Perks:
* [Reputation]({{< relref "/perks_flaws/#reputation" >}})

## Automatic Flaw:
* [Code of Honor]({{< relref "/perks_flaws/#code-of-honor" >}}) (Strict
adherence to naval protocol and superiority).

## Starting Equipment:
A naval uniform, a personal void-suit (vacuum suit) designed for
zero-G emergencies, and an officer's sidearm.

## Special Ability:
**Bridge Intuition:** You have a -1 step bonus to all
[System Operation]({{< relref "/skills/system-operation/" >}})
specialty skill checks while on the bridge of a starship, representing
your deep familiarity with fleet-standard interfaces and emergency
protocols.

## Tendencies (Pick 2):
* **The Ship’s Soul:** Prioritize the structural integrity and long-term
maintenance of the vessel over personal equipment or mission
objectives.
* **The Protocol Purist:** Insist on following standard
naval communications and engagement protocols even when operating in
the "wild" or with civilian crews, potentially causing social
friction.
