You are a relentless investigative journalist or freelance whistleblower. You use words to topple corporate empires and expose government corruption, making you a very dangerous person to know.

## Favored Broad Skill:
* [Creativity]({{< relref "/skills/creativity/" >}})

## Favored Specialty Skills:
- [Creativity-Writing] ({{< relref "/skills/creativity/#writing" >}})
- [Investigate-Search] ({{< relref "/skills/investigate/#search" >}})
- [Interaction-Interview] ({{< relref "/skills/interaction/#interview" >}})
- [Street Smart-Street Knowledge] ({{< relref "/skills/street-smart/#street-knowledge" >}})

## Favored Perks:
* None.

## Automatic Flaw:
* [Obsessed] ({{< relref "/perks_flaws/#obsessed" >}}) (With uncovering the absolute truth of a situation, regardless of the danger it poses to yourself or your allies).

## Starting Equipment:
A high-end micro-recording rig, an official (or highly accurate forged) press pass, a burner comm-link, and a portable data- scrubber.

## Special Ability:
**Off the Record:** You receive a -1 step bonus to [Interaction-Interview] ({{< relref "/skills/interaction/#interview" >}}) or [Street Smart-Street Knowledge] ({{< relref "/skills/street-smart/#street-knowledge" >}}) checks when trying to coax rumors, classified information, or unofficial statements out of a reluctant source.

## Tendencies (Pick 2):
- **The Exposé:** Prioritize documenting a crime, conspiracy, or event over stopping it, putting yourself in tactical danger to ensure the footage or story survives.
- **The Hard Question:** Aggressively interrogate or provoke a powerful individual in a social setting, destroying diplomatic progress in order to gauge their reaction.
