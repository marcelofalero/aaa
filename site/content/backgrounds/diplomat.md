## Starting Equipment:
A tailored high-society suit, an encrypted datapad with a localized legal codex, and a ceremonial or easily concealable sidearm.

## Special Ability: Diplomatic Immunity
In settled systems, you are often exempt from minor local laws and routine searches while on official business. This immunity extends strictly to your person and your personal cabin aboard a vessel, unless the ship itself is registered as a diplomatic transport. You receive a -1 step bonus to checks involving legal navigation.

## Tendencies (Pick 2):
*   **The Appeaser:** Compromise a hard-won position or objective to avoid "unpleasantness" or open conflict, even when it weakens the party's hand.
*   **The Formalist:** Insist on strict legal protocols or diplomatic immunity in situations where a quick, decisive (but illegal) action would have been more effective.
*   **The High-Society Snob:** Overtly insult or alienate a low-status contact (like a dockworker or pirate) because their lack of "class" makes negotiation impossible for you.
*   **The Documentarian:** Insist on drafting a formal contract or memorandum of understanding before acting, creating critical delays in time-sensitive deals.
