## Starting Equipment:
A tailored high-society suit, an encrypted datapad with a localized legal codex, and a ceremonial or easily concealable sidearm.

## Special Ability: Diplomatic Immunity
In settled systems, you are often exempt from minor local laws and routine searches while on official business. This immunity extends strictly to your person and your personal cabin aboard a vessel, unless the ship itself is registered as a diplomatic transport. You receive a -1 step bonus to checks involving legal navigation.

## Tendencies:
*   **The Mediator:** Attempt to resolve a hostile situation through negotiation even when combat is easier.
*   **The Architect:** Delay action to ensure every social variable or consequence is accounted for.
