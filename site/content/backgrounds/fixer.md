You know everyone, you owe everyone, and you're the only one who can get the job done.

## Favored Specialty Skills:
*   [Interaction-Bargain](/skills/interaction/#bargain)
*   [Administration-Bureaucracy](/skills/administration/#bureaucracy)
*   [Street Smart-Street Knowledge](/skills/street-smart/#street-knowledge)
*   [Deception-Bribe](/skills/deception/#bribe)

## Automatic Perk:
*   None.

## Automatic Flaw:
*   [Powerful Enemy](/perks_flaws/#powerful-enemy) (A dangerous character or slighted client).

## Starting Equipment:
An untraceable comm-link, a pocket full of varied currency or physical trade chits, and a concealed weapon.

## Special Ability: "I Know a Guy."
Once per session, you can locate a specific item, character, or piece of information with a -2 step bonus, finding it in half the normal time.

## Tendencies:
*   **The Negotiator:** Solve a conflict by brokering a deal that moves the plot forward.
*   **The Asset Manager:** Risk an objective to preserve a useful contact or piece of equipment.
