You use your art not for the galleries, but for the streets. You are a propagandist, a hacker of public spaces, and an agitator who knows that a well-placed image can do more damage than a bomb.

## Favored Broad Skill:
* [Creativity]({{< relref "/skills/creativity/" >}})

## Favored Specialty Skills:
* [Creativity-Specific Skill] ({{< relref "/skills/creativity/#creativity-specific-skill" >}}) (e.g., holo art, public graffiti, or propaganda design)
* [Deception-Bluff] ({{< relref "/skills/deception/#bluff" >}})
* [Covert Ops-Stealth] ({{< relref "/skills/covert-ops/#stealth" >}})
* [Leadership-Inspire] ({{< relref "/skills/leadership/#inspire" >}})

## Favored Perks:
* [Powerful Ally] ({{< relref "/perks_flaws/#powerful-ally" >}}) (An underground resistance movement, anarchist collective, or decentralized hacker group).

## Automatic Flaw:
* [Infamy] ({{< relref "/perks_flaws/#infamy" >}}) (Known to local authorities or megacorporations as a dangerous vandal and agitator).

## Starting Equipment:
A portable signal-hijacker or high-yield spray tech, an encrypted data-drive full of propaganda, a breather mask to obscure your face, and rugged street clothes.

## Special Ability:
**Subliminal Messaging:** You have a -1 step bonus to [Leadership-Inspire] ({{< relref "/skills/leadership/#inspire" >}}) or [Deception-Bluff] ({{< relref "/skills/deception/#bluff" >}}) checks when you use your art to incite a crowd, cause a public distraction, or rapidly sway public opinion.

## Tendencies (Pick 2):
* **The Calling Card:** Leave a recognizable piece of your art or propaganda at the scene of a mission, intentionally drawing attention to yourself and risking immediate discovery.
* **The Agitator:** Openly provoke authority figures or corporate security, using the resulting chaos to make a political point even if it jeopardizes the current operation.
