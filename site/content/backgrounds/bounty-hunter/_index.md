The galaxy is vast, but for you, it is just a series of footprints and digital trails. You live for the contract and the satisfaction of a closed case.

## Favored Broad Skill:
* None.

## Favored Specialty Skills:
* [Investigate-Track] ({{< relref "/skills/investigate/#track" >}})
* [Law-Law Enforcement Procedures] ({{< relref "/skills/law/#law-enforcement-procedures" >}})
* [Awareness-Perception] ({{< relref "/skills/awareness/#perception" >}})
* [Street Smart-Street Knowledge] ({{< relref "/skills/street-smart/#street-knowledge" >}})

## Favored Perks:
* [Observant] ({{< relref "/perks_flaws/#observant" >}})

## Automatic Flaw:
* [Powerful Enemy] ({{< relref "/perks_flaws/#powerful-enemy" >}}) (Someone you caught, or someone you’re currently hunting who has the upper hand).

## Starting Equipment:
An armored longcoat, two sets of heavy-duty binders, a tracking fob/scanner, and a heavy pistol or stun baton.

## Special Ability:
**Skip Tracer:** You have a -1 step bonus to [Investigate-Search] ({{< relref "/skills/investigate/#search" >}}) checks when looking for information regarding a specific individual's current location or recent history.

## Tendencies (Pick 2):
* **The Single-Minded:** Pursue a target or a lead into a clearly dangerous or disadvantageous situation instead of choosing a safer, slower path.
* **The Professional:** Honor the specific letter of a contract even when offered a more profitable "side deal" or an easier moral out.
