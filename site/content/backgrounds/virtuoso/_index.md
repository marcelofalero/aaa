You are a master of aesthetics and performance. Your work is highly sought after by the galactic elite, but true art requires an uncompromising vision that often clashes with practical reality.

## Favored Broad Skill:
* [Creativity]({{< relref "/skills/creativity/" >}})

## Favored Specialty Skills:
- [Creativity-Artistic Expression] ({{< relref "/skills/creativity/#artistic-expression" >}})
- [Culture-Etiquette] ({{< relref "/skills/culture/#etiquette-specific" >}})
- [Entertainment-Act] ({{< relref "/skills/entertainment/#act" >}})
- [Interaction-Charm] ({{< relref "/skills/interaction/#charm" >}})

## Favored Perks:
* [Celebrity] ({{< relref "/perks_flaws/#celebrity" >}})

## Automatic Flaw:
* [Temper] ({{< relref "/perks_flaws/#temper" >}}) (A volatile artistic ego that flares up unpredictably when your work, vision, or status is criticized or interrupted).

## Starting Equipment:
High-end, custom-made creation tools (a bespoke holo-stylus or physical mediums), luxury attire, and a portable digital portfolio.

## Special Ability:
**Patron's Favor & Catharsis:** You can leverage your artistic prestige to gain access to exclusive events or secure funding. You receive a -1 step bonus to [Interaction- Charm] ({{< relref "/skills/interaction/#charm" >}}) or [Culture-Etiquette] ({{< relref "/skills/culture/#etiquette-specific" >}}) checks when dealing with high-society patrons or art enthusiasts (this bonus improves to -2 steps if you possess the [Filthy Rich] ({{< relref "/perks_flaws/#filthy-rich" >}}) perk). Additionally, engaging in your art provides psychological relief: if using the Persistent Stress optional rules, a successful [Creativity-Artistic Expression] ({{< relref "/skills/creativity/#artistic-expression" >}}) check grants a +1 (Ordinary), +2 (Good), or +3 (Amazing) step bonus to Personality feats made to resist stress penalties.

## Tendencies (Pick 2):
- **The Aesthete:** Choose a course of action based purely on its aesthetic appeal or dramatic flair rather than its tactical soundness or efficiency.
- **The Diva:** Refuse to cooperate or compromise with the party until your specific, often petty, demands regarding comfort or artistic integrity are met.
