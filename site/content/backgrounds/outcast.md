You come from a place where technology is magic and survival is the only law.

## Favored Specialty Skills:
*   [Survival-Survival Training](/skills/survival/#survival-training)
*   [Athletics-Climb](/skills/athletics/#climb)
*   [Athletics-Throw](/skills/athletics/#throw)
*   [Awareness-Perception](/skills/awareness/#perception)
*   [Covert Ops-Stealth](/skills/covert-ops/#stealth)

## Automatic Perk:
*   None.

## Automatic Flaw:
*   [Primitive](/perks_flaws/#primitive)

## Starting Equipment:
A sturdy archaic weapon (like a spear, bow, or heavy blade), a survival knife, tribal or clan tokens, and rugged survival clothing.

## Special Ability:
None. (Your survival is bought with a wide array of raw, physical skills).

## Tendencies:
*   **The Traditionalist:** Solve a modern problem using an ancient, low-tech, or ritualistic method.
*   **The Loner:** Take a disadvantage to handle a task entirely on your own, refusing the help of "soft" technology or teammates.
