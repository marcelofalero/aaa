You are a relentless investigative journalist or freelance whistleblower. You use words to topple corporate empires and expose government corruption, making you a very dangerous person to know.

## Free Broad Skill:
*   [Creativity](/skills/creativity/)

## Favored Specialty Skills:
*   [Creativity-Writing](/skills/creativity/#writing)
*   [Investigate-Search](/skills/investigate/#search)
*   [Interaction-Interview](/skills/interaction/#interview)
*   [Street Smart-Street Knowledge](/skills/street-smart/#street-knowledge)

## Automatic Perk:
*   None.

## Automatic Flaw:
*   [Obsessed](/perks_flaws/#obsessed) (With uncovering the absolute truth of a situation, regardless of the danger it poses to yourself or your allies).

## Starting Equipment:
A high-end micro-recording rig, an official (or highly accurate forged) press pass, a burner comm-link, and a portable data-scrubber.

## Special Ability: Off the Record
You receive a -1 step bonus to [Interaction-Interview](/skills/interaction/#interview) or [Street Smart-Street Knowledge](/skills/street-smart/#street-knowledge) checks when trying to coax rumors, classified information, or unofficial statements out of a reluctant source.

## Tendencies:
*   **The Exposé:** Prioritize documenting a crime, conspiracy, or event over stopping it, putting yourself in tactical danger to ensure the footage or story survives.
*   **The Hard Question:** Aggressively interrogate or provoke a powerful individual in a social setting, destroying diplomatic progress in order to gauge their reaction.
