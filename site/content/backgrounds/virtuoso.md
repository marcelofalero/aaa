You are a master of aesthetics and performance. Your work is highly sought after by the galactic elite, but true art requires an uncompromising vision that often clashes with practical reality.

## Free Broad Skill:
*   [Creativity](/skills/creativity/)

## Favored Specialty Skills:
*   [Creativity-Artistic Expression](/skills/creativity/#artistic-expression)
*   [Culture-Etiquette](/skills/culture/#etiquette-specific)
*   [Entertainment-Act](/skills/entertainment/#act)
*   [Interaction-Charm](/skills/interaction/#charm)

## Automatic Perk:
*   [Celebrity](/perks_flaws/#celebrity)

## Automatic Flaw:
*   [Temper](/perks_flaws/#temper) (A volatile artistic ego that flares up unpredictably when your work, vision, or status is criticized or interrupted).

## Starting Equipment:
High-end, custom-made creation tools (a bespoke holo-stylus or physical mediums), luxury attire, and a portable digital portfolio.

## Special Ability: Patron's Favor & Catharsis
You can leverage your artistic prestige to gain access to exclusive events or secure funding. You receive a -1 step bonus to [Interaction-Charm](/skills/interaction/#charm) or [Culture-Etiquette](/skills/culture/#etiquette-specific) checks when dealing with high-society patrons or art enthusiasts (this bonus improves to -2 steps if you possess the [Filthy Rich](/perks_flaws/#filthy-rich) perk). Additionally, engaging in your art provides psychological relief: if using the Persistent Stress optional rules, a successful [Creativity-Artistic Expression](/skills/creativity/#artistic-expression) check grants a +1 (Ordinary), +2 (Good), or +3 (Amazing) step bonus to Personality feats made to resist stress penalties.

## Tendencies:
*   **The Aesthete:** Choose a course of action based purely on its aesthetic appeal or dramatic flair rather than its tactical soundness or efficiency.
*   **The Diva:** Refuse to cooperate or compromise with the party until your specific, often petty, demands regarding comfort or artistic integrity are met.
