You didn't train at a prestigious academy or fight under a planetary flag. You learned to command in the grueling, profit-driven world of private military companies or local militias, where logistics are scarce and victory goes to the pragmatic.

## Favored Broad Skill:
* None.

## Favored Specialty Skills:
* [Leadership-Command]({{< relref "/skills/leadership/#command" >}})
* [Tactics-Infantry Tactics]({{< relref "/skills/tactics/#infantry-tactics" >}})
* [Business-Small Business]({{< relref "/skills/business/#small-business" >}})
* [Modern Ranged Weapons-Assault Rifle]({{< relref "/skills/modern-ranged-weapons/#rifle" >}})

## Favored Perks:
* [Reputation]({{< relref "/perks_flaws/#reputation" >}})

## Automatic Flaw:
* [Code of Honor]({{< relref "/perks_flaws/#code-of-honor" >}}) (The Mercenary's Code: You never break a signed contract, you never betray a client who has paid in full, and you expect subordinates to follow your orders without question).

## Starting Equipment:
A personalized, non-standard suit of tactical armor, a heavily modified assault rifle, a digital contract ledger, and a squad-level encrypted comms unit.

## Special Ability:
**Asymmetrical Warfare:** You receive a -1 step bonus to [Tactics-Infantry Tactics]({{< relref "/skills/tactics/#infantry-tactics" >}}) or [Survival-Survival Training]({{< relref "/skills/survival/#survival-training" >}}) checks when executing an ambush, fighting a guerrilla campaign, or operating entirely cut off from official supply lines.

## Tendencies (Pick 2):
* **The Mercenary:** Demand renegotiation, hazard pay, or a clear tactical advantage before committing to a dangerous firefight that wasn't in the original plan.
* **The Pragmatist:** Disregard traditional rules of engagement or "honorable" doctrine in favor of traps, sabotage, or overwhelming disproportionate force.
