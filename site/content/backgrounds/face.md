You are a master of the "hustle." Whether you're scrounging restricted supplies or talking your way past a security checkpoint, you use your charm to requisition the impossible.

## Favored Specialty Skills:
*   [Entertainment-Act](/skills/entertainment/#act)
*   [Deception-Bluff](/skills/deception/#bluff)
*   [Interaction-Bargain](/skills/interaction/#bargain)
*   [Interaction-Charm](/skills/interaction/#charm)

## Automatic Perk:
*   [Great Looks](/perks_flaws/#great-looks)

## Automatic Flaw:
*   [Obsessed](/perks_flaws/#obsessed) (With maintaining your public image, luxury standards, or personal vanity).

## Starting Equipment:
An extravagant wardrobe suitable for high-end venues, a cosmetic disguise kit, and a flashy but low-caliber sidearm.

## Special Ability: The Scrounger
You have a -1 step bonus to [Interaction-Bargain](/skills/interaction/#bargain) or [Street Smart-Street Knowledge](/skills/street-smart/#street-knowledge) checks when trying to acquire specific equipment or restricted items through non-monetary means (cons, favors, or "borrowing").

## Tendencies:
*   **The Spotlight Seeker:** Intentionally draw attention to yourself in a social setting to create a distraction, even when being inconspicuous is safer.
*   **The Hustler:** Attempt to "scam" or talk your way out of a problem or into a restricted area using your personal appeal, even when a more direct (but less elegant) method exists.
