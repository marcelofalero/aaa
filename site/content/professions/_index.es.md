# Professions in AAA

In **AAA (Alterated Alternity Alternative)**, your **Profession** represents your character's primary training and current role in the world. While a Background tells you where you came from, your Profession tells you what you do now.

Choosing a Profession is a key part of character creation. It provides:
1.  **Minimum Ability Requirements:** To qualify for a profession, you must meet certain physical or mental standards.
2.  **Favored Skills:** Each Profession identifies a set of skills that receive a **Purchase Cost Discount** (Market Value - 1).
3.  **Unique Benefits:** Every Profession grants special mechanical advantages.

---

## Available Professions

### [Combat Spec](/professions/combat-spec)
Warriors and tactical experts who rely on physical power and combat training.

### [Free Agent](/professions/free-agent)
Field operatives, troubleshooters, and specialists who rely on agility, intuition, and independence.

### [Tech Op](/professions/tech-op)
Technical operatives skilled in the use, creation, or maintenance of high-tech equipment.

### [Mindwalker](/professions/mindwalker)
(Optional) Specialists in psionic powers and mental disciplines.

---

## Profession Requirements

| Profession | Minimum Requirements |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Combat Spec** | STR 9, CON 9 |
| **Free Agent** | DEX 9, WIL 9 |
| **Tech Op** | DEX 9, INT 9 |
| **Mindwalker** | WIL 9, PER 9 |

---

## Starting Funds

Every hero starts with a bankroll to purchase their initial gear. Refer to the table below based on your chosen profession. Multiply the result by 100 to determine your total starting funds in Credits ($).

| Profession | Starting Funds Multiplier |
| :--- | :---: |
| Combat Spec | 5d6 |
| Diplomat | 5d12 |
| Free Agent | 5d8 |
| Tech Op | 5d8 |
| Mindwalker | 5d4 |

### Rich and Poor
- **Filthy Rich (Perk):** Multiply your starting funds by x10, x20, x50, or x100 depending on your Perk check.
- **Dirt Poor (Flaw):** Reduce your starting funds by 90%, 75%, 50%, or 25% depending on your Flaw check.
