# Campaign Log: The Crimson Dawn

### Entry 01: The Aurora Disaster
**Location:** Commercial Cruiser Aurora, Deep Space.
**Status:** Structural Collapse / Emergency Evacuation.

The commercial cruiser Aurora, a modular passenger and cargo transport vessel, had just transitioned out of drive space. Without warning, the impact of what appeared to be a smart mine or an automated weapon remnant from the war shattered the bow section. The subsequent chain reaction caused the ship's macrostructure to twist and fracture, exposing the cars one by one to the vacuum.

**Chronicle of the Escape**
*   **The Horror of the Void:** The compartment directly ahead of ours suffered a catastrophic rupture. Through the reinforced glass of the sealing door, we witnessed absolute horror: a segment of the adjacent car's fuselage detached completely, exposing the interior to the vacuum. We watched passengers die victims of explosive decompression, ebullism, and fulminant hypoxia as their bodily fluids vaporized from the pressure drop. There was no sound, only the visual chaos of disintegration.
*   **Gravity Failure:** Artificial gravity failed intermittently in the car. The thunder of the twisting fuselage was a constant reminder that time was running out, while red emergency lights bathed the corridor.
*   **The Bulwark Bypass:** With our section's pods rendered useless, we headed aft. The armored pressure bulkhead was jammed due to hull torsion. Tarek Ionis performed an electronic bypass on the magnetic solenoids, successfully opening a breach before the car sealed permanently.
*   **Cargo Section:** Gravity in the cargo section was still functional, likely due to the characteristics of this type of vessel, which, like a train, was composed of independent interconnected modules. Fortunately, the structural failure caused the cargo section to decouple, halting the structural collapse. Some of us began searching for our gear containers in the cargo log, while others opened containers to retrieve whatever meager belongings might be of use.
*   **Engineering:** It was in this situation that we intercepted the engineering team—Sam Logan, Clara Ananta, and a severely wounded Mathew Nadir—fleeing the reactors, which had suffered an overload and were out of control. The engineering personnel loaded additional equipment into the escape pod, including a weight neutralizer cart full of various supplies.
*   **The Extraction:** We boarded the cargo pod in a rush. Seconds after ejecting, the Aurora's engineering section suffered a total structural collapse. With no atmosphere for an explosion, we watched the ship fold in on itself as the reactor failure incinerated the interior in a flash of white plasma, leaving behind a frame of molten metal.

### Entry 02: Adrift and Landing
**Location:** Salvage Pod Model 7-E / Unknown Planetary Surface.
**Duration:** 121 hours adrift.

What was supposed to be an escape turned into a slow moral and physical degradation within a ten-square-meter space.

**Chronology of the Drift**
*   **Day 1: Cold Calculation.** Mathew Nadir was bleeding out from explosion wounds. The Mechalus medic, Tarek Ionis, calculated that the expenditure of medical supplies was not efficient given the engineer's low probability of survival. He made minimal effort. Mathew died that night, leaving an atmosphere of distrust and horror.
*   **Day 2: The Duffle Bag.** The stench of desperation and decomposition became intolerable. To fit Mathew's body into a duffle bag, his arms had to be dislocated. We sealed the bag with duct tape to try and contain the fluids and smell in the recycled air.
*   **Day 3: Tony's Outburst.** Post-traumatic stress and confinement broke Tony Skigrocu. His violent outburst endangered the group's integrity, forcing us to physically restrain him and keep him under heavy sedatives that kept him at bay but just short of unconsciousness.
*   **Day 4: Contrasts.** Hunger and $CO_2$-saturated air consumed us. Basilio Zuzunaga ("Zuzu") remained in an absolute and disturbing calm, while Ssi-T’krik couldn't stop moving in the few free centimeters and Makya clung to her bow, at the edge of her nerves.
*   **Day 5: Breaking Point.** The effect of the sedatives on Tony wore off, and he regained his combative will. Pistol in hand, he pointed at us all in a fit of paranoid lucidity. A massacre was imminent when the planetary proximity alarm interrupted the duel.

**The Landing**
We have touched down on a dry ice wasteland. Sensors indicate we are on the dark side of a tidally locked planet—the technical way of stating that the same face always faces the sun.

Ruins of a colony rise before the hatch. The failed attempt to identify the planet left us with more questions than answers. On the distant horizon, a constant orange glow can be perceived.

**Status Report:**
*   Tony Skigrocu remains armed and tense.
*   Mathew's body remains sealed at the bottom of the pod.
*   The exterior is frigid, around -100°C with a pressure of 3.5 Earth atmospheres, which explains the gentle descent. At that pressure, walking feels like being submerged, and the wind consists of currents that are almost impossible to resist.
*   Unknown location. The emergency beacon, a cry for help in the void.

**Survival Inventory**

*   **Gear:** 12 sleeping bags, 12 thermal units, 12 suits (9 Soft, 3 Hard - one for T'sa).
*   **Medical:** 6 Trauma Pack I, 1 Med Kit, Pharmaceuticals (1,000 cr), 1 MedCare One.
*   **Logistics:** 2 Gliese 300b Domes with corridors, Cart with Weight Neutralizer (10,000 Kg/h), heavy tools and Universal Charger.
