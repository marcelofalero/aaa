A character with Psionic Awareness is entitled to an Intelligence feat check to determine whether he realizes that a psionic power is being used in his vicinity. (characters without this perk are not normally allowed checks to determine whether they can detect psionic activity.) The Gamemaster will determine when such a check should be made, and will roll the check secretly, only giving the player of the character any information when the check is successful. This perk can only be selected if the Gamemaster allows the use of psionics (see Chapter 14).
