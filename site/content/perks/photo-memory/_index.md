This perk gives a character an extraordinary memory. When a player wants to determine what his character remembers, or if he wants his character to remember a specific detail (such as a license plate number or details of a document he glanced at), then a perk check is made. An Ordinary is usually enough to provide some of the details the character wants. The Gamemaster sets the situation die according to the type of detail and memory the character is trying to recall. For example, an attempt to remember something the character knows extremely well might receive a -3 bonus or might not even require a check at all, while recalling the image of a face glimpsed briefly in the dark might receive a +3 penalty.
