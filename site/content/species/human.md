# Human

In the AAA game, members of the human species come in a variety of shades, sizes, and body types, but they all look like the people around us.

## History

The history of humanity is well documented and doesn't need to be repeated here. For the purposes of this science fiction roleplaying game, two events will come to pass in humanity's future: The species will develop the means to expand beyond its homeworld and colonize the solar system. And eventually it will become capable of traveling beyond the solar system and establishing communities of humans in other star systems.

## Special Abilities

- **Specialized Training:** Humans can select one specialty skill Favored by his Profession or Background; He receives a -1 step bonus when using this skill.
- **Free Perk:** Humans receive a free perk from a designated list (TBD) at character creation.
- **Free Broad Skills:** Stamina (CON), Knowledge (INT), Athletics (STR), Vehicle Operation (DEX), Awareness (WIL), Interaction (PER).

## Roleplaying

Human characters are often defined by their ambition and adaptability. They are the benchmark by which other species often measure themselves, and their drive to explore and settle the stars has pulled many other species into a sprawling galactic community.
