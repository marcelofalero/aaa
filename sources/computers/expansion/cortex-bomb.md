The cortex bomb is one of the most brutal means available to ensure compliance. It's a small radio-activated or timed explosive charge, implanted in the subject’s skull. If detonated, it kills the victim in an unforgiving manner. Radio detonators must be within 200 meters for effective operation; cluttering terrain or interior walls may affect this proximity.
