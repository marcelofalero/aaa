Anesthetics (PL 4) are used to prepare a person for surgery. One injection of anesthetic renders a patient unconscious.<br />Using anesthetic against an unwilling target in combat requires a successful Unarmed Attack, and the victim is entitled to a Constitution feat check to determine the exact effect: Critical Failure, opponent is knocked out (all stun points lost); Failure, opponent suffers d6+2s; Ordinary, d6s; Good, d6-1s; Amazing, d4-1s.
