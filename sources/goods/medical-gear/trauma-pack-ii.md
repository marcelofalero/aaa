Basically an intelligent, autonomous version of the trauma pack I, this device is designed to be used with body tank armor and some cybernetic body enhancements.<br />When installed, the pack monitors the vital signs of its user and activates at the first sign of damage. When activated, it provides one of the following benefits:<br />Knockout recovery: When the wearer suffers enough damage to use up the last of his stun points, the pack dispenses a drug that immediately restores enough stun points to leave the wearer with damage equal to only half, or one less than half, of his total stun points.<br />Wound recovery: The moment the wearer suffers enough wound damage to use up more than half of his wound points, the pack dispenses a drug that immediately restores d4+2 wound points to the character.<br />Mortal damage: The moment the wearer suffers any mortal damage, the pack dispenses a drug that immediately stabilizes the damage, removing the need for the wearer to make a Stamina-endurance check. All actions attempted by the wearer after receiving the stabilizing drug are made with a +3 penalty until his mortal damage is healed.<br />Each pack contains drugs sufficient for two applications of each function; no more than one of these packs can be rigged on an individual at a time.
