This device contains a variety of medical sensors and samplers that can accurately diagnose almost any medical condition known by means of a blood sample and an ultrasonic scan.<br />The gauntlet provides a -1 bonus to Medical Science-treatment skill checks when its user is attempting to diagnose or treat known diseases or wounds.
