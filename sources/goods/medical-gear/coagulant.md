Coagulant (PL 6) causes clotting at the point of injury without thickening blood throughout the body.<br />A dose of coagulant gives a dying character a -3 bonus to his next Stamina-endurance check, making it much more likely that his condition will not worsen for at least the next 24 hours. Coagulant can be administered by any character who makes a successful Knowledge-first aid skill check.
