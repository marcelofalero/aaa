Antivenom (PL 5) is an antidote for a natural poison, such as a snake bite or a scorpion sting.<br />If the victim receives a dose of antivenom within the poison's onset time, he gains a -4 bonus to his Constitution feat check. This bonus is halved if the antivenom is administered after the poison begins to affect the victim. Antivenom may be ineffective on some alien toxins or unusual venoms.
