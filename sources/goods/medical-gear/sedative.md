Sedatives (PL 5) are used to calm people who might be dangerous to themselves or those around them. Sedated individuals suffer a +3 penalty to all action checks and actions. That penalty is reduced by 1 step per hour until the effect of the drug wears off.<br />Using a sedative against an unwilling target in combat requires a successful Unarmed Attack roll, and the victim is entitled to a Constitution feat check to fight off the effects: Ordinary, penalty reduced to +2; Good, penalty reduced to +1; Amazing, drug has no effect.
