At a mass of 100 kg, the workshop can be stowed in many vehicles. It includes heavy work surfaces, a multipurpose mill and press, a powered table saw and miter, and a variety of other tools. Its principal purpose is to allow a character in a remote area to perform major vehicular repairs.<br />Any complex skill check involving Technical Science or repair is considered to be of Ordinary complexity if the workshop is being used. It takes 1 hour to set up or take down the workshop.
