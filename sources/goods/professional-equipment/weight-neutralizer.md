This is a handheld gravity inducer about the size of a hardcover book. It measures the weight of an object it is attached to—which must be made of a substance that is attracted by a magnet—and then generates a perfect counter-field. Under the neutralizer's influence, objects become gravity-neutral.<br />The neutralizer's endurance is 10,000 kg-hours. When its power is depleted, it must be fully recharged (requiring 6 hours) before it can be used again.
