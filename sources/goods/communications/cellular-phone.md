This is a low-power transmitter and receiver that sends radio signals to a nearby relay tower. At PL 5, the phone is a large handset with a 100 km range. At PL 6, it is the size of a credit card (500 km range). At PL 7, it is a pin or patch (1,000 km range).<br />Cell phone batteries are rechargeable and last for 1 day of use at PL 5 plus 2 days for each PL beyond that.
