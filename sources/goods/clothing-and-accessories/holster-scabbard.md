A holster is a case of leather or similar material shaped to hold a pistol. A scabbard is a sheath of leather or similar material used to hold a sword, dagger, or knife.<br />A concealed holster or scabbard is worn in a place not usually searched and not subject to easy spotting—the forearm, inside of the thigh or calf, the small of the back, or the ankle. The concealment provides a +2 penalty to all but the most intrusive of searches.
