In their simplest form, glasses consist of a pair of lenses mounted in a frame. They are worn to improve vision, to make a fashion statement, or to do both. Designer models are 2 to 3 times more expensive than the listed price.<br />At PL 5, contact lenses become available; cost is $50 for a six-month supply.
