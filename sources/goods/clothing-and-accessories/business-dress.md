The clothes an executive wears. For men, this includes the equivalent of a business suit, collared shirt, tie, and dress socks. For women, this includes the equivalent of a woman's business suit or dress with matching accessories.<br />For the true fashion-conscious elite, business dress of superior make and style can be 2 to 10 times more expensive than the listed cost. This sort of indulgence marks the character as a potential member of certain power circles.
