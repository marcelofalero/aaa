name: Goggles, protective

These come in various forms for different purposes, the most common being a set of eyepieces that protect the wearer from extremely bright light. The standard model reduces any penalty for operating in bright light by 1 step.<br />At PL 7 a special variety of protective goggles comes into existence for sesheyans. They completely negate any penalty for bright light, and adjust instantly to changes in illumination.
