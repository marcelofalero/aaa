This specialty skill covers navigation through a star system, usually movements between planets, moons, and stations.
### In-System Navigation Success
- **Ordinary:** Arrive at destination +d4 hours from predicted time.
- **Good:** Arrive at destination on schedule.
- **Amazing:** Arrive at destination -d4 hours early.
