name: Medical Science

This broad skill provides a character with the training needed to perform medical procedures, including diagnosing ailments and researching medical information. A character with just the broad skill cannot treat damage;
**Treatment** or **Surgery** specialties (or the **First Aid** specialty from the Knowledge broad skill) are required for those tasks.
