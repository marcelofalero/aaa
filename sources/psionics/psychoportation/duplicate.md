This ability allows a character to summon a duplicate of himself or herself
from another location in the multiverse. The duplicate is identical in every
way and possesses the same skills and attributes as the psion. The duration
that the duplicate persists depends on the success:
- **Ordinary:** 1 round.
- **Good:** 2 rounds.
- **Amazing:** 5 rounds.
**Rank Benefits:**
- **At rank 4 [Duration]:** the duration of the duplicate's existence increases by 2 rounds.
- **At rank 8 [Independent Action]:** the duplicate can act independently of the psion, although they still share the same psionic energy pool.
- **At rank 12 [Multiple Duplicates]:** the psion can summon up to three duplicates at once.
