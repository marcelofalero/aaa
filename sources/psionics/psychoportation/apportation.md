This ability allows a character to summon an inanimate object from a distant
location or to banish an object to another location. The psion must have seen
the object he or she is attempting to apport. The weight of the object that
can be apported depends on the skill rank and the success:
- **Ordinary:** 1 kilogram per rank.
- **Good:** 2 kilograms per rank.
- **Amazing:** 5 kilograms per rank. The object appears or disappears instantaneously.
**Rank Benefits:**
- **At rank 3 [Living Matter]:** the psion can apport living matter.
- **At rank 4 [Increased weight]:** the weight limit increases: Ordinary, 2kg/rank; Good, 5kg/rank; Amazing, 10kg/rank.
- **At rank 6 [Precise Apportation]:** the psion can apport an object exactly to a specific location with no drift.
- **At rank 8 [Increased weight]:** the weight limit increases: Ordinary, 5kg/rank; Good, 10kg/rank; Amazing, 20kg/rank.
- **At rank 9 [Large Apports]:** the psion can apport objects that are up to 1 cubic meter in volume.
- **At rank 12 [Increased weight]:** the weight limit increases: Ordinary, 10kg/rank; Good, 20kg/rank; Amazing, 40kg/rank.
- **At rank 12 [Distant Apports]:** the psion can apport objects from locations he or she has never seen, as long as he or she has a clear description.
