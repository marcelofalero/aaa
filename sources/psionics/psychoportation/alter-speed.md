By concentrating, the character can accelerate or decelerate himself or
herself or another living target within 8 meters. The psion must decide
whether he or she is accelerating or decelerating before making the skill
check. The effect on the target's action check score depends on the success:
- **Ordinary:** +1 or -1 to the action check score.
- **Good:** +2 or -2 to the action check score.
- **Amazing:** +3 or -3 to the action check score. The ability lasts for one round, but the duration can be extended by spending 1 psionic energy point for each additional round.
**Rank Benefits:**
- **At rank 6 [Action Points]:** the target gains or loses one action per round (to a maximum of 4 actions) while the effect lasts.
- **At rank 12 [Instant Stop/Start]:** the target gains or loses another action (to a maximum of 4, minimum of 0).
