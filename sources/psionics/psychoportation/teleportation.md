Teleportation allows a psion to instantaneously move from his or her current location to another. The psion can also teleport objects or other characters. The range and weight limit depend on the skill rank and the success:
- **Range:** 10/100/1000 meters.
- **Weight:** 10 kilograms per rank (Ordinary success), 20 kilograms per rank (Good success), or 40 kilograms per rank (Amazing success).
**Rank Benefits:**
- **At rank 4 [Increased Distance]:** the range increases: 1km/10km/100km.
- **At rank 8 [Increased Distance]:** the range increases: 100km/1000km/World.
- **At rank 12 [Increased Distance]:** the range increases: World/Galaxy/Universe.
