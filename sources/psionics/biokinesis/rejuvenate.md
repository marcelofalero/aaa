To use this skill, a psion concentrates on his or her own body to help it offset the effects of fatigue or stun damage (it provides no help in recovering lost psionic energy). The results of the check are immediate, although the body’s reaction to the rejuvenation takes about one hour. During that hour, the psion cannot use the rejuvenate skill again. The success of the skill check determines how many "rejuvenation points" the character receives: Ordinary success, 2 points; Good success, 4 points; Amazing success, 6 points. It costs 2 rejuvenation points for every point of fatigue damage to be recovered, and 1 rejuvenation point for every point of stun damage.
**Rank Benefits:**
- **At rank 4 [Improved Effect]:** the rejuvenation points received increase: Ordinary, 4 points; Good, 6 points; Amazing, 8 points.
- **At rank 8 [Laying On Hands]:** the psion can use the rejuvenate skill on other characters through physical contact.
- **At rank 12 [Two Recipients]:** the psion can affect two individuals simultaneously.
