By mentally reordering the molecules of his or her own body, a psion can become immaterial. He or she can then pass through solid objects (except for other living beings). The character’s clothes and up to 1 kilogram per rank of gear also become intangible. The ability lasts for one phase, although the duration can be extended by spending 1 psionic energy point for each additional phase. Vertical movement is not possible while intangible; the psion merely sinks through any solid objects at a rate of 3 meters per phase. Horizontal movement through solid objects is possible at a rate equal to an "easy swim."
**Rank Benefits:**
- **At rank 4 [Movement Bonus]:** the psion can move through solid objects at his or her walk rate.
- **At rank 6 [Dodge]:** a character can use the intangibility skill to dodge an attack. This is treated as a reaction parry; the psion must have a phase available and must decide to use the skill when the attack is announced.
- **At rank 8 [Movement Bonus]:** the psion can move through solid objects at his or her run rate.
- **At rank 12 [Selective Intangibility]:** the psion can render only a portion of his or her body intangible. This allows the character to reach through a solid object to manipulate something on the other side. Such an attempt carries a +2 step penalty to the intangibility skill check, and a +4 step penalty to any skill used to manipulate the object on the other side.
