By using this skill, a psion can absorb the wounds or the disease of another character. To use the skill, the psion must touch the character to be helped. The success of the skill check determines the amount of damage transferred:
- **Ordinary:** the psion can absorb 1 wound point from the subject, or reduce the grade of an illness by one. The psion becomes infected by the illness, but at one grade lower than that already suffered by the subject.
- **Good:** the psion can absorb 2 wound points or reduce the grade of an illness by two.
- **Amazing:** the psion can absorb 3 wound points or 1 mortal point. In the case of an illness, the psion can automatically transfer the entire illness (of any grade) to himself or herself. On a Critical Failure, the psion suffers 1 wound point as he or she fails to correctly channel the energy.
**Rank Benefits:**
- **At rank 6 [Displace Damage]:** the psion can transfer the damage from one character to another (both of whom must be in physical contact with the psion). The target of the transfer must be willing.
- **At rank 12 [Unwilling Transfer]:** the psion can transfer damage from one character to another living recipient who is in contact with the psion, even if the recipient is unwilling. This requires a successful contact skill check against the unwilling recipient.
