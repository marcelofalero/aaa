By using this skill, a psion can alter his or her physical features. This ability is most often used to create a disguise, although it can also be used to allow a character to reach or squeeze into areas that are normally inaccessible. The character must spend one round (4 phases) concentrating and making the skill check to activate the ability. Morph does not allow a character to change his or her mass, only to rearrange it. The duration of the transformation depends on the result of the skill check: Ordinary success, 1 round; Good success, 2 rounds; Amazing success, 3 rounds. The duration can be extended by spending 1 psionic energy point per extra round.
**Rank Benefits:** A character can choose to gain one of the following benefits at the listed rank:
- **Rank 1 or 3:** Elongate fingers (provides a +0.5 meter reach), Elongate arms (provides a +1 meter reach), or Elongate legs (provides +1 meter height).
- **Rank 5 or 7:** Disguise (anyone trying to recognize the psion suffers a +2 step penalty to Awareness—perception checks), Elongate both arms and legs simultaneously, or Skeleton alterations (allows the character to pass through an opening as small as 0.5 meters in diameter).
- **Rank 10 or 12:** Improved disguise (anyone trying to recognize the psion suffers a +4 step penalty to Awareness—perception checks, or +2 if the examiner is using psionics), or Lessen damage (the psion can rearrange his or her internal organs to avoid serious injury; reduce any damage delivered to the psion: Ordinary success, d4; Good success, d4+2; Amazing success, d6+2).
- **At rank 12 [Morph Control]:** a psion is able to have two forms of morphing active at the same time.
