This specialty skill allows a character to heal wounds or to help the character’s body fight off a disease. To use the skill, the character concentrates on the wounds to be healed. The results of the skill check are immediate, although the body’s reaction to the healing process takes about one hour. During that hour, the character cannot be healed again using the heal specialty skill. For each success achieved, the character can heal wound points or reduce the grade of a disease: Ordinary success, 1 wound point or reduce the grade of a disease by one; Good success, 2 wound points or reduce the grade of a disease by two; Amazing success, 3 wound points or reduce the grade of a disease by three.
**Rank Benefits:**
- **At rank 4 [Treat Poison]:** the psion can use heal to help a body counteract the effects of a poison. The target of the skill check receives a bonus to his or her Constitution feat check to resist the poison: Ordinary, -2 steps; Good, -3 steps; Amazing,
  -4 steps.
- **At rank 6 [Heal Mortal]:** the psion can heal mortal damage. On an Ordinary success, 2 wound points are healed; on a Good success, 3 wound points and 1 mortal point are healed; on an Amazing success, 4 wound points and 2 mortal points are healed.
- **At rank 8 [Laying On Hands]:** the psion can use the heal skill on other characters through physical contact.
- **At rank 12 [Master Healer]:** the amount of damage that the psion can heal increases: Ordinary, 3 wound points and 1 mortal point; Good, 4 wound points and 2 mortal points; Amazing, 6 wound points and 3 mortal points.
