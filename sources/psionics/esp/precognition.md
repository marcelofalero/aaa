This skill is the ability to receive impressions about possible future
events—what the psion sees will probably happen if no one takes action to
change it. In general, a character can see a number of hours into the future
equal to his or her skill rank. On a successful skill check, the character
gains information about the event as well as a free last resort point (LRP),
which can only be used during the scene.
- **Ordinary:** perceptions of vague images. The LRP can only be used to modify a check which directly affects the psion (e.g. reducing an opponent's attack roll).
- **Good:** brief flashes of coming events. The LRP may be used to modify the result of any action made against or by him or her during the scene.
- **Amazing:** mentally experiences a brief encounter as though seeing events transpire. The LRP can be applied to any skill check made by any character involved in the scene. Forcing a flash doubles the cost in energy points, applies a +3 step penalty, and the skill can't be used again consciously for 2d6 days.
**Rank Benefits:**
- **At rank 3 [Increased Time]:** extend precognitive ability to a number of days equal to skill rank.
- **At rank 6 [Increased Time]:** extend to weeks equal to rank.
- **At rank 9 [Increased Time]:** extend to months equal to rank.
- **At rank 12 [Increased Time]:** extend to years equal to rank.
