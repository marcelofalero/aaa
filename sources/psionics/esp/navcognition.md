A character who uses this skill can instinctively determine his or her present
location and mentally plot a course. This can be accomplished on a planetary
surface, in normal space, and even through drivespace. This mental ability
replaces the use of the Navigation skill. At rank 1, the character selects one
specialty: surface navigation, system astrogation, or drivespace astrogation.
| Situation/Condition | Modifier | | :--- | :---: | | Combat / Hostile
conditions | +4 / +2 | | Unexplored / Partially explored territory | +4 / +2 |
| Less than 5 minutes plotting | +1 per min. | | More than 5 minutes plotting
| -1 per min. |
**Rank Benefits:**
- **At rank 4 [Travel Time]:** becomes able to determine the clearest way to the destination. Cuts travel time (surface/system): Ordinary 75%; Good 50%; Amazing 25% of normal.
- **At rank 4 [Travel Distance]:** for drivespace, travel distance is multiplied: Ordinary x2; Good x5; Amazing x10.
- **At rank 5 [Extra Specialties]:** a second Navigation specialty can be selected.
- **At rank 6 [Clarity of Mind]:** allows a -4 step bonus during chases, races, or vehicle combat for reducing situational penalties.
- **At rank 9 [Extra Specialties]:** the remaining specialty becomes available.
- **At rank 12 [Tracking]:** attempt to track the most likely destination of a target subject, even through drivespace. (+2 seen, +4 witness/recorded).
