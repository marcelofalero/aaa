This ability allows a character to telekinetically choke a target. The character must have a clear line of sight to the target, and the target must be within 24 meters. A successful skill check indicates that the psion has successfully grasped the target's throat. The target suffers a +2 step penalty to all actions and suffers stun damage each round: Ordinary success, d4s; Good success, d4+2s; Amazing success, d4w. The ability lasts for one round, but the duration can be extended by spending 1 psionic energy point for each additional round.
**Rank Benefits:**
- **At rank 4 [Increased Damage]:** the damage increases to d4+2s/d6+2s/d4w.
- **At rank 8 [Increased Damage]:** the damage increases to d6+2s/d4w/d4+2w.
- **At rank 12 [Multiple Targets]:** the psion can affect a number of characters equal to his or her skill rank.
