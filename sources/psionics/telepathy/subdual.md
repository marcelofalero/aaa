Requirement: [[Mechalus Species]]({{< relref "/species/mechalus" >}})

This skill allows the user to engender feelings of calm, or even detachment,
in any intelligent species. The target must be within visual range. 

Most Aleerins, incidentally, would never dream of using this ability to take
advantage of another being. To do so requires that the Aleerin succeed at a
Resolve-mental resolve check.

### Check Results
- **Ordinary:** the target becomes less violent, suffering a +1 step penalty to combat skill checks, including action checks.
- **Good:** the target becomes less violent, suffering a +3 step penalty to combat skill checks, including action checks.
- **Amazing:** the subject becomes incapable of launching an attack against another living being, and will hesitate for a phase before attacking objects, structures, or other nonliving things.

### Rank Benefits
▶ **Rank 4, 8, 12 [Increased Effect]:** the user can affect two targets with the same skill check. At rank 8, he or she can affect four targets, and eight targets at rank 12.
