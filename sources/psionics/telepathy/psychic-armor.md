This skill creates a psychic "buffer zone" around the character, which helps
to lessen the effect of drain, mind blast, and tire attacks directed at him or
her. 

Psychic armor provides the character with a number of "psionic defense points".

A character cannot use this skill to gain more defense points until any previous defense points are lost. 

- One defense point reduces one point of damage suffered from [[telepathy-mind blast]]({{< relref "/psionics/telepathy#mind-blast" >}}).
- Two defense points reduce one point of fatigue loss caused by [[telepathy-tire]]({{< relref "/psionics/telepathy#tire" >}})
- Two defense points negates 1 step penalty from [[telepathy-thought worm]]({{< relref "/psionics/telepathy#thought-worm" >}}), if the penalty reaches zero the power is negated.
- Four defense points reduce by one degree the extent to which a character's emotional state has been altered by [[telepathy-empathic projection]]({{< relref "/psionics/telepathy#empathic-projection" >}}).

### Check Results
- **Ordinary:** the character gains 6 defense points.
- **Good:** the character gains 8 defense points.
- **Amazing:** the character gains 10 defense points.

### Rank Benefits
▶ **Rank 4 [Increased Effect]:** the character gains 8 defense points on an Ordinary success, 10 on a Good success, and 12 on an Amazing success. 

▶ **Rank 8 [Increased Effect]:** the character gains 12 defense points on an Ordinary success, 14 on a Good success, and 16 on an Amazing success. 

▶ **Rank 12 [Increased Effect]:** the character gains 16 defense points on an Ordinary success, 18 on a Good success, and 20 on an Amazing success.
