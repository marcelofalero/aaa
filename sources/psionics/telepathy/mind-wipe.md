With this skill, a psionic character can erase memories from a target's mind by rendering them unaccessible.
One minute's memories can be rendered unaccessible for every rank the character has in mind
wipe. 

The target must be within visual range, and no more than 10 meters away
(range 2/5/10). The memories must be no older than 1 day per rank of the
psionics-user. The character's skill check determines how difficult it is for
the target to recall events during the 'erased' time.

In any case, characters with erased memories will definitely
know that something is wrong, assuming they are aware of the passage of time
or of changes to themselves or their environment. They simply will not quite
remember how these things happened.

### Check Results
- **Ordinary:** the target receives a +2 step penalty on Intelligence feat checks to recall anything that happened during that time.
- **Good:** the target receives a +3 step penalty on Intelligence feat checks to recall anything that happened during that time.
- **Amazing:** the target receives a +4 step penalty on Intelligence feat checks to recall anything that happened during that time.

### Rank Benefits
▶ **Rank 3, 6, 9, 12 [Extended Reach]:** the character can erase the memory of an event that happened a number of weeks into the past equal to his or her skill rank. At rank 6, this becomes months; at rank 9, it becomes years; and at rank 12, it increases to decades.

▶ **Rank 6 [Edit]:** the character can replace the memories that were erased with false memories. The success of the skill check determines the modifier for attempts to spot the false memories, if the target has reason to doubt his memories.
Without a gap in the memories is unlikely the target will doubt his memories unless the change was unrealistic for him, or someone points it out to him. 

▶ **Rank 12 [Lock/Unlock]:** a character who reaches rank 12 with this skill can keep the memories locked until a particular event trigger or condition is met. It can also unlock memories that have been locked by another psionic. Or recover erased memories that were rendered unaccessible by this power.
