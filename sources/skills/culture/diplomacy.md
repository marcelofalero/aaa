This specialty skill allows a character to improve his ability to reach agreements and understandings with representatives of different nations, cultures, subcultures and species. In general, diplomacy will come into play when a character wants to reach an accord with a member of a different culture. 

The skill can only be used on a particular target if the character has at least rank 1 in the specific Culture--etiquette skill that pertains to the target's culture. Interaction and its specialty skills are usually used for interpersonal dealings between members of the same culture, but the Gamemaster may decide that Culture--diplomacy is also useful in such cases. (If so, it's not necessary for a character to have the etiquette skill that pertains to his or her culture in order to use diplomacy on other members of that culture.) Depending on the nature of the issue to be resolved, the diplomacy skill check may be a single dice roll, or it may be a complex skill check requiring an extended period of time to complete.
