This specialty skill represents the character's ability to lead others by virtue of sheer authoritativeness and persuasive ability. This is the skill used by military officers, elected officials, and law enforcers to issue orders and see that they're carried out.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Command can't be used on targets who are Hostile or Combative; before such targets can be led, their attitude must be shifted (perhaps by the use of the Inspire skill).

### Rank Benefits
⊗ **Rank 3, 6, 9, 12 [Increased Effect]** As a character achieves higher ranks in this specialty, the bonus to his or her Leadership skill checks improves to **-1 at rank 3**, **-2 at rank 6**, **-3 at rank 9**, and **-4 at rank 12**. (Note: This bonus is not cumulative.)
