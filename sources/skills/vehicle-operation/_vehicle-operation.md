name: Vehicle Operation

This broad skill provides a character with a basic understanding of and proficiency with modern vehicles. Using this skill, a character can attempt to operate most normal land and water vehicles. Some military or specialized land and water vehicles may have controls that are too complex for the broad skill to cover, requiring at least some level of specialization before a character is able to operate them successfully. The ordinary operation of any normal land or water vehicle doesn't require a skill check. During chases, races, or vehicle combat, or when navigating through challenging obstacles, skill checks may be called for.
