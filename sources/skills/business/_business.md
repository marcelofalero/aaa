This broad skill provides a character with basic knowledge of the theories of production, distribution, supply and demand, advertising, inventory control, and business planning.

### Uses
Skill checks are called for when a character wants to:
- Play the stock market.
- Apply business knowledge to conclude a trade.
- Attempt an illegal business transaction.
- Determine the value of goods in their own society.
- Locate commodity marketplaces.
- Compute the local fair market value of goods and services.

### Interaction and Haggling
If a character uses Business to haggle over the buying or selling of a commodity, the other party's **Intelligence** resistance modifier is applied to the situation die.
- **Character vs. Character:** If the other party also has the Business skill, the Gamemaster may handle it as a contested check.
- **Encounter Skill:** Business can be treated as an [encounter skill](/core-mechanics/characters-in-action/#encounter-scene). If the target's attitude becomes Friendly or better, it provides a bonus to subsequent cooperation checks.

### Modifiers
- **Penalties:** Usually apply when dealing in exotic, rare, or unfamiliar goods; attempting illegal transactions; or dealing with an unfamiliar culture.
- **Bonuses:** Apply if the character has inside information or is dealing with a familiar commodity or culture.
