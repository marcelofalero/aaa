This specialty skill covers automatic rifles—standard infantry weapons capable of selective fire and automatic modes using rifle-caliber cartridges. The [**Automatic Weapon Attack Modes**]({{< relref "equipment/weapons#automatic-weapon-attack-modes" >}}) rules apply.
▶ **Rock-n-Roll:** When a character reaches rank 3 in this specialty, he learns to change clips more efficiently. The penalty for changing a clip and firing in the same action is reduced to +1 step.
