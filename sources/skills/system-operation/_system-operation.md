This broad skill provides a character with basic training in the use of spaceship systems other than the helm or piloting systems. (Vehicle Operation covers the actual piloting of a spaceship.) It also applies to other types of vehicles or ground-based installations that use one or more of the specific systems described below in the Specialty Skills section. These other vehicles and installations include (but are not limited to) large water vessels such as battleships, military submarines, and cargo ships; jet fighters and large commercial aircraft; and radar outposts and gun emplacements. For full details on how **System Operation** skills are used, see the sections on ["What''s in a Spaceship?"](/core-mechanics/vehicles-and-vessels/) and ["Space Vehicle Combat"](/core-mechanics/vehicles-and-vessels#space-vehicle-combat).
