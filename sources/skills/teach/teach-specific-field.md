This specialty skill allows a character to expand their teaching ability to specialized profession groups. For each separate purchase of this specialty skill, the character selects a profession group: **Combat Spec**, **Diplomat**, **Free Agent**, or **Tech Op**. The character then becomes able to teach all skills associated with that profession group in addition to those belonging to the Open group. (e.g., a teacher specialized in Combat Spec can help another character learn or improve a Combat Spec skill).
### Rank Benefits
▶ **Rank 1, 4, 5, 6 [Mindwalker Training]:** If the optional rules for Psionics are being used, a teacher who also has psionic abilities can teach psionic skills to another character who also has psionic abilities.
