name: Survival training

This specialty skill enables a character to improve his ability to use the Survival broad skill in a certain type of terrain. If he wants to improve his skill in more than one type of terrain, he must first succeed on a **Survival – Adaptability** challenge and master the terrain.
Available terrain types include all of those listed in the “Survival Situation Modifiers” sidebar, plus any other type the Gamemaster allows.

When starting a new campaign, a character can buy ranks in this skill for any terrain the GM consider reasonable for the character's background and concept.
