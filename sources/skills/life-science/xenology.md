Xenology is the study of animal life forms of an alien nature and can be used to identify or classify such life forms, including alien plant life. It allows a character to make educated guesses as to the nature, behavior, and abilities of alien life forms.
### Rank Benefits
⊗ **Rank 3, 6, 9, 12 [Increased Effect]:** A character receives a **-1 bonus** at rank 3,
**-2** at rank 6, **-3** at rank 9, and **-4** at rank 12 on certain other skill checks that can be assisted by this specialty. These skills include Survival (in alien environments), Animal Handling (with alien creatures), and Medical Science—xenomedicine.
