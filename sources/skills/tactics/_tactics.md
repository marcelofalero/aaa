This broad skill provides a character with basic training in and an understanding of tactics and tactical situations. While tactics may come into play in many types of situations, it's most commonly used during infantry combat, space combat, and vehicle combat. A successful use of the skill provides a penalty to the opponent's next skill check, indicating that you developed a tactical plan and successfully employed it against him.

### Tactics Check Results
- **Critical Failure:** The opponent sees through your intentions and receives a **-1 bonus** on their next skill check.
- **Failure:** No modifier to the opponent's next skill check.
- **Ordinary:** **+1 penalty** to the opponent's next skill check.
- **Good:** **+2 penalty** to the opponent's next skill check.
- **Amazing:** **+3 penalty** to the opponent's next skill check.
