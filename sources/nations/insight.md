# Insight

### Capital System:

*[Vision]({{< relref "/nations/vision_system" >}})

### Description:
Formerly a subdivision of [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}), betrayed them in the [GW2]({{< relref "/nations/second_galactic_war" >}}) by selling intelligence. They are the best [Gridpilot]({{< relref "/nations/gridpilot" >}})s and hold the free flow of information as their highest virtue.

Insight is the youngest stellar nation, born only 40 years ago. On the surface, it barely deserves mention. It boasts the fewest star systems and the weakest military. But as the saying goes, knowledge is power.

### History
Insight’s history is inextricably entwined with [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) and the [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}). Although systems for electronic data transfer existed in the 1960’s, the first true [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}) came into use in 2032. The Earth-Grid linked all of the planet in a sophisticated virtual environment, and the computer-focused corporation that eventually became [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) had its hand in the [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}) from the Beginning.

While claiming massive areas of space in its early days, [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) made technological leaps. In 2193, the first drivespace communication relay – commonly known as a [drivesat]({{< relref "/nations/drivesat" >}}) – was designed. Initially limited to simple data exchanges, in only seven years [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) Employees integrated [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}) technology with the [drivesats]({{< relref "/nations/drivesat" >}}). While real-time interstellar [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}) exchanges remained impossible, [gridpilot]({{< relref "/nations/gridpilot" >}})s developed the use of shadows to cross [drivespace]({{< relref "/nations/drivespace" >}}) and interact in the interstellar [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}).

Profits and fortunes could be made from this medium, and [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) hastened to realize that goal. Most [stellar nation]({{< relref "/nations/stellar_nations" >}})s licensed creation and even maintenance of [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}) systems to [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) Employees. No independent corporation could compete with the [stellar nation]({{< relref "/nations/stellar_nations" >}}). After two decades of [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) dominance, competitors sensed the opportunity for profit, and [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) saw the need to maintain a division devoted to advanced [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}) design and shadow programming.

Insight was incorporated as a [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) division in 2198. From its inception, Insight produced success after success. [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) technology and implementation became the standard for the interstellar [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}). With a near monopoly, sale of [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) computers skyrocketed as the corporation integrated proprietary hardware and software systems. Insight’s achievements gave its vice president unprecedented influence, and Insight was granted unheard-of independence.

Insight was the closest thing that [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) had to an institutionalized forum for artists. As hefty profits continued to roll in, the [Inseer]({{< relref "/nations/inseer" >}})s, as they came to be known, got whatever they wanted. No [Inseer]({{< relref "/nations/inseer" >}}) held lower than N rank, and top designers were Q’s or higher – the equivalent of a subdivision manager. The unique environment became an accepted part of Insight’s organization. Free intellectual exchange blossomed in one small division of [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}).

#### [The Second Galactic War]({{< relref "/nations/second_galactic_war" >}})
A year before [GW1]({{< relref "/nations/first_galactic_war" >}}), Insight marked its centennial as an incorporated division. Generations of Insight Employees had been born, educated, and even died as members of the division. The top-notch computer professionals had unrestricted access to the [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}) and all the information it could provide. Insight Employees enjoyed not only the liberty to exchange ideas internally, but also channels that connected them to all of humanity. Insight’s intellectual freedom manifested itself in a multitude of ways. Their freedom led to revolution and betrayal.

Insight’s revolt started with small things. Often difficult to manage, the [Inseer]({{< relref "/nations/inseer" >}})s were allowed to choose one of their own as their division’s new vice president in 2314. The practice of promoting [gridpilot]({{< relref "/nations/gridpilot" >}})s from inside the division to leadership soon became an Insight tradition. Under their own leaders, Insight [gridpilot]({{< relref "/nations/gridpilot" >}})s popularized radical systems of thought. The [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}) , [Inseer]({{< relref "/nations/inseer" >}})s posited, is not an artificial reality, or even a secondary reality. The [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}) was, waiting for us to find it. The [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}) is, more real than real, the haven of conscious minds embracing one another. The [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}) will be humanity’s ascension.

Information born free became the Insight motto. To this day, the [Inseer]({{< relref "/nations/inseer" >}})s still believe that data has a natural inclination to disseminate, and no government, religion, or corporation has the right to restrict the flow of information. Given this difference in attitude, the break with [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) was foreordained. Aware of how [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) executives would react to any statement of Insight’s principles, the Insight revolution stayed underground. The [gridpilot]({{< relref "/nations/gridpilot" >}})s expanded the audience for their messages but found the typical [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) Employee uninterested. Most non-Insight Employees were consumed by corporate ambition and unfamiliar with free thought. The [Inseer]({{< relref "/nations/inseer" >}})s realized their lonely situation, and kept looking for the proper means to achieve independence.

While Insight Employees kept up the image of loyal, productive servants, Vice President ZV237 82WBR ([Stalker]({{< relref "/nations/stalker" >}})) met with representatives of the [FreeSpace Alliance]({{< relref "/nations/freespace_alliance" >}}) in 2394. The clandestine gathering set in motion a plan for the next 50 years. In those 50 years, Insight was the most valuable intelligence asset in human history. Insight [gridpilot]({{< relref "/nations/gridpilot" >}})s uncovered key elements of the [Expansion Pentad]({{< relref "/nations/expansion_pentad" >}})’s strategy, plans, and military placements. Unaware of the source of their intelligence reports, the [Orion]({{< relref "/nations/orion_league" >}}) and [Orlamu]({{< relref "/nations/orlamu_theocracy" >}}) militaries were remarkably well prepared for the [Pentad’]({{< relref "/nations/expansion_pentad" >}})s offensives. Time after time, [FreeSpace]({{< relref "/nations/freespace" >}}) attacks found [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) weaknesses – without significant damage to Insight assets.

Insight’s treachery can account for many of the [FreeSpace Alliance]({{< relref "/nations/freespace_alliance" >}})’s successes. Still trapped in the corporation’s grasp, the [Inseer]({{< relref "/nations/inseer" >}})s anxiously awaited the opportunity to leave [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) behind. Their philosophy abhorred the secrets they found themselves keeping. In 2454, the [Inseer]({{< relref "/nations/inseer" >}})s met face to face with President Hale of the [Orion League]({{< relref "/nations/orion_league" >}}). Knowing the value of Insight’s inside information, Hale advised patience. Long tired of this message from the [Orions]({{< relref "/nations/orion_league" >}}), the [Inseer]({{< relref "/nations/inseer" >}})s made their final preparations. On February 5, 2460, independence was declared. Insight positioned its strength in what was formerly [Dreth Commonwealth]({{< relref "/nations/dreth_commonwealth" >}}) space, and seven defecting [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) fortress ships gave the new stellar nation a fierce bite.

[VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) was stunned. Insight’s treachery was the best kept secret of the century. Ignoring Insight warnings, [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) massed their fleet to crush the upstart state. On May 10, 2460, the [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}) crashed. Entire datacores were erased, and star systems found their networks unresponsive. Many [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) [drivesat]({{< relref "/nations/drivesat" >}})s simply vanished. The damage to the [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) economy and infrastructure couldn’t be measured; [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) is still recovering today. The invasion of Insight was aborted; buttressed by [FreeSpace]({{< relref "/nations/freespace" >}}) assistance, Insight survived until the war’s end.

#### Today
With their freedom established, the [Inseer]({{< relref "/nations/inseer" >}})s shed most relics of the [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) organization. Inseers were declared private citizens, no longer held accountable by management and the board of directors. No formal government has emerged to replace the old system. Governors exert nominal control over Insight planets and systems, but the only term to describe the current situation is institutionalized anarchy. To outsiders, no order can be discerned. [Inseer]({{< relref "/nations/inseer" >}})s admit the administration is unconventional, but point to a clear pecking order set by reputation and [gridpilot]({{< relref "/nations/gridpilot" >}})ing ability.

Insight leads much of the computer industry, specializing in [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}) hardware, software, and interfaces. While [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}) systems remain universal and fixed – boring, say the [Inseer]({{< relref "/nations/inseer" >}})s – Insight [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}) systems are beautiful, unpredictable works of art. Despite their complexity, the Insight environments allow greater individuality and creativity. When used by a skilled [gridpilot]({{< relref "/nations/gridpilot" >}}), Insight systems provide the best performance known. Insight’s relentless advances into [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) ’s markets has fueled the hatred [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) feels for its rebellious child.

[VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) has refused to recognize Insight’s independence except to the extent required by the [Treaty of Concord]({{< relref "/nations/treaty_of_concord" >}}). [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) often refuses to attend diplomatic conferences where [Inseer]({{< relref "/nations/inseer" >}})s are expected, and [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) Employees are taught that Insight is composed of deviants and traitors. For their part, most [Inseer]({{< relref "/nations/inseer" >}})s believe that [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) represents a step in mankind’s evolution that they have transcended. In the [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}) , the war quietly rages on, as [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) uses its greater resources to test Insight skill. The guerrilla warfare remains largely invisible to all save its players, and the [Galactic Concord]({{< relref "/nations/galactic_concord" >}}) is powerless to stop the giants of the industry from tearing at one another’s throats.

Most [Inseer]({{< relref "/nations/inseer" >}})s have adopted the doctrine of the Insightful. The [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}) represents the first environment in which minds can exchange information uncluttered by hindrances. Free information is a natural state to which the [Inseer]({{< relref "/nations/inseer" >}})s believe all sentients will ascend. Of course, the demands of a successful economy mean that Insight endorses free information but markets the technology to receive, manipulate, and send information. Like all stellar nations, Insight is invested in a range of industries from agriculture to weapons manufacture; in all of these, though, the Insight bent toward advanced technology is visible.

Nowhere is Insight’s technophilia more apparent than on its capital planet, [Vision]({{< relref "/nations/vision_planet" >}}). The Insight homeworld boasts a systemwide [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}) network with a complexity rivaling the ancient [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}) of [Sol]({{< relref "/nations/solar_system" >}}). Implemented using only the best equipment, the Vision [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}) has both the fastest carrier rates and the highest carrier load. Even those unfamiliar with [gridpilot]({{< relref "/nations/gridpilot" >}})ing admit its frenzied splendor; [gridpilot]({{< relref "/nations/gridpilot" >}})s, hackers, and reality entertainment junkies come from around the galaxy to behold Vision’s wonders.

The Insight military remains one of the smallest in the galaxy. It lost one of its fortress ships in the war and has not rebuilt it, but six fortress ships still bear the Insight banner. Plans call for only a modest increase in the Insight defense forces; the fledgling [stellar nation]({{< relref "/nations/stellar_nations" >}}) continues to rely on its ability to successfully wage an information war against any foe.

In the [Verge]({{< relref "/nations/verge" >}}), Insight took over the [VoidCorp]({{< relref "/nations/voidcorp" >}}) colony at [Walin III]({{< relref "/nations/walin_iii_planet" >}}) in the [Walin]({{< relref "/nations/walin_system" >}}) system by a bit of logistical sabotage, sending only Insight employees to found the colony. Elsewhere in the region, Insight techs are busy building or rebuilding the local [Gridspace]({{< relref "/nations/gridspace" >}})s. Curious [Inseer]({{< relref "/nations/inseer" >}}) [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}) tourists are a common sight in the [Aegis]({{< relref "/nations/aegis_system" >}}), [Oberon]({{< relref "/nations/oberon_system" >}}), and [Karnath]({{< relref "/nations/karnath_system" >}}) Grids, and the planet [Bhruu]({{< relref "/nations/bhruu_planet" >}}) in the [Karnath]({{< relref "/nations/karnath_planet" >}}) system is a major Insight settlement.

[Inseer]({{< relref "/nations/inseer" >}})s gain a -1 step bonus to all computer activities, including design, use hacking, programming and any [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}) -based actions. All [Inseer]({{< relref "/nations/inseer" >}})s suffer a +1 step penalty on real-world action checks.

### Game Benefits:
[Inseer]({{< relref "/nations/inseer" >}})s gain a -1 step bonus to all computer activities. Insight heroes and their shadows gain a - 1 step bonus on Grid action checks. They also have a +1 step penalty to real world action checks.
