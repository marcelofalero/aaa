# Voidcorp

### Capital System:

* [Catalog]({{< relref "/nations/catalog_system" >}}) 

### Description:
Order is the primary virtue of VoidCorp. Nothing can be obtained before order is established. Formed from an immense corporation, it is the primary producer of computer software and hardware as well as the [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}). 

Space is chaotic and unpredictable. Although humanity may never be able to fashion stability in the stars themselves, it can put its own house in order. Only when order is maintained is humankind free to pursue goals of profit, advancement, and societal growth. Only in the [stellar nation]({{< relref "/nations/stellar_nations" >}}) called VoidCorp can one find such order, and it is that very order which explains the economic and political leadership of VoidCorp in the Galactic community.. or so claim the corporate brochures.

### History
VoidCorp traces its roots to a corporation born in the late 20th century. Originally a software company, VoidCorp’s ancestor [Microtel]({{< relref "/nations/microtel" >}}) grew into Earth’s largest and most powerful corporation. When the 21st-century wars between corporations, nations, and superpowers broke out, the corporation invested in dozens of industries while maintaining its supremacy in computer systems. With a virtual monopoly on the market, the transnational corporation wielded power greater than that of many nations, and attempts to break it met with a violent failure. By the end of the 21st century, VoidCorp’s precursor had secured a position as the most powerful corporation on the planet. It had already gained its reputation for ruthless business practices and territorialism.

With the beginning of exploration into human space, a new corporate division came into existence. The new division, VoidCorp, was stocked with the most talented employees. granted vast resources, the VoidCorp mission was explicit and direct: carry the stellar corporation into space. Out among the stars, VoidCorp had the opportunity to found an autonomous collective free of interfering governments. At last, the corporation could define every aspect of the new state.

The corporate plan met with great success. VoidCorp employees settled dozens of star systems, and in 2193 VoidCorp developed the first [drivesats]({{< relref "/nations/drivesat" >}}) to link its star systems together. Of course, some VoidCorp Employees were shocked as the corporation took the final step: Personnel, long listed as a corporate asset, became property of the corporation. Its internal strength assured, VoidCorp grew much as any nation in space.

Humans were not the only servants of the corporation. In 2274, a VoidCorp vessel landed on [Sheya]({{< relref "/nations/sheya_planet" >}}), the largest moon of the system’s fourth planet. There, project leader JT795 43IWQ (Erim Ollander) has first contact with an alien [species]({{< relref "/nations/species" >}}) known as the [Sesheyan]({{< relref "/nations/sesheyan" >}}). JT795 43IWQ negotiated a contract with the [species]({{< relref "/nations/species" >}}). All [Sesheyan]({{< relref "/nations/sesheyan" >}})’s are held to the [Sesheyan Compact]({{< relref "/nations/sesheyan_compact" >}}): In return for the benefits of technology and the freedom to leave their own planet, the [Sesheyan]({{< relref "/nations/sesheyan" >}})’s were named VoidCorp Employees in perpetuity. In essence, they became subject to all VoidCorp corporate rules and VoidCorp authorities, and each [sesheyan]({{< relref "/nations/sesheyan" >}}) was assigned an Employee Identification Number.

In VoidCorp space, every child, human or [sesheyan]({{< relref "/nations/sesheyan" >}}), is a VoidCorp Employee from the moment of birth. VoidCorp Employees attend corporate schools and corporate colleges, compete in corporate athletic and academic games, and take their assigned places in the corporate structure.

#### The [First Galactic War]({{< relref "/nations/first_galactic_war" >}})
Within two generations, the remaining vestiges of Old Earth ties were erased. One of those ties was to VoidCorp’s parent corporation. When the [First Galactic War]({{< relref "/nations/first_galactic_war" >}}) erupted in 2299, VoidCorp took up sides with many of the colonies of space against the [Terran Empire]({{< relref "/nations/terran_empire" >}}) and the superpowers of Earth. The VoidCorp leadership had long ago relocated itself from Earth to the planet [Catalog]({{< relref "/nations/catalog_planet" >}}) to take a hands-on approach, and VoidCorp brought with it all of its parent’s stellar assets. As a result, a civil war was fought on economic grounds. The parent corporation bankrolled the Terran’s forays into VoidCorp space, and VoidCorp retaliated with the aid of neighboring states. The war fought between VoidCorp and its parent corporation in cyberspace was the first of its kind.

At the end of the war, VoidCorp claimed victory. Its parent had depleted its financial resources, and collapsed under the relentless attacks of its former employees. VoidCorp was named one of the 26 [stellar nations]({{< relref "/nations/stellar_nations" >}}) in the [Treaty of Earth]({{< relref "/nations/treaty_of_earth" >}}).

In the years after [GW1]({{< relref "/nations/first_galactic_war" >}}), VoidCorp tightened control internally even as it expanded externally. In 2330, an official corporate memo forbade Employees from ever leaving the company. Ironically, the memo didn’t mention even death as a valid form of leave-taking; the memo was eventually called the Death Oath. At the same time, the edges of VoidCorp advanced farther from [Sol]({{< relref "/nations/union_of_sol" >}}) than those of any other [stellar nation]({{< relref "/nations/stellar_nations" >}}). Management demanded more and more raw resources and hoped to discover another client [species]({{< relref "/nations/species" >}}).

#### The Second Galactic War
Such aggressive inclinations led directly into the second war between the [stellar nations]({{< relref "/nations/stellar_nations" >}}). Just before the war, VoidCorp signed alliances with two of its neighbors, the [Nariac Domain]({{< relref "/nations/nariac_domain" >}}) and the [Dreth Commonwealth]({{< relref "/nations/dreth_commonwealth" >}}). Both became client states under the leadership of the big corporation, and VoidCorp borders were secure. Although [Rigunmor]({{< relref "/nations/rigunmor_star_consortium" >}}) and [Solar Union]({{< relref "/nations/union_of_sol" >}}) forces occasionally raided VoidCorp territories, its planets survived the first three decades of the war largely unscathed. In 2361 VoidCorp joined the [Expansion Pentad]({{< relref "/nations/expansion_pentad" >}}). The [Rigunmor]({{< relref "/nations/rigunmor_star_consortium" >}})’s shattered the [Dreth Commonwealth]({{< relref "/nations/dreth_commonwealth" >}}) in 2380, and VoidCorp losses rose as it took over the front of the war. Expenditures reached heights that drained even VoidCorp’s immense treasury.

Unknown to most VoidCorp Employees, the worst was yet to come. [Insight]({{< relref "/nations/insight" >}}), a corporate division responsible for [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}) and software design, had grown disillusioned with VoidCorp. For decades, [Insight]({{< relref "/nations/insight" >}}) provided the [FreeSpace Alliance]({{< relref "/nations/freespace_alliance" >}}) with information about VoidCorp plans and battle tactics. During a [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}) battle between the [Orions]({{< relref "/nations/orion_league" >}}) and VoidCorp in 2433, shadows of the [Inseers]({{< relref "/nations/inseers" >}}) handed the [Orions]({{< relref "/nations/orion_league" >}}) the victory. In 2460, [Insight]({{< relref "/nations/insight" >}}) grew tired of waiting and declared its independence, claiming the area of the former [Dreth Commonwealth]({{< relref "/nations/dreth_commonwealth" >}}). As VoidCorp readied its response, the rebels countered by crashing the [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}) upon which VoidCorp relied. History repeated itself as a corporate division once again spun off into autonomy.

#### Today
VoidCorp remains at heart unchanged. It has retained its centuries-old focus on computer software, hardware, and the [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}). Its draconian practices still form the most regimented way of life known to man. To VoidCorp management, this structure is the most advantageous avenue for profit. VoidCorp Employees, raised in its culture, accept it as a fact of life. Indeed, when [Insight]({{< relref "/nations/insight" >}}) broke off, most VoidCorp dissidents left with it, and now VoidCorp homogeneity is greater than ever.

The VoidCorp corporate structure mirrors its business practices. At the lowest level, Employees are little more than slaves. Over 70% of the population fits this mold. Once in a skilled or managerial position, Employees grow intensely competitive. The society succeeds, evolves, and holds itself together through struggle. Inferiors expect denigration and abuse. Sycophancy, submission, and currying favor mark behavior to a supervisor. When the superior is gone, machinations to gain position take over. Political maneuvering, favoritism, and backstabbing are well established; informers are rewarded. Deaths of superiors that remain unexplained guarantee promotion. Officers of vice president rank and above live anonymously.

As Machiavellian as it may sound, the system works.

In terms of sheer wealth, only the [Rigunmor Star Consortium]({{< relref "/nations/rigunmor_star_consortium" >}}) can boast deeper pockets than VoidCorp. Thanks to its strict hierarchy, VoidCorp can bring resources to bear more effectively than the more disorganized, if richer, [Consortium]({{< relref "/nations/rigunmor_star_consortium" >}}). The VoidCorp military is recovering following [Insight]({{< relref "/nations/insight" >}})’s treachery. VoidCorp commands only 10 fortress ships; eight were lost during the war – seven to [Insight]({{< relref "/nations/insight" >}}) and another wasted in tithe to the [Concord]({{< relref "/nations/galactic_concord" >}}). The prodigious VoidCorp assets are rebuilding a dominant military. Only the tenacious corporate will to succeed has allowed VoidCorp to weather the times. The VoidCorp Grid, ironically updated by [Insight]({{< relref "/nations/insight" >}}) before its defection, has kept the [stellar nation]({{< relref "/nations/stellar_nations" >}}) at the top.

Rule of the [Sesheyan]({{< relref "/nations/sesheyan" >}})’s continues as it has for centuries. Few [Sesheyan]({{< relref "/nations/sesheyan" >}})’s have climbed the corporate ladder; most are pawns working as specialist couriers, bounty hunters, or assassins. VoidCorp executives also retain [Sesheyan]({{< relref "/nations/sesheyan" >}})’s as bodyguards. [Sesheyan]({{< relref "/nations/sesheyan" >}}) attempts to win freedom are crushed whenever discovered. The native, planet-bound [Sesheyan]({{< relref "/nations/sesheyan" >}})’s can only mourn the loss of their friends who willingly serve the corporate state.

According to management, the corporate structure is the next stage in humanity’s evolution. Minor setbacks such as [Insight]({{< relref "/nations/insight" >}})’s revolt are the last throes of humans resisting their destiny. ([Insight]({{< relref "/nations/insight" >}})’s existence is not officially acknowledged.)

Unofficially, the [Grid]({{< relref "/nations/grid" >}}) war between the corporations rages on, largely unseen. Shadow saboteurs on both sides scramble to destroy relay stations, databases, and public records. Recent years have been filled with the results: phony press releases from both companies, tampering with annual reports, as well as unexplained power and data losses. Both deny being either the instigators or victims of any act of sabotage.

VoidCorp is extremely active in the [Verge]({{< relref "/nations/verge" >}}), despite the distances involved. It is pursuing legal and harassment campaigns against the [Grith]({{< relref "/nations/grith" >}}) [Sesheyan's]({{< relref "/nations/sesheyan" >}})  of the [Corrivale]({{< relref "/nations/corrivale_system" >}}) system and is busily exploiting resources, such as its gas mines on [Redcrown]({{< relref "/nations/redcrown_planet" >}}) in the [Aegis]({{< relref "/nations/aegis_system" >}}) system and the refineries and strip mines of [Iphus]({{< relref "/nations/iphus_planet" >}}) in the [Corrivale]({{< relref "/nations/corrivale_system" >}}) system. Its exploitation teams (resource-oriented survey teams) are investigating all likely sources of raw materials in the region. Corporate strategy dictates that the [Verge]({{< relref "/nations/verge" >}}) will be one of the corporation’s next great profit centers.

Rank is everything. Stop at nothing to get it. Among employees (as VoidCorp citizens are called), such statements are common, even accepted. Every citizen holds a corporate rank from birth, expressed in his Employee Identification Number. They range from AA, AB to ZZ. At ranks M and above, Employees accept management positions. VoidCorp does not permit retirement – Employees must work until death. VoidCorp takes steps to ensure that all of its Employees are prepared for a life in the harsh realities of interstellar business.

VoidCorp heroes gain the Business broad skill and the Business-corporate specialty skill for free and all uses of the broad skill and their respective specialty skills receive a -1 bonus.

Employees permanently fill in the leftmost box on the achievement track, thus advancing in levels faster than others (can never be turned in for skill points).

### Game Benefits:
VoidCorp Heroes start with Business broad skill and Business-corporate specialty skill. Any use of the Business broad skill and its specialties gain a -1 step bonus.
