The Gliese 300 habitat dome is made of microengineered numaplas—a tough, heavy plastic fabric chambered with self-inflating pneumatic bladders. A single standard COg cartridge (two are included) can deploy the dome in moments. When inflated, the dome is 2 meters tall and 5 meters in diameter.
