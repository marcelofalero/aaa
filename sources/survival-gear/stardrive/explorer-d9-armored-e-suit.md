The Explorer's armored E-Suit is lined with a self-sealing, puncture-resistant inner layer. It provides protection against environmental hazards like vacuum, gravity, radiation, and atmospheric pressure changes. The suit loses its integrity if the wearer suffers 4 points or more of primary wound or mortal damage.
