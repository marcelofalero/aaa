The TerrainMaster stows in a convenient backpack frame. While it is a serious load for one hiker, the stowage frame includes a built-in mass neutralizer that reduces its mass to 5 kilograms when activated. The neutralizer has an endurance of 20 hours at this setting or 50 hours if set to reduce to 15 kg.
