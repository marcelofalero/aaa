PARTICLE SCREENS

Energy screens rely on the use of electromagnetic or
gravitational energy to deflect attacks. Particle screens
wark a little differently, by interposing a wall of subatom-
ic matter contained within a magnetic field. Incoming
attacks are scattered and absorbed by the particles, atten-
uating the attack. Particle screen technology is still new
and unrefined; the machinery is heavy, expensive, and
impractical. Typically, man-portable particle screens are
limited to the heaviest powered armor. Note that no
screen or field can be combined with any other.
