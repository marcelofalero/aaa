The autoflechette gun fires a 25mm round that contains a bundle of tiny, razor-sharp aerofoils. A single hit can carve an unarmored person to ribbons, but the autoflechette gun can also be used in full automatic mode with a rate of fire equal to that of most submachine guns—thus, it can clean out a roomful of targets with a few bursts.<br />Like the standard shotgun, damage from the autoflechette is highly dependent on range. At short range, damage is doubled. At long range, damage rolls are halved (round down, to a minimum of 1 point).
