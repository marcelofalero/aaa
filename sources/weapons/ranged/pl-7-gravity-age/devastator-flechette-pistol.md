The Devastator is a succinct statement of the Soze Arms design philosophy-overkill is the way to go. This pistol fires a wide-pattern flechette round, shredding anything in the general vicinity. It inflicts double the listed damage at close range (8 meters or less), but only half damage for shots at long range (16 to 40 meters). The flechette pistol doesn't have the full-auto capability of the autoflechette shotgun. (Payload: 25mm flechette round)
