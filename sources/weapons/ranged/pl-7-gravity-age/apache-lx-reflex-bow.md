The bow's greatest advantage is silence and stealth. It has no muzzle flash or loud report to give away the user's location, making it perfect for dense terrain such as jungle or heavy brush. Its disadvantage is the low rate of fire (after each 'shot', the user must waste an action to ready another arrow, or shoot with a +2 step penalty) and its size, which prevents it from being fired from a prone position. (Payload: Razor-tipped arrow)
