Made famous by the action holomovies of Jack Everstar, the Nova 6 is a top-of-the-line mass pistol. Slightly more efficient than most mass pistols, the charge cells of a Nova 6 can power up to six shots before exhausting the weapon's energy reserves. Mass pistols are virtually useless in open terrain, but there aren't many weapons better for close-quarters combat against armored foes. (Payload: Gravity point-source)
