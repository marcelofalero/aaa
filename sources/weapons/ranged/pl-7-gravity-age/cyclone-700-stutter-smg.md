The stutter SMG isn't a real submachine gun, of course; it's referred to as an SMG because of its high cyclic rate of fire. It's intended for use in close-quarters riot control or police raid situations in which the authorities must subdue a large number of people in a very short time. Stutter SMGs are also favored by special forces teams attempting to take prisoners instead of simply killing their enemies. (Payload: Compressed air)
