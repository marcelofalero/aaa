The Gravity Age successors to the venerable flamethrower, flamers are vicious weapons that spew a tight aerosol spray of self-oxidizing fuel gel at the target. Its broad pattern ensures great accuracy at close range, and the flame spray inflicts terrible wounds. By holding down the trigger and fanning the weapon from side to side, the user can achieve an autofirelike effect, but this rapidly exhausts his fuel. (Payload: Incendiary stream)
