Sabot pistols are big and heavy, but they're the most powerful handguns produced in any numbers. The weapon has great range for a pistol, and almost no kick-the accelerator pushes the slug out of the barrel with a smooth, nonexplosive action. The sabot pistol's lanthanide cell provides enough energy to fire four clips of ammo before it's exhausted; the cell can be recharged for $10 or replaced for $200. (Payload: 10mm sabot ramjet)
