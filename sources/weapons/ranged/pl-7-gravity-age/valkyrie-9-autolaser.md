The largest and most powerful autolaser available, the Valkyrie offers damage equal to a laser rifle with full automatic fire. While many people refer to the autolaser as a laser submachine gun, this is a misnomer-the device is not a machine gun and has little in common with any conventional firearm. Bursts or full-auto attacks with this weapon are actually sweeps and slashes of its white-hot beam. (Payload: Coherent light)
