IF-3 has been the standard infantry weapon of the Concord Defense Corps and the Concord Marines. Adopted from Austrin-Ontis as the best combat rifle available at the time, the IF-3 is light, rugged, and long-ranged. With the addition of a $50 conversion kit, the IF-3 can be configured for zero-g combat. This halves all range figures, but reduces zero-g penalties by 1 step. (Payload: 11mm bullet)
