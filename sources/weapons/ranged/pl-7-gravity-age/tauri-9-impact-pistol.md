A deadly refinement of stutter weapon technology, the impact pistol fires an even more powerful blast of compressed air that can powder concrete at close range. It can be set for a high-speed mode that allows burst fire (but not full automatic), or for low-speed, single-shot mode. Like any stutter weapon, the impact pistol doesn't work in vacuum due to the lack of air to compress. (Payload: Compressed air)
