The Bulldog is a fine example of a second-generation weapon. Many of the flaws of earlier charge SMGs were corrected in this design. It's compact and sturdy, standing up to the worst field conditions. The most innovative feature is a recoil suppression system that controls the natural tendency of an automatic weapon to "walk" when fired in full auto-the Bulldog's accuracy is legendary. (Payload: 9mm bullet)
