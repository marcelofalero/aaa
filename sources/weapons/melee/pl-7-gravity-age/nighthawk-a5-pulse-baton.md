Pulse batons have been in use for more than a century. Few unarmored targets can stand up to more than one or two shocks from its powerful capacitors. The Nighthawk is a telescoping stealth model made from low-density plastics. It's extremely concealable and does not show up on most weapon detectors, allowing the user to smuggle it into places he might not normally be armed.
