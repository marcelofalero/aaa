A filament blade's fighting edge is a single strand of monomolecular carbonate fiber. The weapon consists of a hilt and a small, free-floating lead module, between which the monofilament is stretched. The lead module senses the user's swings and maneuvers accordingly, keeping the filament taut at all times. The weapon can be set for a length of up to 4 meters for melee attacks against distant targets.
