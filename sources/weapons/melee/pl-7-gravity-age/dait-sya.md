The dait'sya is a traditional t'sa weapon, a double-bladed dagger held in the wielder's closed fist. They're always used in pairs (the listed cost, mass, and damage is for two weapons). Combined with the lightning speed of most t'sa, the dait'sya is especially dangerous in close-quarters combat. Because of the dait'sya's light design, any penalties for fighting with a weapon in each hand are reduced by 1 step.
