TN stands for tungsten-neutronite. It's the toughest conventional alloy known, capable of punching through cerametal or vanadium steel like an iron crowbar through tin foil. This weapon is a large combat knife, but it's far denser (and heavier) than a conventional blade. The TN knife is the only nonpowered weapon capable of piercing powered attack armors such as the body tank.
