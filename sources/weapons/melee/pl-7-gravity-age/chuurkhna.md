An ancient weren design, the chuurkhna is still favored by members of that race. It's a glaive or halberd with a haft about a meter and a half long. Its blade is large and heavy, intended for chopping rather than thrusting. A weren can wield it one- or two-handed, but most humans would have to use both hands to control it. Weren have been known to drive chuurkhnas through the heaviest body armor.
