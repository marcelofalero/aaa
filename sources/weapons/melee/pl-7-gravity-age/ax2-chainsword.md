The chainsword is heavy and unwieldy, but it delivers outstanding damage in hand-to-hand combat. Its fighting edge is a chain-driven array of serrated, microengineered teeth, capable of cutting through almost any normal material. (In fact, the chainsword serves as a decent concrete saw in a pinch.) The AX2 is a heavy-duty model designed for use against heavily armored targets.
