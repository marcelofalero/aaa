Winner of the Merrick's Triple-Bullseye, the HeadBuster is the best power cestus available. It's lighter and more concealable than most power cesti, but provides the same hitting power. The HeadBuster is so easy to use that most wielders require no formal training in powered weapons; the wielder can choose to use either his Melee Weapons or Unarmed Attack skill when attacking with the HeadBuster.
