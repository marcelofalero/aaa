The first powered melee weapon capable of inflicting lethal damage, the pulse baton is a half-meter-long rod that delivers a concentrated energy discharge when it hits a target.<br />While the baton appears to be a simple flashlight, the operator must wear a pair of insulated gloves to prevent an accidental discharge.
