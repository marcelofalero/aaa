This low-powered version of the pulse baton is sometimes known as a taser. Above the insulated handle, its surface crackles with a high-voltage, low-current electrical charge that can temporarily short-circuit the human nervous system.<br />Insulated gloves are necessary to prevent the user from accidentally shocking himself. Without gloves, the user suffers Ordinary damage (d4+1s) if his Melee Attack-powered weapon skill check result is a Critical Failure.
