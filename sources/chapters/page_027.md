sesheyan point of view, the world

undisputed masters of that world. When human star vessels reach
the sesheyan homeworld, they find

the stone age. Though many sesheyans may decide to travel the stars as
allies of the galactic community,

traditions. A sesheyan’s leathery flesh comes

mist-filled air are the sesheyan's
