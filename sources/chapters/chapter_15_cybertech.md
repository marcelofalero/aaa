# CYBERTECH

Cybernetic technology combines complex computer electronics with the human nervous system. Some cyber gear is purely decorative, used to make a techno fashion statement. Other cyber gear serves a necessary function, replacing limbs or organs lost to accident or disease. Finally, there’s the gear that users believe give them an edge—cyber weapons, defenses, and utilities to make a cyber-knight stand out from the crowd.

Cyber gear may or may not be available in a given campaign, and even in those where cyber gear can be purchased, the Gamemaster might limit quantities and availability. The only campaigns in which some form of cybernetics must be used are those that include mechalus (one of the alien species described in Chapter 2). Because mechalus have several cybernetic enhancements, it's not possible to have that species in a campaign setting without also using at least some of these rules.

## INSTALLING CYBER GEAR

Any living creature can be outfitted with cyber gear. There are two important things to remember about cyber gear: the initial skill point cost to train a character in the use of the equipment, and the limits imposed by the character's own Constitution.

A character must spend **10 skill points** to prepare himself for cyber gear installation. This represents the intensive training required to learn to use even passive implants like body plating. (Some minor items are exempt from this cost).

A character’s Constitution score determines the upper limit of the amount of cybernetics his body can support. All cyber gear has a "size” associated with it. No character can possess gear whose sizes total more than his Constitution score.

### Cyber Surgery

Any character with the Medical Science-surgery skill and the cyber installation rank benefit can attempt to install cyber gear. A hero can purchase installation from a reputable cyber surgeon or a cheap cyber “chop shop”.

| Surgeon Skill | Facility | Check Mod | Install Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--: | :--- |
| 6-12 | Chop Shop | +2 | Item Cost x 0.5 |
| 13-16 | Ordinary | -1 | Item Cost |
| 17-19 | Good | -2 | Item Cost x 2 |

**Surgery Results:**
*   **Critical Failure:** Installation fails; patient suffers d4 points of mortal damage.
*   **Failure:** Installation fails; patient suffers 1 point of mortal damage.
*   **Success:** Cyber gear is installed. The patient may need to make a Constitution feat check to see if their body accepts the implant (see Cyber Tolerance).

### Cyber Tolerance

A character's cyber tolerance score is equal to their Constitution score (or CON +4 for mechalus). This score is divided into three sections on the supplemental hero sheet to track the amount of cyber gear installed.
*   **Left Section:** 1/2 CON score (rounded down)
*   **Center Section:** 1/4 CON score (rounded up)
*   **Right Section:** The remainder

When a new piece of gear is installed, the character marks off a number of boxes equal to the gear's size. If the new gear causes more than half of the total cyber tolerance boxes to be marked, the character must make a Constitution feat check. If the check fails, the body rejects the implant, and the surgery is considered a Failure.

### Damage to Cyber Gear
*   If the **center section** of the cyber tolerance track has any boxes marked, any mortal damage the hero suffers is considered damage to their cyber gear and must be repaired by a cyber surgeon.
*   If the **right section** has any boxes marked, any mortal *or* wound damage is considered damage to cyber gear.
*   If a character takes damage amounting to more than half their total mortal rating in a single battle, the Gamemaster may declare some cyber gear destroyed.

### Cykosis Sidebar
Cykosis, or cyber-psychosis, is a dangerous mental condition that can result from the overuse of reflex-enhancing cybernetics like the Fast Chip. Each time a character activates such a device under stressful conditions or pushes it beyond its limits, the Gamemaster may call for a Willpower feat check. Failure can lead to temporary or permanent psychological instability, manifesting as paranoia, aggression, emotional detachment, or other personality changes.

### Enhance Programs Sidebar
Enhance programs are specialized software that requires both a nanocomputer and Reflex cyberware to function. When active, they provide a direct bonus to specific skills by streamlining the user's neural responses for that task. The quality of the program determines the bonus. However, this performance boost comes at a cost: for every round the enhance program is active, the user suffers stun damage due to neural strain.

---

*   **BattleKlaw:** A cybernetic talon that improves unarmed damage. Can be fixed or retractable. Amazing quality requires an exoskeleton.
*   **BioArt:** Purely decorative subdermal neon implants (glowing tattoos) or fiber-optic filaments.
*   **BioWatch:** A subdermal timepiece with various functions, visible only when tapped.
*   **Body Plating:** Metal and plastic armor bonded to the body. Obvious on close inspection.
*   **CF Skinweave:** Carbonate fiber woven with living flesh for hidden protection. Adds to durability ratings.
*   **Cyberlimb:** A cybernetic replacement for a lost limb, or an "improvement." Can house other gear and increases Strength for that limb.
*   **Cyberoptics:** A cybernetic eye replacement or additional sensor. Can provide normal vision, digital recording, zoom, night vision, or thermal vision depending on quality.
*   **Data Slot, Passive:** An external, hidden storage slot for data mediums like 3Ds. Not connected to a nanocomputer.
*   **ER Slot:** An "emergency room" slot containing a trauma pack II that automatically activates when the user is knocked out.
*   **Exoskeleton:** Reinforces a character's natural skeleton, required for powerful cyber gear. Also increases durability ratings.
*   **Fast Chip:** A dangerous, often banned, device that provides a significant bonus to action checks for a short duration at the cost of fatigue and the risk of cykosis.
*   **MusclePlus:** Replaces living muscle with synthetic material, increasing Strength in the affected limbs.
*   **Nanocomputer:** An implanted processor required for most cyber gear. Includes cytronic circuitry to link with the user's nervous system.
*   **Neural 3D Data Slot, external:** An external port for a 3D or other data storage, connected to an installed nanocomputer.
*   **NIJack (Neural Interface Jack):** A physical plug on the body allowing data cables to connect to the user's cytronic circuitry. Essential for gridcasters.
*   **Optic Screen:** A lens over an eyeball that acts as a heads-up display for computer data without obstructing normal vision.
*   **Reflex:** Specialized circuitry that allows the use of Enhance programs to boost skill performance.
*   **Self-Repair Unit:** An internal device with nanite robots that can repair damaged cyber gear over time.
*   **Subdermal Comm Gear:** A hidden internal communications system.
*   **Subdermal Weapon Mount:** A hidden mount within the body for a small weapon.
*   **Wireless NIJack:** A sensor that creates a wireless data link to a computer or gridcaster within 10 meters.
