# VEHICLES

An entire adventure can take place inside a confined space, and heroes might need nothing more than their own two feet for motive power. But more often than not, succeeding on a mission involves traveling—getting from a starting point to a destination, and perhaps also being able to move around at high speed to catch someone or avoid being caught. That’s where vehicles come in.

This chapter describes basic vehicles available in the ALTERNITY universe. It provides brief descriptions of land, water, air, and space vehicles, as well as rules for what happens when two vehicles attempt to occupy the same place at the same time. Each of the vehicles described below are listed on TABLE P44: VEHICLES, on the following page, which gives characteristics and statistics for all available vehicles.

## How to Read the Vehicles Table
*   **Vehicle (PL):** The name of the vehicle and the Progress Level at which it was developed.
*   **Skill:** The specialty skill used to operate the vehicle.
*   **Drv/Hnd:** The Drive/Handling bonus or penalty for the vehicle.
*   **Move (kph):** The vehicle's cruise and maximum speeds in kilometers per hour.
*   **Durability:** The vehicle's stun, wound, and mortal damage ratings.
*   **Cost:** The base price of the vehicle in credits ($).

## VEHICLE DESCRIPTIONS

### PL 0-3: Stone Age through Age of Reason
Early vehicles that did not rely on animal power used the medium of water for transport.
*   **Canoe:** A small boat powered by oars or paddles. The table describes a two-person version.
*   **Raft:** A flat flotation device, usually made by tying logs together.
*   **Rowboat:** Similar to a canoe but with more stability, propelled by oars.
*   **Sailboat:** A small boat powered by wind. The standard vessel holds six people.

### PL 4: Industrial Age
The first automobiles and air vehicles appear.
*   **Bicycle:** A simple, one-person multi-speed bike.
*   **Cabin cruiser:** A water vessel with bunking for up to six people; range of 500 km.
*   **Motorcar:** The first automobile, replacing animal power.
*   **Motor yacht:** A luxury boat with a respectable speed and range.
*   **Propeller plane:** The first type of aircraft, using propellers to achieve flight.
*   **Speedboat:** A small, fast boat with a range of 100 km.
*   **Tank:** A military design for heavy assault on the modern battlefield, equipped with a 75mm cannon and moderate alloy armor.

### PL 5: Information Age
Refinements and improvements over the previous age's developments.
*   **Compact car:** A small, inexpensive automobile.
*   **Executive jet:** A luxury aircraft with the accoutrements of an office.
*   **Fighter jet:** A military aircraft providing reconnaissance and attack capabilities.
*   **Helicopter:** A light utility aircraft that holds a pilot and six passengers.
*   **Jet ski:** A recreational watercraft for two people.
*   **Luxury car:** A large, expensive automobile with luxurious amenities.
*   **Mid-sized car:** A medium-sized automobile, typical for families.
*   **Motorcycle:** A two-wheeled, motorized vehicle for one or two people.
*   **Pickup truck:** A flatbed truck for an operator and up to two passengers, plus cargo.
*   **Private jet:** A small jet aircraft for a pilot and three to eight passengers.
*   **Semi:** A powerful cab and engine for pulling large trailers.
*   **Snowmobile:** A vehicle for two people for use over snow-covered or icy terrain.
*   **Sport utility vehicle (SUV):** A powerful all-wheel-drive vehicle for various terrains.
*   **Sports car:** A small, sleek automobile with a powerful engine.
*   **Ultralight:** A hang glider with a tiny engine, weighing only 50 kg.
*   **Van:** A panel van with seats for an operator and one passenger, with room for cargo.

### PL 6: Fusion Age
Gravity induction technology brings personal air and commercial space travel into reach.
*   **Electric car:** A silent, easy-to-handle car that runs for 24 hours before requiring a one-hour recharge.
*   **Jetpack:** Allows flight at speeds up to 300 kph with a range of 500 km.
*   **Reentry capsule:** A personal emergency escape device for surviving a trip from orbit to a planet's surface.
*   **Skybike:** A single-person flying machine using gravity induction.
*   **Skycar:** An air vehicle available in various styles, like a sports coupe or luxury sedan.
*   **Skytank:** The backbone of ground assaults, equipped with a 120mm rail cannon and heavy cerametal armor.
*   **STG shuttle:** A space-to-ground shuttle for people and cargo between a planet and orbit.
*   **System liner:** A vessel for travel within a star system; cannot enter a planet’s atmosphere.

### PL 7: Gravity Age
This era sees the propagation of vessels that traverse the space between the stars.
*   **Cutter:** A small warship designed for high speed and long endurance.
*   **Escape pod:** A small sphere that can hold up to eight people with supplies for up to four weeks.
*   **Launch:** A light space-only vehicle for short interplanetary runs.
*   **Trader:** A tramp freighter, smaller and faster than a transport, often the choice of spacefaring heroes.
*   **Transport:** A common spacecraft for carrying cargo or up to a dozen passengers.
*   **Yacht:** A small, luxurious, and fast spacefaring palace.

### PL 8: Energy Age
*   **Space fighter:** A small, personal fighter for one or two individuals, capable of interstellar travel and combat.

## VEHICLE COMBAT

Vehicle combat scenes revolve around high-speed attacks and chases. Heroes use Vehicle Operation and its specialty skills to operate vehicles. For multi-operator vehicles, other crew members use System Operation skills.

### Ramming a Character with a Vehicle
A driver attempting to ram a pedestrian makes a Vehicle Operation check. The pedestrian adds their Dexterity resistance modifier to the driver's check and can attempt an Acrobatics-dodge check. If hit, the victim makes a Dexterity feat check (or Acrobatics-fall skill check) and consults TABLE P43, modified by the vehicle type:
*   **Motorcycle:** -2
*   **Compact car:** -1
*   **Mid-sized car:** No modifier
*   **Large car, van:** +1
*   **Truck or tank:** +2

## SPACESHIP SYSTEMS

Every spaceship has a certain number of compartments, each dedicated to a purpose.
*   **Command:** The control center with helm, main computer, sensors, and communications.
*   **Engineering:** Houses the ship's engine, power plant, and drive unit.
*   **Auxiliary:** Monitors life support and secondary power systems.
*   **Defense:** Controls special defensive systems.
*   **Weapons:** Houses the ship's weapon systems and operator station.
*   **Electronics:** Reserved for fragile systems like computers and sensors.
*   **Cargo:** Space for carrying cargo.
*   **Crew:** Living space for crew members and passengers.
