Cruising Speed A vehicle’s cruising speed is the
fastest speed at which the vehicle
can be safely controlled in a combat
or chase situation. If an operator decides to push his craft beyond its
cruising speed, a penalty is applied
to his Vehicle Operation checks: +1 if
cruising speed is exceeded, or +3 if
the vehicle is pushed all the way to
its maximum speed. The Gamemaster may decide to
invoke this +3 penalty even if a vessel isn't literally traveling at its maximum speed. For instance, although
a sports car's maximum speed is 320
kph, an operator should not be able
to avoid the +3 penalty by having the
car do “only” 300 kph for an indefinite length of time. Failing a Vehicle
