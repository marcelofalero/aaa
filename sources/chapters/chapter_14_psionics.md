# PSIONICS

If psionic powers are available in your campaign, you might want to try your hand at playing a Mindwalker. Mindwalkers are powerful psionics-users who have been trained to harness and exploit the powers of their minds. Essentially, a Mindwalker hero is generated using the rules in Chapter 2: Hero Creation. However, there are a few alterations to those rules, which are discussed below.

The supplemental hero sheet has spaces to record a psionic hero’s characteristics. A blank line is provided at the end of each group of psionic skills in case your Gamemaster allows the existence of some psionic skills beyond those described in this chapter.

## Creating a Mindwalker

*   **Species:** As long as your Gamemaster agrees, a hero of any species can choose to be a Mindwalker. The alien species known as the fraal receive special bonuses related to this profession.
*   **Career:** Mindwalker careers focus on different ways to utilize mental powers. Possible Mindwalker careers include biokineticist, biowarrior, ESPion, mystic, telekineticist, psiguard, telepath, and mind knight. These careers are detailed later in this chapter.
*   **Profession:** Mindwalker is a profession, just as Combat Spec, Diplomat, Free Agent, and Tech Op are. Mindwalkers are a select group of characters who are gifted with great mental powers and trained to use them.

### Mindwalker Profession

Hero Mindwalkers must have the following scores or greater in these Abilities: Will 11, Intelligence 9, Constitution 9. Mindwalkers have these benefits:
*   **Action Check Score Increase:** A Mindwalker's action check score is increased by 1 point.
*   **Situation Bonus:** Choose one psionic broad skill. This broad skill and all of its specialty skills receive a situation die improvement of 1 step. The broad skill now has a base situation die of +d0, and all of its specialty skills have a base situation die of -d4.

**Note:** Diplomats in a campaign that allows Mindwalkers can use this profession as their secondary profession.

## Psionic Skills

Psionic broad skills provide a Mindwalker with a basic knowledge of and proficiency in the use of mental abilities. The psionic broad skills are **Biokinesis** (Constitution), **ESP** (Intelligence), **Telekinesis** (Will), and **Telepathy** (Personality). No psionic broad skill can be used untrained.

Psionic specialty skills provide a Mindwalker with detailed knowledge and training in the use of a specific aspect of the broad skills they’re associated with.

## USING MENTAL ABILITIES

Psionic skills work just like other skills in the game. A player can decide that his hero is going to use a psionic skill in place of any other action (assuming he has psionic energy points available).

### Psionic Energy

Because the use of psionic skills requires a lot of mental energy, a psionic character has a limited number of psionic energy points. Each Mindwalker starts with psionic energy points equal to his Will score. A Diplomat with the Mindwalker secondary profession has psionic energy points equal to one-half his Will score.

Whether a psionic skill check succeeds or not, the character suffers a loss of psionic energy points with every attempted mental action:
*   **Critical Failure result:** 3 points
*   **Broad skill, success or failure:** 2 points
*   **Specialty skill, success or failure:** 1 point

If a character runs out of psionic energy points, he can't use his psionic abilities until he recovers at least some of his points. If a character uses a psionic ability and loses more points than he has available, he suffers 1 point of fatigue damage for each point that wasn't available. A character must have 2 points available to attempt the use of a broad skill.

### Extended Duration

Many psionic skills remain in effect for a time after they are activated. In the descriptions that follow, these skills are identified by the line "Extended duration.” If a character makes a successful skill check, the power goes into effect at the start of the next phase. It remains in effect automatically for the remainder of the current round and throughout all of the next round. The effect can be further extended on a round-by-round basis if the character is willing and able to spend 1 psionic energy point to keep it going.

### Psionic Energy Recovery

A character can attempt to recover psionic energy points at the end of any hour in which he did not use a psionic skill. The outcome depends on a Will feat check or a Resolve-mental resolve skill check: Critical Failure, lose 1 point; Failure, no recovery; Ordinary, 1 point recovered; Good, 2 points; Amazing, 3 points. If a character rests for 8 hours without using mental abilities, all psionic energy points are recovered.

### Resisting Mental Abilities

If a mental ability is directed at another character, that character's Will resistance modifier is figured into the psionic-user's skill check. Also, a target’s skill in Resolve-mental resolve can provide additional modifiers.

## PSIONIC SKILL DESCRIPTIONS

### CONSTITUTION SKILLS (BIOKINESIS)
*   **Bioweapon:** Extended duration. Generates a staff or club of biokinetic energy used as a melee weapon.
*   **Control Metabolism:** Extended duration. Regulate metabolic processes to survive in hostile environments or feign death.
*   **Heal:** *Cannot be used untrained.* Heal your own wound damage or disease.
*   **Morph:** *Cannot be used untrained.* Alter your physical features to disguise yourself or overcome physical obstacles.
*   **Rejuvenate:** Offset fatigue or stun damage.
*   **Transfer Damage:** Absorb another character's damage or disease into yourself.

### INTELLIGENCE SKILLS (ESP)
*   **Battle Mind:** Extended duration. *Cannot be used untrained.* Focus on combat to receive a bonus to action checks.
*   **Clairaudience:** Project your hearing to a distant location.
*   **Clairvoyance:** Project your sight to a distant location.
*   **Empathy:** Read the surface emotions of another character.
*   **Mind Reading:** Read the surface thoughts of another character.
*   **Navcognition:** *Cannot be used untrained.* Instinctively determine your location and plot a course.
*   **Postcognition:** Sense the emotional mood of an area and see events from the recent past.
*   **Precognition:** Receive impressions about possible future events.
*   **Psychometry:** Read psychic impressions from inanimate objects by touching them.
*   **Sensitivity:** Consciously detect the use of psionic skills in your vicinity.

### WILL SKILLS (TELEKINESIS)
*   **Electrokinetics:** *Cannot be used untrained.* Generate and direct an electrical charge as an attack.
*   **Kinetic Shield:** Extended duration. *Cannot be used untrained.* Create an invisible defensive barrier against physical attacks.
*   **Levitation:** Extended duration. Lift and propel yourself through the air.
*   **Photokinetics:** Excite molecules in an object to make it give off light.
*   **Psychokinetics:** Extended duration. Move objects with the power of your mind.
*   **Pyrokinetics:** *Cannot be used untrained.* Excite molecules to generate intense heat and cause an object or area to burst into flame.

### PERSONALITY SKILLS (TELEPATHY)
*   **Contact:** Extended duration. Send and receive thoughts with another character.
*   **Datalink:** Extended duration. *Cannot be used untrained.* Link your mind with a computer or cybernetic machine without a physical connection.
*   **Illusion:** Extended duration. Project an illusory sight or sound into a target's mind.
*   **Mind Blast:** *Cannot be used untrained.* Direct a powerful blast of pure mental energy at another mind.
*   **Mind Shield:** Establish a mental defense against specific psionic powers.
*   **Suggest:** Plant a non-detrimental thought into another person’s mind, convincing them it is their own.
*   **Tire:** Inflict fatigue damage upon a target.

## MINDWALKER CAREERS
*   **Biokineticist:** Focuses on the Biokinesis skill, acting as a psionic healer.
*   **Biowarrior:** A psionic soldier focusing on Biokinesis, particularly the Bioweapon specialty.
*   **ESPion:** A spy or investigator using ESP abilities to uncover information.
*   **Mystic:** Uses ESP to look into the past, present, and future, often with a mysterious or religious air.
*   **Telekineticist:** A psionic soldier who uses the powerful, visible abilities of Telekinesis.
*   **Psiguard:** A bodyguard or security expert combining Telekinesis and ESP for formidable offense and defense.
*   **Telepath:** A communications specialist or spy using the abilities of Telepathy.
*   **Mind Knight:** A warrior who relies solely on mental abilities, eventually mastering all four psionic broad skills.
