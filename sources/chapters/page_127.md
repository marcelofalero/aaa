BENEFITS Heroes get better in two ways. First,
they show improvement in skills by
the skill ranks they've accumulated.
Second, they demonstrate overall experience by the achievement level
they've earned. In addition, as a
hero's level goes up, special achievement benefits become available to
him. These benefits provide a hero
with improvements or bonuses that
affect areas of a hero's persona other
than his skills. Reading the Table The costs for achievement benefits
