# MUTANT HEROES

In game terms, a mutant is a character species derived from human stock. Just as other nonhuman characters do, a mutant gains certain special abilities and powers just for being of a different species.

After a player creates a mutant hero and determines the character’s special abilities, he takes the hero through the rest of the creation process in the same fashion as for any other hero; see Chapter 2: Hero Creation for a full description of that process. The supplemental hero sheet at the back of this book has spaces to record a mutant hero’s characteristics.

Although every mutant originates from human stock, each one’s basic makeup has been altered significantly enough that he is considered an alien for the purpose of determining the skills he possesses at the beginning of his career. Mutant heroes use TABLE P5: HERO STARTING SKILL POINTS (see page 34) to determine the number of skill points, broad skills, and specialty skills they start with. (They don't receive the skill bonus that nonmutant human characters do.)

One of the major differences between a mutant hero and an alien hero is that the mutant’s special advantages aren’t predefined by his race; every mutant in a campaign may have unique powers and capabilities. No two mutants are necessarily alike.

## Types of Mutants

A mutation is a change to the basic blueprint of an organism. This may be an accidental or random process, either naturally occurring through evolution, or a change brought about by manufactured conditions such as a nuclear accident, exposure to mutagenic compounds, or deliberate genetic engineering.

### Common Traits
*   **Ability Score Range:** The range of Ability Scores for a beginning mutant hero is the same as for humans: a minimum of 4 and a maximum of 14 in each Ability. It is possible, however, for one or more of a mutant’s Ability Scores to exceed the maximum after all of the character's mutations have been determined.
*   **Free Broad Skills:** A mutant hero starts play with the same free broad skills as a human hero: Athletics, Vehicle Operation, Stamina, Knowledge, Awareness, and Interaction.
*   **Special Abilities:** All mutant heroes possess at least one advantageous mutation and one mutation drawback, as described below.

The defining characteristic of a mutant hero is a special power, ability, or improvement that a human hero doesn't have. You have two choices for determining your hero's mutation—random generation or personal selection. In both cases, you have to balance the advantages by also taking mutant drawbacks.

The origin of a hero’s mutation has a direct effect on the extent of his powers. The maximum power of any mutation, and the number of mutations the hero possesses, are determined by the hero's origin. Start by consulting TABLE P48: MUTANT ORIGIN, either selecting the option that fits most closely with your hero concept (if your Gamemaster permits such selection) or rolling a die for random determination.

### Spending Mutation Points
If your hero has more than 1 mutation point, you must decide how many mutations to have and how powerful each of them will be. The list below gives all the possible combinations for spending mutation points.
*   **1 pt:** 1 Ordinary
*   **2 pts:** 2 Ordinary or 1 Good
*   **3 pts:** 3 Ordinary or 1 Good + 1 Ordinary
*   **4 pts:** 1 Good + 2 Ordinary or 1 Amazing
*   **5 pts:** 2 Good + 1 Ordinary or 1 Good + 3 Ordinary or 1 Amazing + 1 Ordinary
*   **6 pts:** 3 Good or 1 Amazing + 1 Good or 1 Amazing + 2 Ordinary
*   **7 pts:** 2 Good + 3 Ordinary or 1 Amazing + 3 Ordinary or 1 Amazing + 1 Good + 1 Ordinary

Advantageous mutations are rated either Ordinary, Good, or Amazing. A mutant can't have more than three Ordinary mutations, two Good mutations, or one Amazing mutation.

## ADVANTAGEOUS MUTATION DESCRIPTIONS

### Ordinary Mutations
*(Cost: 1 mutation point each, max 3)*

*   **Adaptation, Environment (CON):** Reduces penalties for a specific environment (hot, cold, arid, thin/thick atmosphere) by 2 steps.
*   **Adaptation, Gravity (DEX or STR):** Eliminates penalties for light/zero-g (DEX-based) or reduces penalties for high-g by 2 steps (STR-based).
*   **Adrenal Control (WIL):** Once per day without penalty, gain a -1 bonus to physical skill checks and +1 to melee/unarmed damage for d4+1 rounds. Subsequent uses cause fatigue.
*   **Biorhythm Control (WIL):** Gain a -1 bonus to Stamina and physical Resolve checks.
*   **Dermal Reinforcement (CON):** Provides natural armor with a rating of d4+1 (LI), d4 (HI), d4-1 (En).
*   **Improved CON/DEX/INT/PER/STR/WIL:** Permanently increase the specified Ability Score by 1 point.
*   **Improved Durability (CON):** Stun rating improves by 3 points.
*   **Improved Healing (WIL):** Once per day, make a Resolve check to heal stun/wound points.
*   **Improved Immunity (CON):** Gain a -2 bonus to checks against disease, infection, or biological agents.
*   **Improved Reflexes (DEX):** Gain a -1 bonus to your action check.
*   **Improved Senses (WIL):** Gain a -1 bonus on perception-based checks using a specific sense.
*   **Night Vision (WIL):** Reduces obscurity penalties from dim light by 2 steps.
*   **Psi Sensitivity (WIL):** Allows an Awareness-intuition check to detect nearby psionic power use.
*   **Toxin Tolerance (CON):** Gain a -2 bonus to checks against poisons or drugs.
*   **Vascular Control (WIL):** Reduce mortal damage taken by 1 point and gain a -1 bonus to mortal damage-related Stamina checks.

### Good Mutations
*(Cost: 2 mutation points each, max 2)*

*   **Acid Touch (CON):** Secrete a corrosive substance from fingertips. Damage is based on a CON feat check.
*   **Dermal Armor (CON):** Provides natural armor with a rating of d6+1 (LI), d4+1 (HI), d4 (En).
*   **Electric Aura (WIL):** Generate an electrical field that can damage on touch, based on a WIL feat check.
*   **Enhanced CON/DEX/INT/PER/STR/WIL:** Permanently increase the specified Ability Score by 2 points.
*   **Enhanced Durability (CON):** Wound rating improves by 3 points.
*   **Enhanced Healing (WIL):** An improved version of the Ordinary mutation, allowing for more healing, including mortal damage at Amazing success.
*   **Enhanced Immunity (CON):** Provides a -3 bonus to checks against disease, infection, or biological agents.
*   **Enhanced Reflexes (DEX):** Gain a -2 bonus to your action check.
*   **Enhanced Senses (WIL):** Gain a -2 bonus on perception-based checks using a specific sense.
*   **Increased Metabolism (WIL):** A more potent version of Adrenal Control, providing a -2 bonus to physical skills and +2 to melee/unarmed damage, but at a higher fatigue cost.
*   **Natural Attack (STR):** Gain a natural weapon (claws, fangs, etc.) that deals d6+2s/d4w/d4+2w damage.
*   **Psionic Power:** Gain a single psionic specialty skill at rank 3. The hero is considered a "talent."
*   **Radiation Tolerance (CON):** Provides a -3 bonus to Stamina checks to overcome radiation effects.
*   **Redundant Vital Organs (CON):** Provides a -2 bonus to Stamina checks related to mortal damage.

### Amazing Mutations
*(Cost: 4 mutation points each, max 1)*

*   **Chameleon Flesh (WIL):** Change skin color and texture to blend with surroundings, providing a -3 bonus to Stealth-hide checks.
*   **Dermal Plating (CON):** The mutant's body is covered with visible, thick organic plates, providing armor with a rating of d8+1 (LI), d6+1 (HI), d6 (En).
*   **Energy Absorption (CON):** Absorb a specific type of energy (e.g., electricity, fire, radiation), taking half damage and converting the other half to temporary stun points.
*   **Energy Reflection (DEX):** Reflect a portion of a specific type of energy attack back at the source.
*   **Flight (STR):** The mutant has natural wings, allowing flight.
*   **Hyper CON/DEX/INT/PER/STR/WIL:** Permanently increase the specified Ability Score by 3 points.
*   **Hyper Durability (CON):** Mortal rating improves by 3 points.
*   **Hyper Healing (WIL):** The most potent healing mutation, allowing significant recovery of all damage types once per day.
*   **Hyper Immunity (CON):** Provides a -4 bonus to checks against disease, infection, or biological agents.
*   **Hyper Metabolism (WIL):** The ultimate adrenaline boost, providing a -3 bonus to physical skills and +3 to melee/unarmed damage, but with a severe fatigue cost.
*   **Hyper Reflexes (DEX):** Gain a -3 bonus to your action check.
*   **Hyper Senses (WIL):** Gain a -3 bonus on perception-based checks using a specific sense.
*   **Improved Natural Attack (STR):** An enhanced natural weapon dealing d8+2s/d6w/d6+2w damage.
*   **Poison Attack (CON):** The mutant can deliver a poison through a bite, scratch, or touch.
*   **Psi Resistance (WIL):** Provides a +4 penalty to any psionic skill checks directed at the mutant.
