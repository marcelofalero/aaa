millennia, various types of armor
emerge over time to protect the vulnerable hero from the deadly array of
arms he might encounter. Armor isn't
impervious, but it's better than nothing, and a complete suit of armor can
guard its wearer against all but the
most well-aimed blows. The finest armor types of the highest Progress Level are self-contained
tanks, equipped with onboard sensors, computers, and weapons systems, and are appropriate only on
the battlefield. In civilized areas,
most people view unconcealed armor
in much the same way they view a holstered sidearm—it's an indication
that the individual expects to shoot
and get shot at. PL O: Stone Age Stone age armor makes use of readily available material (which is limited), and armor technology remains
