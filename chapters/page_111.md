This chapter is designed to help you decide on a career for your
hero. It includes sample careers, complete with concepts,
“signature” equipment, and suggested skill packages to get you
started. You still need to build your hero’s Ability Scores and
purchase a full complement of skills as outlined in Chapter 2:
Hero Creation. This list is far from exhaustive. If you have a career idea not covered
in this chapter, discuss it with your Gamemaster. In the end, a career is
just a character tag to help you give your hero an identity.
