# Manipulation (DEX)

A natural proficiency in manual dexterity and control. Used for picking locks, sleight-of-hand, and other tasks requiring a steady hand.

## Specialty Skills

### Lockpick (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Open physical locks using specialized tools.
- **Complex Check:** Most locks require multiple successes. Modifiers are based on lock quality and tool quality.

### Pickpocket (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Remove or plant objects on a person without detection.
- **Base Die:** Opponent's Will resistance modifier.
- **Success:** Achievement of Ordinary or better means the task is done. Higher degrees offset penalties for repeated attempts.
- **Rank 3, 6, 9, 12:** Increases your own Will resistance modifier against being pickpocketed.

### Prestidigitation
Stage magic and slipping small objects out of sight.
- **Base Die:** Opponent's Will resistance modifier.
- **Failure:** The task occurs but is noticed. Successes are pulled off without a hitch.
