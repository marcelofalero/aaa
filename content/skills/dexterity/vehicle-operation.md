# Vehicle Operation (DEX)

Training in the operation of vehicles. This skill is used for high-speed attacks, chases, and precision maneuvers.

## Specialty Skills

### Air Vehicle
Piloting aircraft, atmospheric fliers, and hover-vehicles.

### Land Vehicle
Driving ground cars, bikes, and tracked vehicles.

### Sea Vehicle
Operating surface ships and submersibles.

### Space Vehicle
Piloting spacecraft and starships. 
- **Crew:** For multi-operator ships, this skill is used by the pilot while others use [System Operation](/skills/intelligence/system-operation).
