# Stealth (DEX)

The ability to move about or remain in a location without attracting attention. 

- **Mechanic:** Degree of success provides a penalty to the observer's Awareness or Investigate check: Ordinary (+1), Good (+2), Amazing (+3).
- **Critical Failure:** Observer receives a -2 bonus to spot you.

## Specialty Skills

### Conceal
Hide objects from view or sensors.

### Disguise
Change your appearance to look like another person or blend in.

### Shadowing
Follow a target without being noticed.
- **Base Die:** Target's Will resistance modifier.

### Sneak
Move silently to avoid being heard or seen while in motion.

## Rank Benefits
- **Rank 3, 6, 9, 12 [Increased Effect]:** Increases the penalty applied to observers.
- **Stealth Cloak (PL 8):** Provides a +3 bonus to Stealth checks when active.
