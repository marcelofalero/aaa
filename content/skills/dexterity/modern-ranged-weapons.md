# Modern Ranged Weapons (DEX)

Training in projectile and energy weapons, including pistols, rifles, and submachine guns.

- **Clip Change:** Uses 1 action. Change and fire in same phase: +2 step penalty.

## Specialty Skills

### Energy Weapon
Proficiency with lasers, blasters, and other high-tech energy projectors.

### Firearm
Proficiency with traditional slug-throwers.

### Pistol
- **Rank 3 [Quick Draw]:** Draw and fire in the same phase without the usual +1 penalty.

### Rifle
- **Rank 3 [Improved Aim]:** -1 bonus to the situation die for any rifle attack.
- **Rank 6 [Precision Shooting]:** Autofire penalties reduced to 0 / +1 / +2.

### SMG
- **Rank 3 [Rock-n-Roll]:** Clip change and fire penalty reduced to +1 step.
- **Rank 6 [Precision Shooting]:** Autofire penalties reduced to 0 / +1 / +2.
- **Rank 9 [Extra Burst]:** Fire 4 bursts on autofire (fourth die at +3 penalty).

## Ranged Weapons Rank Benefits
- **Rank 3, 7, 11 [Distance Precision]:** Increase weapon range categories.
- **Rank 5 [Double-Shot]:** Fire two shots as one action (+2 step penalty).
- **Rank 12 [Rate of Fire Increase]:** Use weapon's burst fire more effectively.
