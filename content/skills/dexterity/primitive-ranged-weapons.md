# Primitive Ranged Weapons (DEX)

Training in bows, crossbows, slings, and primitive firearms like flintlocks.

## Specialty Skills

### Bow
Load and fire in the same action. Proficiency increases with short and long bows.

### Crossbow
Takes 1 action to load and a separate action to fire.

### Flintlock
Use of primitive black-powder weapons.
- **Pistol:** 1 action to load, 1 to fire.
- **Rifle:** 2 actions to load, 1 to fire.

### Sling
Load and fire in the same action.

## Rank Benefits
Shares similar rank benefits to Modern Ranged Weapons regarding distance and precision.
