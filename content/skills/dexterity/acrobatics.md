# Acrobatics (DEX)

This broad skill provides basic training in gymnastics, tumbling, vaulting, rolling, and dodging. It is essential for characters who need to move through dangerous environments or avoid attacks.

- **Vaulting:** Use either Athletics-jump or Acrobatics. If you have both, increase jump distance by +1m.

## Specialty Skills

### Daredevil
Perform high-risk activities like skydiving, scuba diving, or bungee jumping.
- **Success:** Ordinary (Aloft/Stable), Good (Limited Control), Amazing (Total Control).
- **Critical Failure:** Equipment failure or personal injury.

### Defensive Martial Arts (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Focus on redirecting force, throws, and holds.
- **Damage:** d4s / d4+1s / d4+2s (LI).
- **Rank 2 [Block]:** Attempt to block unarmed attacks (Success >= Attacker's).
- **Rank 4 [Reaction Block]:** Block any unarmed attack as a reaction (uses next available action).
- **Rank 4, 8, 12:** Improve STR resistance modifier by +1.

### Dodge
Use agility to evade attacks or area-effect dangers.
- **Effect:** Increases STR or DEX resistance modifier based on success: Ordinary (+1), Good (+2), Amazing (+3).
- **Rank 3:** Dodge and perform an action in the same phase (+2 penalty to the action).
- **Rank 7 [Reaction Dodge]:** Dodge earlier in the round than your Action Check (loses all other actions).
- **Rank 4, 8, 12:** Improve DEX resistance modifier by +1.

### Fall
Twist and slow your descent to reduce impact damage. Adds specialty rank to the check.

### Zero-g Training (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Function in weightless or light-gravity conditions.
- **Rank 1:** Reduces zero-g penalty to +2 steps; eliminates light-gravity penalty.
- **Rank 4:** Zero-g penalty +1; light-gravity bonus -1.
- **Rank 7:** Zero-g penalty eliminated.
- **Rank 10:** Zero-g bonus -1.
- **Sesheyan Advantage:** Start as if having Rank 1. All penalties/bonuses are 1 step better.
