# Leadership (PER)

Ability to coordinate a group to perform better or work as a team. Only one character can assume the leader role at a time.

- **Check Results:** Success provides a -1 bonus to actions for followers for the remainder of the current round and all of the next.
- **Scope:** Ordinary (2 followers), Good (4 followers), Amazing (6 followers).
- **Critical Failure:** Leader confuses followers (+1 penalty to actions).

## Specialty Skills

### Command
Lead through sheer authoritativeness. Used by officers and officials. Cannot be used on *Combative* targets.

### Inspire (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Command by example. Can favorably alter the attitude of even *Hostile* or *Combative* targets toward the user.
- **Rank Benefits:** Higher ranks increase the number of followers who can be inspired.
