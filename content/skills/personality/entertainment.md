# Entertainment (PER)

Proficiency in the performing arts, stage presence, and the ability to elicit emotional responses from an audience.

- **Base Die:** Modified by target's Will (to fool/alter emotions) or Personality (to impress).
- **Critical Failure:** Bad luck (microphone fails, instrument breaks, etc.).

## Specialty Skills

### Act
Play a role, impersonate others, or perform technical stage/screen tasks.
- **Rank 4 [Disguise]:** Use makeup and techniques to change appearance. Success penalizes observers' Awareness checks.

### Dance
Move rhythmically to music. Used to tell a story or impress an audience.

### Musical Instrument (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Play a specific instrument and read music. Must be purchased per instrument type.

### Sing
Trained vocal performance. Higher ranks indicate greater strong and melodious voice quality.
