# Deception (PER)

Proficiency at deceiving others through conversation and interaction. Covers bluffs, bribes, and games of chance.

- **Base Die:** Modified by target's Intelligence resistance modifier.
- **Duration:** Attitude shifts from Deception are often temporary.

## Specialty Skills

### Bluff
Mislead through false bravado, outright lies, or unwarranted confidence.
- **Success:** Ordinary (Works for scene), Good (Attitude +1 for d8+1 time), Amazing (Attitude +2 for d12+2 time).

### Bribe
Offer money, goods, or info to influence another.
- **Failure:** Bribe refused; must increase offer to try again.
- **Success:** Ordinary (Accepted), Good (Accepted + Attitude +1), Amazing (Accepted + Attitude +2).

### Gamble
Proficiency at games of chance. Used to estimate odds, spot cheaters, or cheat personally.
- **Cheating:** -3 bonus against untrained; +3 penalty against experts.
- **Success:** Higher success wins the pot. Ordinary (+0.5 pot side bet), Good (+1 pot side bet), Amazing (+2 pot side bet).
