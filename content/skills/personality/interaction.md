# Interaction (PER)

Basics of interpersonal contact within your own species and culture. Used to win friends and influence people.

- **Encounter Skill:** Result depends on target's current attitude. Cannot influence *Combative* or *Fanatic* targets.
- **Base Die:** Modified by target's Will resistance modifier.

## Specialty Skills

### Bargain
Haggle and close deals. Used by merchants and negotiators to get better outcomes.

### Charm
Change attitudes through kind words and a personable manner. Only works on *Neutral* or *Friendly* targets.

### Interview
Get information from a cooperative character. Less aggressive than Interrogate.

### Intimidate
Threaten through physical presence or authority to force cooperation. Cannot be used on *Combative* or *Fanatic* characters.

### Seduce
Entice or beguile another through planned romantic exchanges. Only works on *Friendly* or better targets.
- **Duration:** Long-lasting if interest is feigned.

### Taunt
Trade insults to rattle an opponent.
- **Success:** Ordinary (+1 penalty to target), Good (+2), Amazing (+3 and shifts attitude toward Combative).
- **Critical Failure:** Target gains -2 bonus to their next action against you.
