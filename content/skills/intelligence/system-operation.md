# System Operation (INT)

Training in spaceship systems other than the helm. Also applies to large aircraft, ocean vessels, and radar outposts.

## Specialty Skills

### Communications
Operate shipboard comms, decipher codes, and jam signals.

### Defenses
Operate shields and defensive systems. In combat, provides penalties to an opponent's attacks.

### Engineering
Monitor and repair ship systems. Route power and direct remote repairs.

### Sensors
Operate sensor arrays. Provide data bonuses to weapons or defenses.

### Weapons
Operate shipboard weapon systems. Used to pick targets and make attacks in vehicle combat.
