# Tactics (INT)

Understanding of tactical situations in infantry, space, or vehicle combat.

- **Check Effect:** Success provides an immediate penalty to the opponent's next skill check.
- **Ordinary (+1), Good (+2), Amazing (+3).**

## Specialty Skills

### Infantry Tactics
Ground-based combat involving individual troops.

### Space Tactics
Combat between spaceships.

### Vehicle Tactics
Combat involving air, land, or water vehicles.
