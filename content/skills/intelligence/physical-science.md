# Physical Science (INT)

Training in the physical sciences. Checks are handled similarly to Knowledge checks.

## Specialty Skills

### Astronomy
Study of celestial bodies and phenomena.

### Chemistry
Study of matter and chemical reactions. Produce compounds if equipment is available.

### Physics
Solve problems relating to motion, energy, and force.

### Planetology
Classify, log, and scan new celestial bodies.

### Rank Benefits
- **Rank 3, 6, 9, 12:** Grants an increasing situational bonus to related checks.
