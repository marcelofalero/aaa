# Life Science (INT)

Training in the biological sciences. Checks are handled similarly to Knowledge checks.

## Specialty Skills

### Biology, Botany, Zoology
Identify species and ascertain biochemical processes.
- **Rank 3, 6, 9, 12:** Grants an increasing bonus to related skill checks.

### Genetics (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Study of heredity and genetic engineering.

### Xenology (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Study of alien life forms and environments.
- **Rank 3, 6, 9, 12:** Grants an increasing bonus to related skill checks.
