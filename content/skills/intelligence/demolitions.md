# Demolitions (INT) (U)

*Cannot be used untrained.*

Training in the handling of explosives, primers, and timing devices for maximum effect.

- **Set Charge:** Simple check to place explosives. Complex check to hide them.
- **Concealment Penalty:** Success creates an Awareness penalty for those trying to find the charge: Marginal (+1), Amazing (+4).
- **Vulnerability Bonus:** A successful [Good] complex check increases blast damage by 1 grade; [Amazing] by 2 grades.

## Specialty Skills

### Disarm
Render bombs and mines inoperative. Usually a complex skill check.

### Set Explosives
Improve ability to set manufactured charges.

### Scratch-Built Explosives (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Build demolition packages from scratch or tampered ordnance.
- **Critical Failure:** The bomb explodes immediately.
