# Navigation (INT)

Training in navigating through 3D space or across planetary surfaces.

- **Check Time:** Optimum time is 5 mins. Rushing adds penalties; taking more time (up to 10 mins) adds bonuses.

## Specialty Skills

### Astrogation, Drivespace (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Navigation through FTL "drivespace" from one star system to another.
- **Success:** Determines where you arrive relative to the target star system.

### Astrogation, System
Navigation within a star system using normal space propulsion.

### Navigation, Surface
Navigation on a planetary surface (air, water, land).
