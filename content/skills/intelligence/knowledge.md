# Knowledge (INT)

Represents a character's ability to know a little bit about any subject. Used to recall info from classes, articles, or documentaries.

- **Check Types:** Simple (recall info) or Complex (research).
- **Categories:** Trivial, General, Specific, Expert. (Determines difficulty/complexity).

## Specialty Skills

### Computer Operation (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Operate software and OS effectively. Differs from Computer Science in that it covers use, not theory.

### Deduce
Use reason and logic to solve mysteries or technical problems.
- **Rank 4, 8, 12:** Improve Intelligence resistance modifier by +1.

### First Aid (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Basic medical training.
- **Tasks:** Heal Stun (2-4 pts), Knockout Recovery (1-3 pts), Stabilize Dying.
- **Heal Wound:** Requires first aid kit/trauma pack and a complex check.
- **Improved First Aid:** Rank 4, 8, 12 reduces penalties by -1 step.

### Language (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Fluency in a specific language.
- **Rank 3:** Native level fluency.
- **Communication Bonus:** High ranks provide bonuses to Business and Interaction checks involving that language.

### Street Knowledge
Local info about a specific city or region.

### World Knowledge
Specific info about a planet, system, or academic field (e.g., History, Math).
