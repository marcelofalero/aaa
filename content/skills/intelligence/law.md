# Law (INT)

Knowledge of laws and legal systems. Can be used as an encounter skill to influence others or to handle "off-camera" legal proceedings.

## Specialty Skills

### Court Procedures
Argue cases or points of view in official legal proceedings.

### Law Enforcement Procedures
Follow (or bend) rules concerning arrest, surveillance, and search/seizure.

### Law, Specific Skill (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Mastery of a specific culture's law or a specific legal discipline (e.g., Salvage Law).
- **Rank 3, 6, 9, 12:** Grants an increasing bonus (-1 to -4) to related Law checks.
