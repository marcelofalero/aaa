# Business (INT)

Knowledge of production, distribution, supply and demand, and market value. Used for trade, illegal transactions, and navigating commodity marketplaces.

- **Haggling:** Used as an encounter skill to change an NPC's attitude or get a better deal.
- **Modifiers:** Dealings in exotic/unfamiliar goods or unfamiliar cultures incur penalties. Inside information provides bonuses.

## Specialty Skills

### Corporate
Function in large-scale trade, stock markets, and understanding corporate operations.

### Illicit Business
Knowledge of extortion, smuggling, fencing, and the black market.
- **Effect:** Reduces penalties for illegal transactions by -1 step (improves at ranks 4, 7, 10).

### Small Business
Operating as a merchant or consumer-level trader.
- **Bonus:** Grants a -1 step bonus when making deals or haggling (improves at ranks 4, 8, 12).
