# Medical Science (INT) (U)

*Cannot be used untrained.*

Advanced training in medicine. Allows diagnosis and treatment beyond basic first aid.

## Specialty Skills

### Forensics
Causes of death and physical evidence analysis.

### Medical Knowledge
Stay up-to-date on techniques.
- **Rank 2, 5, 8, 12:** Situation bonus to Treatment checks.

### Psychology
Study of mental processes and behavior.

### Surgery (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Heal mortal damage and install cybernetics.
- **Complex Check:** Complexity depends on the amount of mortal damage. 
- **Time:** Usually hours. Requires a sterile environment for best results.

### Treatment (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Superior healing compared to First Aid.
- **Improved Recovery:** Heals more damage points per success.
- **Rank 3, 6, 9, 12:** Situation bonus to treatment checks.

### Xenomedicine (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Medical study of a specific alien species.
- **Penalty:** Healing aliens starts at a +3 penalty, reduced by rank increases.
