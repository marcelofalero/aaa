# Creativity (WIL)

A well-developed sense for the aesthetically pleasing and a facility for communication through art or writing.

- **Complex Check:** Turning this into a career requires achieving professional quality through a specialty.
- **Failures:** If the check fails too many times, the work is scrapped.

## Specialty Skills

### Artistic Expression
Master a medium such as illustration, computer graphics, or holo art.

### Writing
The ability to communicate effectively and artistically through the written word.
