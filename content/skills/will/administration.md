# Administration (WIL)

Understanding the forms and procedures involved in running an office or organization. Used to navigate bureaucracy, manage personnel, and cut through red tape.

- **Bureaucracy Interaction:** Used as an encounter skill to influence employees or get results from an organization.
- **Complex Check:** Used to produce results from a large system. Complexity is based on the size of the system and the legality of the request.

## Specialty Skills

### Bureaucracy
Ability to "grease" or clog the wheels of a system. Process permits and clearances more efficiently.

### Management
Manage personnel and administrative duties to increase productivity and inspire workers.
