# Resolve (WIL)

*Note: This skill is also categorized under Constitution for physical determination. Under Will, it focuses on mental fortitude.*

Cope with emotional stress, fear, and mind control.
- **Resistance Modifier:** Rank 4, 8, 12 improves Will resistance modifier by +1.
