# Culture (WIL)

Knowledge of social customs, history, and behavior of different societies.

## Specialty Skills

### Alien Culture
Master the etiquette and customs of a specific non-human species.

### Etiquette
The rules of social behavior for a specific culture or class (e.g., High Society, Military).

### First Encounter
Protocol and techniques for making initial contact with a new sentient species.
