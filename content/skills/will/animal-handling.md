# Animal Handling (WIL)

Basic ability to train animals for labor, riding, tracking, or attacking. Also used to calm or capture wild creatures.

- **Complex Check:** Training an animal requires continuous work over weeks.
- **Riding:** Maneuver any animal used for ground transportation (similar to vehicle rules).

## Specialty Skills

### Animal Riding
Improve stunts, racing, and mounted combat.
- **Rank 3 [Trick Riding]:** Jump fences, leap chasms, or move backward.

### Animal Training
Handle and train specific types of animals (Pack, Attack, Guard). Must be purchased separately per type.
