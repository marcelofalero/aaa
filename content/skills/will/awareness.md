# Awareness (WIL)

Represents a character's powers of observation and instinct. 

- **GM Driven:** Skill checks are typically called for by the GM to see if a character notices a clue or danger.
- **Automatic Success:** Routine observations usually don't require checks.

## Specialty Skills

### Intuition
Natural instincts and sensing danger.
- **Surprise:** Success allows the character to act in the surprise phase.
- **Best Guess:** Can be used once or twice an adventure to make an intuitive leap of logic.

### Perception
Spot signs using normal senses (broken twigs, clicks of a safety, subtle scents).
- **Potential Danger:** Used to avoid surprise when danger is obvious.
