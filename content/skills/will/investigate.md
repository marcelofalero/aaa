# Investigate (WIL)

Systematic examination through inquiry and attention to detail. Differs from Awareness in that it takes time and focused effort.

- **Check Results:** Success provides a significant bit of information. Higher degrees provide more detail.

## Specialty Skills

### Interrogate
Probe a target for information using reasonable debate or trick questions.
- **Base Die:** Target's Will resistance modifier.

### Search
Systematically hunt for hidden objects, clues, or individuals in a specific area.

### Track
Follow a person, creature, or vehicle by noting signs of passage.
- **Terrain:** Tracker selects a terrain (Forest, Urban, etc.). -1 bonus in favored terrains; +1 penalty in others.
