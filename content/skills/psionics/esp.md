# ESP (INT) (U)

*Cannot be used untrained.*

The ability to perceive information beyond the standard physical senses.

## Specialty Skills

### Battle Mind (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Focus on the flow of combat to receive a bonus to your Action Checks. (Extended duration).

### Clairaudience / Clairvoyance
Project your hearing or sight to a distant, unseen location.

### Empathy
Read the surface emotions of a target character.

### Mind Reading
Read the surface thoughts of a target character.

### Navcognition (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Instinctively determine your physical location and plot courses without instruments.

### Postcognition / Precognition
Receive impressions from the recent past (area-based) or possible future events.

### Psychometry
Read psychic impressions from inanimate objects by touching them.

### Sensitivity
Detect the use of other psionic skills in your immediate vicinity.
