# Biokinesis (CON) (U)

*Cannot be used untrained.*

Psionic control over the biological functions of your own body or others.

## Specialty Skills

### Bioweapon
Generate a staff or club of biokinetic energy used as a melee weapon. (Extended duration).

### Control Metabolism
Regulate metabolic processes to survive in hostile environments, hold breath longer, or feign death. (Extended duration).

### Heal (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Psionically heal your own wound damage or treat diseases within your own body.

### Morph (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Alter your physical features to disguise yourself or gain minor physical advantages (e.g., gills, claws).

### Rejuvenate
Use mental energy to offset fatigue or recover from stun damage.

### Transfer Damage
Absorb another character's damage or disease into your own body.
