# Telepathy (PER) (U)

*Cannot be used untrained.*

Direct mental contact with the minds of others.

## Specialty Skills

### Contact
Establish a two-way mental link to send and receive thoughts. (Extended duration).

### Datalink (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Directly interface your mind with a computer or cybernetic system. (Extended duration).

### Illusion
Project false sensory information into a target's mind. (Extended duration).

### Mind Blast (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
A focused offensive strike of mental energy against another mind.

### Mind Shield
Defensive mental barrier against psionic intrusion.

### Suggest
Subtly plant a thought or impulse into a target's mind.

### Tire
Psionically drain a target's energy, inflicting fatigue damage.
