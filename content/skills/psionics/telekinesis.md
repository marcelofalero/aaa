# Telekinesis (WIL) (U)

*Cannot be used untrained.*

The use of mental energy to directly influence or manipulate physical matter.

## Specialty Skills

### Electrokinetics (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Generate and direct an electrical charge as a ranged attack.

### Kinetic Shield (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Create an invisible defensive barrier that deflects physical attacks. (Extended duration).

### Levitation
Propel yourself through the air using mental force. (Extended duration).

### Photokinetics
Manipulate light or excite molecules to make an object glow.

### Psychokinetics
The classic ability to move, lift, or manipulate objects with your mind. (Extended duration).

### Pyrokinetics (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Generate intense heat or cause an object to burst into flames.
