# Teleportation (WIL) (U)

*Cannot be used untrained.*

The newest discovery in the field of psionics, Teleportation allows a character to fold the distance between two points, moving themselves or objects instantaneously. This skill is extremely rare and was first observed among entities originating from Drive Space.

## Specialty Skills (Proposed)

### Blink
A short-range, combat-effective jump within line of sight. Used to dodge attacks or reposition instantly.

### Jaunt
A medium-to-long range teleportation to a known or visible location. Requires significant concentration.

### Spatial Shift
The ability to teleport objects or other willing characters without moving yourself.

### Dimensional Anchor
The ability to prevent others from teleporting into or out of a specific area.
