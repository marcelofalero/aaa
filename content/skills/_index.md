# Skills in AAA

Skills are the heart of the AAA system. Most of what a character is capable of doing—fighting an opponent, piloting a spacecraft, or developing a vaccine—is governed by their skills and how accomplished they are at using them.

## Broad vs. Specialty Skills
- **Broad Skills:** Represent a general area of knowledge, talent, or training (e.g., *Athletics*, *Knowledge*).
- **Specialty Skills:** Represent narrow fields of expertise (e.g., *Climb*, *First Aid*).

## Skill Categories by Ability

### [Strength Skills](/skills/strength)
Focus on physical power and prowess. Includes Armor Operation, Athletics, and Melee Weapons.

### [Dexterity Skills](/skills/dexterity)
Focus on agility, coordination, and reflexes. Includes Acrobatics, Stealth, and Vehicle Operation.

### [Constitution Skills](/skills/constitution)
Focus on physical fitness, endurance, and health. Includes Resolve and Stamina.

### [Intelligence Skills](/skills/intelligence)
Focus on mental quickness and learning. Includes Science, Engineering, and Tactics.

### [Will Skills](/skills/will)
Focus on mental fortitude, intuition, and creativity. Includes Awareness and Culture.

### [Personality Skills](/skills/personality)
Focus on social ability and charisma. Includes Deception, Interaction, and Leadership.

### [Psionic Skills](/skills/psionics)
Focus on the powers of the mind. Includes Biokinesis, ESP, Telekinesis, and Telepathy.
