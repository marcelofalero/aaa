# Resolve (CON)

Inner strength of will to defend against mental stress, stand up to physical pain, and stick to your convictions.

## Specialty Skills

### Mental Resolve
Cope with emotional or mental stress (trauma, fear, madness, mind control).
- **Check:** Used to set aside trauma and act without hindrance.
- **Rank 4, 8, 12:** Improve Will resistance modifier by +1.

### Physical Resolve
Cope with physical trauma and determination to resist torture or exhaustion.
- **Knockout Recovery:** Success allows the character to regain consciousness and some stun damage.
- **Avoiding Exhaustion:** Check to keep going when all fatigue points are spent.
- **Fatigue Recovery:** Determine points recovered after rest.

### Interrogate
Ask the right questions to gain information from a target.
- **Base Die:** Target's Will resistance modifier.
- **Note:** While listed under Resolve in the core skill table, it focuses on the application of will against another.
