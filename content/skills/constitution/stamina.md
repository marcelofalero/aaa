# Stamina (CON)

Measures physical fortitude regarding the effects of mortal damage and fatigue.

- **Dying:** Dying characters make Stamina checks to see if their condition worsens. Success prevents further damage.
- **Fatigue:** Characters make Stamina checks after extreme exertion to avoid fatigue damage.

## Specialty Skills

### Endurance
Acquire higher ranks to replace standard Stamina checks with Endurance checks, representing superior physical conditioning.

### Resist Pain (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Ignore situation penalties for suffering large amounts of damage.
- **Trigger:** First time in a scene damage > 50% Stun/Wound or 1+ Mortal damage.
- **Check:** Does not count as an action. Success reduces or eliminates dazed penalties.
