# Survival (CON)

General training in survival necessities, including finding food, water, and shelter in various environments.

- **Mechanic:** A character can become so familiar with a locale that survival is automatic.
- **Success:** Ordinary (find basics for a day), Good (find extras), Amazing (highly efficient survival).

## Specialty Skills

### Survival Training
Improve survival ability in a specific terrain type (Forest, Desert, Mountain, Arctic, Jungle, Swamp, Ocean, Urban).
- **Bonus:** -1 bonus in chosen terrains; +1 penalty in others.
