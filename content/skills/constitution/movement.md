# Movement (CON)

This broad skill allows a character to increase the distance they can travel in a given time and makes journeys less arduous. 

- **Overland Movement:** A character with this skill can travel for longer than an untrained character before making Stamina-endurance checks. Checks are made every 4 hours (stroll), 3 hours (march), or 2 hours (forced march).

## Specialty Skills

### Race (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Improve ability to move faster in combat scenes.
- **Rank 1:** Run movement rate increases by +2m.
- **Rank 5, 9:** Run rate increases by +2m.
- **Rank 4, 7, 12:** Sprint rate increases by +2m.

### Swim (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Necessary to use your full swim movement rate. Without it, you can only tread water or paddle at an easy swim rate.
- **Holding Breath:** Grants a -1 bonus to Stamina-endurance checks while holding breath (improves at ranks 4, 8, 12).
- **Rank 4, 8, 12:** Swim and easy swim rates increase by +1m.

### Trailblazing
Find paths through rough terrain or create paths for others.
- **Effect:** Reduces penalties for traveling in difficult conditions for the whole group.
- **Success:** Ordinary (-1 step), Good (-2 steps), Amazing (-3 steps).
