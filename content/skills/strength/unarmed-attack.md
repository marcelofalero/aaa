# Unarmed Attack (STR)

Basic knowledge and experience in hand-to-hand fighting. 
- **Base Damage:** d4s / d4+1s / d4+2s (LI).

## Specialty Skills

### Brawl
Discipline-free fighting, boxing, or wrestling.

### Power Martial Arts (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Styles emphasizing power (e.g., Karate).
- **Improved Damage:** d6s / d6+2s / d4w.
- **Rank 4, 8, 12:** Improve STR resistance modifier by +1.

### Rank Benefits (All Specialties)
- **Rank 2 [No Hands]:** Use head butts or kicks for normal damage.
- **Rank 3, 6, 9, 12 [Increased Damage]:** Add +1 to unarmed damage.
- **Rank 5 [Knockout]:** Attempt a knockout attack (+4 step penalty). Target must resist or be unconscious for d6 rounds.
- **Overpowering:** Attempt to restrain an opponent.
