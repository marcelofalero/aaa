# Armor Operation (STR)

This broad skill allows a character to become accustomed to wearing armor, negating some or all of the penalties associated with its use. Most armor types have an Action Check and Dexterity resistance modifier penalty. 

- **Broad Skill Effect:** Reduce the Action Check and Dexterity resistance modifier penalty of any type of armor by 1 step (minimum 0).
- **Untrained:** A character untrained in Armor Operation suffers the full Action Check and DEX resistance modifier penalties and can only walk and make simple attacks (no rank benefits).

## Specialty Skills

### Combat Armor
Improve ability to perform actions while wearing standard combat armor.
- **Effect:** Reduce Action Check and DEX resistance modifier penalty by 1 additional step (for combat armor only).
- **Rank 4, 8, 12:** Reduce penalties by one additional step.
- **Rank 5+ [Shaking Off Stuns]:** Once per round, make a skill check to ignore stun damage: Ordinary (1pt), Good (2pts), Amazing (4pts).

### Powered Armor (U)
*Cannot be used untrained.*
Improve proficiency with powered combat armor and its strength enhancements.
- **Effect:** Reduce Action Check and DEX resistance modifier penalty by 1 additional step (for powered armor only).
- **Rank 4, 8, 12:** Reduce penalties by one additional step.
