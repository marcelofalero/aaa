# Athletics (STR)

This broad skill provides a character with the abilities of a natural athlete—strength, grace, and physical prowess.

## Specialty Skills

### Climb
Scale walls, mountains, or other obstacles.
- **Combat Movement:** Ordinary (1m), Good (2m), Amazing (3m) per phase.
- **Critical Failure:** Fall from current height.

### Jump
Perform horizontal or vertical leaps.
- **Standing Jump:** Ordinary (1m), Good (2m), Amazing (3m).
- **Running Jump:** Ordinary (2m), Good (4m), Amazing (6m).
- **Vertical Leap:** Ordinary (1m), Good (1.5m), Amazing (2m).
- **Rank Benefits:** Distance increases at various ranks (check full rules for specific milestones).

### Swim
Movement through water.
- **Base Rate:** Check Movement Rates table.

### Throw
Accurately toss objects like knives, grenades, or stones.
- **Range:** Short (STR), Medium (STR x 2), Long (STR x 4).
- **Accuracy:** Success depends on the [Accuracy by Range](/rules/chapter-03#accuracy-by-range) table.
