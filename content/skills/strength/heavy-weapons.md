# Heavy Weapons (STR)

Training in heavy weaponry that isn't part of a ship or vehicle system. Includes mortars, grenade and rocket launchers, and shoulder-fired missiles.

## Specialty Skills

### Direct Fire
Improve proficiency with heavy weapons that require line-of-sight aiming, such as plasma guns and heavy machine guns.

### Indirect Fire
Improve proficiency with heavy weapons that fire over or around obstacles delivering area effects, such as mortars and grenade launchers.
- **Accuracy:** Success depends on the [Accuracy by Range](/rules/chapter-03#accuracy-by-range) table.
