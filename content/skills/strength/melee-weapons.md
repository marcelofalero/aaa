# Melee Weapons (STR)

Training in any object used in a close-quarters fight, from knives and swords to stun batons and gravmaces.

- **Parry:** You can use a Melee Weapons check to deflect an incoming melee or unarmed attack. Requires an available action in the current phase.

## Specialty Skills: Blade, Bludgeon, Powered Weapon

### Rank Benefits
- **Rank 3, 7, 11 [Resistance Modifier]:** Improve DEX resistance modifier by +1.
- **Rank 4 [Reaction Parry]:** Can parry as a first action even if it occurs before your initiative phase.
- **Rank 6 [Double-Strike]:** Make two attacks as a single action (+2 step penalty).
- **Rank 12 [Multistrike]:** Make up to three attacks as a single action.
