# Project Notes & Design Philosophy

This page serves as a repository for ongoing thoughts and planned changes for the **AAA (Alterated Alternity Alternative)** system.

## Planned System Changes

### Disconnecting Broad Skills from Attributes
I plan to disconnect Broad Skills from specific Attributes. This change is intended to provide more flexibility in character development, allowing for more diverse combinations of natural talent and trained expertise.

### Unified "Covert Ops" Broad Skill
I will create a new Broad Skill called **Covert Ops**. This skill will consolidate several related activities into a single training path, including:
- **Unified Stealth:** Merging hide, sneak, and shadowing.
- **Security Bypass:** Consolidating physical lockpicking and electronic security bypass.
- **Sleight of Hand:** Incorporating traditional pickpocketing and prestidigitation.

### Profession & Background Changes
- **Diplomat:** The Diplomat will no longer be a standalone Profession (class). Instead, its core identity and skills will be redistributed into one or several **Backgrounds** (e.g., Ambassador, Negotiator, Politician). This allows characters of any profession to have a background in high-level social interaction.

### New Species & Psionics
- **Drive-Space Entity (Name TBD):** A new species originating from "Drive Space" (the dimension used for FTL travel). They are specialized psions with a physiology adapted to the non-Euclidean nature of that realm.
- **Teleportation Broad Skill:** A new Psionic broad skill allowing for the manipulation of space and distance. This will include specialties for personal blink-steps, long-range jaunts, and potentially shifting objects.

This change aims to streamline "rogue-style" characters and reduce skill bloat in the dexterity and intelligence categories.
