# Human

In the AAA game, members of the human species come in a variety of shades, sizes, and body types, but they all look like the people around us.

## History
The history of humanity is well documented. For the purposes of AAA, two events define the species' future: the development of the means to expand beyond Earth to colonize the solar system, and the eventual achievement of interstellar travel to establish communities in other star systems.

## Special Abilities
- **Skill Bonus:** As a by-product of their anatomical versatility and cultural diversity, humans begin with **5 more skill points** than members of other species do, and are allowed to start with **one more broad skill**.
- **Free Broad Skills:** Stamina (CON), Knowledge (INT), Athletics (STR), Vehicle Op (DEX), Awareness (WIL), Interaction (PER).

## Roleplaying
Human characters are often defined by their ambition and adaptability. They are the benchmark by which other species often measure themselves, and their drive to explore and settle the stars has pulled many other species into a sprawling galactic community.
