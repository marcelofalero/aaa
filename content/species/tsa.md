# T'sa

A t'sa (pronounced “t-sah”) conveys a constant impression of speed and sharp reflexes. Standing 1.4 meters tall, they are sleek, reptilian humanoids covered in interlocking scales. Their expressive eyes and childlike exuberance make them very likeable to humans.

## History
The t'sa evolved from a world resembling Earth’s prehistoric era where dinosaurs remained dominant. Spurred by natural curiosity and a lack of patience, they developed a technological society and founded a star-spanning empire (the **T'sa Cluster**) before humans even discovered faster-than-light travel. They are motivated by discovery rather than expansionism or power.

## Special Abilities
- **Action Check Bonus:** Natural quickness grants a **-1 step situation die bonus** to action checks (base die becomes -d4).
- **Jury-rig Bonus:** Natural tinkering ability grants a **-1 step situation die bonus** to Technical Science-jury-rig checks.
- **Body Armor:** Natural scales provide protection: d4+1 (LI), d4 (HI), d4-1 (En).
- **Free Broad Skills:** Athletics (STR), Manipulation (DEX), Stamina (CON), Knowledge (INT), Awareness (WIL), Interaction (PER).

## Culture & Roleplaying
Everything a t'sa does, they do quickly. They are consummate tinkers, disassembling devices to understand and improve them. They are cheerful, curious, and usually bond with group members as lifelong friends. They excel as Tech Ops, engineers, and inventors, but seldom aspire to violence.
